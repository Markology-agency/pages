Ashford Sky: ChatGPT ads for the QoE offer

9 ad variants across 3 ad groups, for OpenAI Ads Manager. Built November 12, 2026.

The angle

A full quality of earnings report sized for deals under $5M, for buyers who are under LOI, in diligence or buying their first business.

Where the prompts came from

The first four prompts are searches the site already shows for in Google Search Console, worded the way buyers ask an AI tool.

Character limits

Titles are written to 24 characters and copy to 48. OpenAI's help pages block automated checks, so treat these as our house limits and confirm them in Ads Manager when you build.

Before these can run

The site has no OpenAI pixel yet. Create a pixel in OpenAI Ads Manager and send over the pixel ID, and the booking event goes on the site.

The conversion is a booked call. Every Calendly booking on the site already lands on /thank-you/, so the pixel fires appointment_scheduled there.
