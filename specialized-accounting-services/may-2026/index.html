May / June Game Plan

SPEED UP
THE RECOVERY.

Three focuses to accelerate consolidation onto sas-acct.com.

01

Audit the 301 redirect map page-by-page

The old domain still pulled 87 organic visits in April with 44 keywords ranking, which means Google has not finished consolidating signals. A page-by-page audit confirming every old URL maps to its closest new equivalent (not all to the homepage) is the single highest-impact move. Submit the new sitemap in Search Console and request indexing on the priority pages.

Technical SEO
02

Rebuild the non-brand keyword cluster on the new domain

The old domain ranked for 96 keywords at peak in February, of which only the brand terms have recovered so far. The next priority is identifying which non-brand pages drove the most traffic on sas-cpas.com (Search Console export from before the migration) and refreshing those pages on the new domain with stronger internal linking from the homepage.

Content
03

Reach out to top referring domains for backlink updates

Backlinks pointing at sas-cpas.com pass less authority through redirects than direct links to sas-acct.com. A targeted outreach to the top 10 referring domains, asking them to update the URL, would compound with the redirect work and shorten the recovery timeline.
