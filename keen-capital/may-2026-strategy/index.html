May / June Game Plan

WHAT WE WORK
NEXT.

Seven items, ordered by impact. Items 1 and 2 ship this week. Items 3 to 7 ship in the next 30 to 60 days. The big change this month: a focused local pilot in Chapin, South Carolina. Niching down lets Keen Capital own a defined geography before scaling outward.

01

Title and meta rewrite on the Safe Withdrawal Rates article

The article currently sits at position 7 with 102,443 impressions and only 17 clicks over 90 days. The page is already ranked. The title and meta description are not communicating the value clearly enough. A rewrite focused on "what the 4% rule looks like in 2026 for $1M to $5M portfolios" sharpens the relevance for the actual searcher. Even a 1% CTR (well below standard for the position) projects roughly 1,000 monthly clicks from this single page.

This Week
02

Audit and fix Google Ads conversion tracking

Two enabled conversion actions ("Let's Chat" button and lead form submission) but only 4 tracked conversions in 90 days against $618 spend, including a Max Conversions campaign that registered zero. Verify the tracking events fire on the live site, ensure the Google Ads conversion linker tag is installed correctly, and consider tracking a confirmed advisor consultation as the primary outcome instead of a button click.

This Week
03

Pilot a Chapin, South Carolina local play

Keen Capital is already physically located in Chapin. Lake Murray and the broader Columbia metro have a meaningful HNW retiree population, and local search competition for fee-only fiduciary advisor terms is far less crowded than the national landscape. Three pieces ship together: (1) a dedicated location page at /chapin-sc/ with local schema markup and Google Business Profile alignment, (2) a Chapin-focused landing page tied to a specific HNW pre-retiree pain ('Independent fiduciary advisor in Chapin, SC'), (3) a geo-targeted Search and PMax ad campaign with a small starter budget targeting Chapin, Lexington, Irmo, Lake Murray, and downtown Columbia. Local pilot before regional or national scale.

New Plays

FOUR NEW
DIRECTIONS.

Outside the standard SEO and content rhythm. Four bigger swings worth piloting if you're open to them.

Idea 01

HNW retirement readiness assessment

An interactive on-site tool that helps a $1M to $10M portfolio holder understand where they stand on retirement readiness. Educational, not advisory. Output is generic guidance, with a soft call to action to discuss the specifics in a one-on-one consultation.

Captures the same HNW pre-retiree audience already landing on the safe withdrawal page. Higher conversion intent than a static blog reader.

Idea 02

Quarterly research note newsletter

One quality piece per quarter: "What the 2026 data shows about retirement income strategies." Educational, evidence-based, no specific recommendations. Distributed via email and posted to the site.

Builds the calm-authority brand voice into a recurring marketing asset. Becomes the piece prospects forward to peers, building referral flow inside HNW networks.

Idea 03

LinkedIn thought leadership cadence

One quality LinkedIn post per week from a Keen Capital principal, focused on educational observations about market structure, retirement research, or behavioral finance. Measured tone, no market predictions, no soliciting.

Per the brand's measured-thoughtful voice, low frequency is intentional. One quality post per week beats five generic ones for an HNW professional audience.

Idea 04

Fee-only advisor directory listings

Listings on NAPFA, XY Planning Network, and similar fee-only fiduciary directories. Many HNW prospects start their search via those filtered directories before they ever land on a firm's site.

Steady inbound flow at no ongoing marketing cost. Aligns with the trustworthy, fiduciary-first positioning. Authoritative backlink benefits as a side effect.
