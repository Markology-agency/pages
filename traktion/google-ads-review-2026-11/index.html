WHAT IS GOING ON.

The search campaign reaches the right people, but there are very few of them and each click is expensive.

The keywords are the ones we want: "private practice bookkeeping", "accountant for therapists" and "bookkeeping for therapists". The trouble is volume. In the first 11 days of November those keywords were shown 93 times in total. Each click cost between $8 and $12, and none of the 50 clicks since September 16 booked a call.

Confidence: high

The brand campaign is cheap and produced the one booking, but fewer people are searching for Traktion by name.

This campaign shows when someone searches for Traktion directly. It had 26 clicks in October and 2 in the first 11 days of November. Clicks on Traktion's name in Google's regular results fell over the same period, from 26 in June and 35 in July to 8 in October.

Confidence: medium

The search ads only appear for about 1 in 10 of the searches they could.

About 40% of the missed searches are down to the daily budget running out. About 50% are because other advertisers outrank us on those keywords. Spending more would buy more of the same $8 to $12 clicks, which have not booked calls so far.

Confidence: high

WHAT WE RECOMMEND.

  1. Pause the therapist search campaign. It has spent $475 since September 16 without a booked call, and the search volume is too small for that to change soon.
  2. Move its $8 a day to the Year-End Practice Review campaign on Facebook and Instagram. That campaign went live on November 11. It offers the free year-end worksheet and collects a name and email inside the ad, so we can follow up with each person.
  3. Keep the brand campaign running. It has cost under $20 a week and catches people who are already looking for Traktion.
  4. Review again in two weeks. We will compare the cost of a worksheet lead with the cost of a search click, and see how many worksheet leads go on to book a call.
