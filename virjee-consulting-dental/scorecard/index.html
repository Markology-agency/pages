Red flags: 7 to talk through

1
Leads: 25 in September vs 31 a month
Why
September is the slowest dental month, and the Google budget ran out by midday on most days.
Fix
Google budget doubled to about $1,400 a month on Sep 28. We review results on Oct 30 before going to $2,000.
Owner: MarkologyBy: Oct 30
2
12 of 18 September clients have no lead source
Why
They booked by calendar, phone or email instead of the website form, and the Lead Source field on the deal is left blank. So we cannot tell which channel brought in $94,570 of September's $126,970.
Fix
1. Make Lead Source a required field when a deal is created. 2. Add "How did you hear about us?" to Emily's HubSpot meeting booking page. 3. Emily fills in the 12 in the sheet so September is complete.
Owner: 1 Shahzad. 2 Shahzad and Markology. 3 EmilyBy: Oct 9

3
Facebook funnel: spend per client unknown for September
Why
Funnel spend hit its highest month ($4,554) while Meta-reported bookings fell from 48 in May to 25. No September client is recorded as coming from Facebook, and funnel bookings do not reach the CRM yet.
Fix
The funnel team embeds the booking page so funnel leads land in the CRM. Omar reviews the funnel with them and decides whether to move part of that budget to Google.

4
The CRM does not count Closed Won as closed
Why
The Closed Won stage in the sales pipeline is set as not closed, so the CRM's own reports undercount wins.
Fix
Change the stage setting so Closed Won counts as won.
