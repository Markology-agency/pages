Head of agency push-backs

What I'd Change Before Beta

  • 01
    YouTube is risky for most accountants. Camera + editing cadence kills non-naturals. Month 9 stays OPTIONAL in fast-track. For solos, replace with a "leads-now sprint" instead.
  • 02
    Ads stay minimal. Most accountants should not DIY Google Ads. Module 12.3 plus a clean "or hire Markology" link is the right scope.
  • 03
    For fast-track, swap Months 6 and 7. Reviews + GBP drive faster local leads than lead magnets. Deep-dive keeps current order.
  • 04
    Canopy / Carbon / Karbon integration is your moat. No other accountant marketing course integrates with practice management. Worth real research effort. Could become a Month 1 sub-topic AND a deeper Bonus module.
  • 05
    The "5-tab test" deserves to be a named teaching device. Memorable, recommendable. Use it on the sales page too.
  • 06
    Reconciliation needed: "weekend completable" vs "12 months." The fix is to position Month 1 as the whole core course (weekend binge), Months 2-12 as monthly NEW skill drops that add tools as your firm grows. Same sales-page promise as Will's spine quotes. No contradiction.
