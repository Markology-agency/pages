INSTAGRAM BIO
OPTIONS.

Three options for the Thompson Flaherty Instagram bio, all written in brand voice, all under the 150-character limit. Pick the one that feels right and paste.

Voice & format rules baked in:
  • "We" voice (no third person)
  • No em dashes, no jargon, no AI cliches
  • Edwardsville / St. Louis / Chicago geography signal
  • Light emoji use (one or two per option)
  • All under Instagram's 150-character cap

OPTION A · SENTENCE, SIMPLE

A 140 chars Direct & informative
☕ We're the CPAs trusted by Edwardsville, St. Louis, and Chicago business owners. Taxes, bookkeeping, and advisory, with the jargon left out.

Plain, clean, and complete. Leads with the geographic credibility, names the services, and lands a soft brand beat at the end. Best if you want the bio to feel professional first and personable second.

OPTION B · SENTENCE, VOICE ANGLE

B 138 chars Markology pick
📞 We're the CPAs Edwardsville, St. Louis, and Chicago business owners trust to actually pick up the phone. Taxes, bookkeeping, advisory. ☕

Same geographic credibility, but the "actually pick up the phone" beat carries the Thompson Flaherty brand without spelling it out. The 📞 and ☕ bookend reinforce the "coffee-with-your-CPA" idea. Strongest balance of voice and information.

OPTION C · STACKED, FAST SCAN

C 128 chars Visual scan
☕ Trusted by business owners in Edwardsville, St. Louis & Chicago 🧾 Taxes, bookkeeping & advisory 📞 We actually pick up the phone

Three short lines stacked for the fastest scan on a profile page. Best if you want the bio to read like a profile, not a sentence. Each line carries one idea (geography, services, brand voice).

OUR PICK: OPTION B

Captures the three-city geographic credibility and the Thompson Flaherty brand voice in one clean sentence. The "actually pick up the phone" beat is a quiet contrast to the bigger firms that don't. Option C is the strongest alternative if you'd rather have the bio read as a quick visual stack than as a tagline.
