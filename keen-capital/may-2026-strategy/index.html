The current setup is a measured pivot: HNW frame stays, geographic anchoring narrows to SC/Midlands. There are three ways to pace it. Each carries different risk and different upside.

A

Pure additive (current default)

Build net-new Chapin / Columbia / Lake Murray location pages and local-anchored blogs. Leave existing national HNW content alone. Lowest risk to existing rankings (none of the retirement-by-age cluster gets touched). Slowest ramp on local signal since each new page starts from zero authority.

Low risk · slow ramp
B

Selective folding (recommended)

Net-new local pages PLUS surgical SC framing inserted into the highest-ranking existing assets (Safe Withdrawal Rates 2026 is already the model: title + meta rewrite in Internal Review). Touches 2 to 4 ranking URLs with a sentence + location-aware author bio, no full rewrites. Faster local-signal ramp with controlled ranking risk. This is the path Markology is currently scoped to.

Recommended
C

Full national-to-local rewrite

Rebrand every page top-down as a Midlands HNW advisory. Highest local-signal velocity but real ranking risk: keywords that landed via national framing (e.g. the retirement-by-age cluster) may slip if Google reads the topic as having narrowed. Recommend against until the retirement cluster is locked in on page 2.

High risk · not recommended
The recommendation: Stay on path B through Q3. Ship the three Chapin/SC infrastructure pieces (location page, ad landing, Safe Withdrawal meta), measure the new-keyword pickup in July's SEMrush data, then decide whether to broaden the folding to other existing URLs. Don't touch the retirement-by-age cluster URL until it's on page 2.

Q1 · HNW Retirement Income Lead Magnet Shape

Standalone PDF worksheet, or multi-touch sequence?

The W3 "Build retirement income lead magnet" task captures the existing retirement-by-age organic traffic and converts it into an owned email list. Two viable shapes, each with different production cost and different downstream commitment.

Option A · Standalone PDFA single, high-quality HNW retirement income worksheet (fillable PDF, 6 to 10 pages). Lower production cost. Single download moment. Ties to one CTA: book a discovery call. Easier to ship in May/June.
Option B · Multi-touch email sequencePDF download triggers a 4 to 6 email nurture sequence (one per week) with deeper retirement-income concepts. Higher production cost. More qualified leads at the end. Asks more of Thomas's voice up-front.

Markology recommendation: Ship Option A first, layer Option B's sequence on top in Q3 once the magnet is converting. Also worth confirming the CTA target service page (Asset Management vs. Tax-Aware Investing vs. a Retirement-specific page if one is in scope).

Q2 · Quarterly Update Opt-In Audience

Same audience as the lead magnet, or separate channel?

The "Add quarterly update to the website + opt-in" task (W2, Not Started, dev) is an email-capture mechanism for a low-pressure HNW newsletter. The question is whether it's the same list as the lead magnet or a distinct channel.

Option A · One unified listLead magnet downloads + quarterly opt-in both feed the same audience. Simpler ESP setup. Treats both as "warm HNW prospects + clients." Easier to manage.
Option B · Two separate listsQuarterly update is for existing clients + warm referrals (positioned on About/Team pages). Lead magnet is for cold organic prospects. Different tone, different cadence, different signal-to-noise.

Markology recommendation: Option B is cleaner for an RIA. Existing-client communications and prospect lead-gen should never share the same nurture sequence. The opt-in placement (about/team only vs. site-wide) follows from that decision.
