The Sept-Oct 2025 "win" was 91 button clicks, not 91 leads
During that period the "Leads-Search-October" campaign produced 32 + 25 + 29 + 5 = 91 conversions split between Get Started Now and Book a Call button clicks. Whitney confirms real qualified leads from Google Ads are tracking at roughly 1 every few months. That means those 91 button clicks did not translate to 91 booked consultations - they were people clicking the button but not completing the booking flow. The campaign got paused, probably because actual lead volume didn't follow the platform-reported conversion count.
Primary bidding action shows 0 conversions in last 30 days
"GTM Submit lead form" is the only action currently set as primary for bidding. It should be firing every time someone completes the contact form at vastcfo.com/contact/ (Whitney's confirmed definition of a real lead). It produced zero conversions in the last 30 days. Most likely cause: the GTM tag is not firing correctly on the contact form's submission event - either the tag listens for the wrong selector / event, or the form does an AJAX submit that the tag doesn't see. Less likely but possible: nobody submitted the form in 30 days, which would itself be a separate problem (landing page or campaign quality issue, not a tracking issue).
The actions worth optimizing for are not primary
Book a Call and Get Started Now are BOOK_APPOINTMENT category - the right intent for a fractional CFO. They're set as secondary, so the bidding system sees them but doesn't optimize toward them. Even if the button-click signal is noisy (per the Sept-Oct experience), it's still closer to a real lead than a page view. Recommend: replace the button-click events with a clean "thank-you-page-after-Calendly-booking" event, and set that single event as the only primary action.
Recommended diagnostic steps (before any spend changes)
1. Submit the contact form at vastcfo.com/contact/ as a test, with GTM Preview mode open. Confirm the "GTM Submit lead form" tag fires on submit. If it doesn't, the trigger is misconfigured - fix it before anything else.
2. After a confirmed test submission, watch Google Ads conversion reporting over 24-48 hours to verify the conversion lands. Some accounts have data sync issues even when the GTM tag fires correctly.
3. Identify what page (if any) the user lands on after submitting. If there's a thank-you page (e.g., /contact/thank-you/) use that page view as a backup conversion action - it's harder to break than a form-submit listener.
4. Once verified, demote the Book a Call, Get Started Now, and Contact form Submission actions to secondary. Keep "GTM Submit lead form" as the single primary. The bidding system should then optimize toward the real lead event.
5. Wait 30 days at the corrected setup, then re-evaluate. If conversions still read zero, the issue is upstream - either the contact form isn't getting submissions from ad traffic (landing-page problem) or the test submission itself revealed a tag failure that needs developer work on the GTM container.
Waste

Recoverable spend and structural waste

At $200/mo total spend, the dollar-recoverable waste here is small. The structural waste - campaigns that can never produce a lead in their current state - is the bigger issue.

CUT

"LocalServicesCampaign:SystemGenerated:00062b2ce083e014" sits at $0.01/day budget and was created automatically by Google. It serves no purpose and adds clutter to account reporting. Pause or remove entirely. If a real Local Services Ads presence is wanted, set it up deliberately in the LSA console - this auto-generated stub is not it.
