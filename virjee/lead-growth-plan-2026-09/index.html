Google Ads

Where we are

~$700Monthly ad spend across both sites
~$150Typical cost per lead on dental searches (about $125 once this week's fixes settle)
~$40Cost per lead when people search "Virjee Consulting"
$9.50 → $6.80Average cost per click on main dental keywords, peak earlier this year vs now

What's happening in the market

  • Clicks got more expensive, then eased. The average cost per click on our main dental keywords was about $5 to $7.50 through 2025, rose to about $9.50 at its peak earlier this year, and is about $6.80 now.
  • The search pool has a ceiling. About 3,700 people a month search for a dental CPA, dental accountant, valuation or buying a practice nationally. Once our ads show on most of those searches, extra budget runs out of searches to buy, which is why the ad options in section 4 stop paying off past about $3,500 a month.
  • Phones convert less than desktops. Desktop visitors turn into leads far more often. The homepage speed-up this week targets exactly that.
  • Why we weren't winning more searches: our ad showed on about 1 in 5 eligible searches. Roughly 33% to 46% of the misses were because the daily budget ran out partway through the day. Another 35% to 49% were lost on ad rank, which Google sets from bid times quality score. Google rates the ad and landing page for "dental cpa" as average (5 out of 10), and with a limited budget we capped the cost per click at $9, which lost some auctions to higher bids. A higher rating wins more auctions at a lower cost per click.

How we get there

  • More budget so the ad runs all day. Faster landing page, sharper ads and cleaner keywords to raise quality score, which wins more auctions and lowers cost per click.
  • Two weeks of clean tracking, then Google bids on real leads instead of clicks.
  • A dedicated fast ad landing page by October 10.
  • Scale the budget to the level Omar chooses (see section 4). Spend goes to the dental campaign first, then medical searches on virjeecpa.com.
  • Review search terms weekly and move money to whatever produces the cheapest real leads.

Questions we looked into

Why is dental traffic down when we've been publishing content?

Publishing slowed. Of the 18 finished articles sent for review this year, 14 have not been approved, and 10 are still waiting, some since March. So far fewer went live from April on (3 posts from the April to September plans, against 11 from January to March). We also merged 28 overlapping pages this week so each topic has one strong page, and cleaned up a wave of spam links hitting the whole dental CPA niche. Positions have been climbing since early September, and clearing the review queue is the fastest way to add traffic.

Are competitors getting the same spam links?

Yes. The same gambling and casino link network is hitting every dental CPA site we checked (Tooth & Coin, Engage, EAG, Cain Watters and others), 74% to 98% of their new links. It isn't aimed at Virjee. We disavow it as routine cleanup so it can't hold us back.

Why weren't our ads winning more searches?

Two reasons. The daily budget ran out partway through most days, which cost us a third to almost half of searches. The rest were lost on ad rank: Google rates the ad and landing page as average, and the $9 cap per click we set to stretch a limited budget lost some auctions. This week's changes address both.

We spent $380 and got 2 conversions. Is that right?

Google was under-counting. When we matched ad clicks to actual form leads, a real lead cost about $150. Every form is now tracked, so the ad accounts will show the true number.

Is Google better than Facebook?

On every number we can see, yes. From recent sales outcomes, Google produced 16 of the 27 new clients with a known source. Facebook website leads produced 2.

How much should we spend?

$2,100 to $2,800 a month is the sweet spot. Past about $3,500 the search pool runs out and leads get expensive (section 4).

When will we see results?

Ads, email and website changes show up in October and November. SEO shows up in the January to March tax season. We report leads by source every month.

How will we know it's working?

A monthly scorecard: leads by site and source, owners, ad cost per lead, organic visits, and new clients from each source.
