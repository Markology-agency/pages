The opportunity

~5,300Monthly searches across buying, selling, valuation and consulting, about 7x "dental cpa"
160 to 320Extra organic visits a month once the pages rank
3 to 13Extra leads a month, mostly owners and buyers

Tax and accounting are becoming a commodity. Advice on buying, growing and selling a practice is where dentists will pay for expertise, and where the acquired firms don't yet compete online. Pages take 6 to 9 months to rank, so starting in October puts them to work by the January to March tax season.

Pages to build

Ownership Transitions hub

  • Buying a Practice (upgrade the existing page with a form and the purchase review offer)
  • Preparing to Sell (the 2 to 10 year pathway: know the number, raise the value, then sell)
  • Selling a Practice (for owners 0 to 2 years out)
  • Practice Valuation (the written valuation service; the calculator is the free first step)
  • Associate to Owner

Dental Consulting hub

  • A "how can we help?" page built on the questions owners actually ask: "I'm at a million in collections and still feel broke," "Am I charging enough for a crown in my area?", "Can I retire on what my practice will sell for?"
  • Profitability and overhead
  • Fee schedule review
  • Practice benchmarks (KPI checker)

Written from Omar's own words (his videos, webinars and the voice library) so they sound like him and need fewer edits.
