Right now the campaigns measure button clicks, not actual booked calls. We'll set up a Calendly booking webhook that fires a Google Ads conversion event the moment a booking is confirmed. New Plays

FOUR NEW
DIRECTIONS.

Outside the standard SEO and content rhythm. Four bigger swings worth piloting if you're open to them, ranked from lowest-effort to most ambitious.

Idea 01

Podcast guest tour

Pitch you onto two or three dental practice owner podcasts per quarter. Short list: Shared Practices, The Dentalpreneur, ACT Dental, The Dental Hacks.

Direct access to the exact target audience, plus a backlink and a Calendly CTA on every appearance. One of the most underrated channels for B2B advisors in any niche.

Idea 02

Calculator-page retargeting on Meta

Anyone who hits the Practice Valuation Calculator and doesn't book a call gets retargeted on Facebook and Instagram with a 'Want help reading the number?' creative leading back to your calendar.

Hyper-qualified audience. Low spend, fast feedback loop. Modest budget required to test (around $500 per month).

Idea 03

Targeted LinkedIn outbound for Thomas

10 to 15 personalized DMs per week to dental practice owners in specific situations: 5 to 15 ops, recently bought a practice, recently hit a milestone.

Each message shares one specific tax or financial insight relevant to that owner, with a soft 'happy to chat if useful' close. Conversion rate runs 30 to 50% on this style when done correctly. Small numbers, big returns.

Idea 04

The 'Second Opinion' guarantee

Free 30-minute review for any dentist currently working with another CPA or financial advisor. Pitch: 'If we don't identify $5,000+ in savings or improvements, we'll send you a $200 Amazon gift card.'

Bold offer, but for a target client whose lifetime value is $30K+ per year, the math works heavily in your favor. Removes the biggest objection in the category: 'I already have a guy.'
