Google Ads

SIGNAL IS CURRENTLY
UNDER-AUDITED.

Google Ads investigation is paused on tooling. Pipeboard's API quota is exhausted; Composio's googleads connector has a header bug that 400s every query. What we know from GA4: paid attribution looks broken. Direct sessions collapsed from 2,817 in December to 827 in April. Paid Search dropped from 93 to 17 sessions in the same window. The April top landing page is /?gadid=, with 110 sessions attributed to Direct, suggesting the Google Ads click identifier is landing in Direct rather than Paid Search.

Working theory: Google Ads auto-tagging may be off, the GA4 to Ads link is misconfigured, or campaigns paused mid-period and the lingering /?gadid= URLs are coming from old indexed referrals. Either way, paid spend is currently optimizing against a bad signal. Once Composio's connector is reconnected (or Pipeboard's quota resets), the audit takes about 30 minutes: verify auto-tagging, check campaign status history, review final URL templates, confirm the GA4 link is healthy.
