What we look at

The parts of a franchise that generalist preparers tend to leave on the default setting.

The franchise fee

Initial fee, renewal fees, and territory rights, written off the way the code allows rather than the way the software defaulted.

Build-out and equipment

Leasehold improvements, fit-out equipment, signage, and the elections that decide when you deduct them.

Entity setup and owner pay

Whether your entity type still fits, and how you pay yourself out of it.

Multi-location structure

How locations are held, how costs move between them, and what that does to your return.

State and local taxes

Sales tax, payroll tax, and state filings in every jurisdiction you operate in.

Estimated payments and timing

Safe harbor, quarterly amounts, and the moves that only count if they happen before year end.

Questions we get

What if I already have a CPA?

Keep them. The plan is a separate engagement, and most of our clients hand it to their preparer to implement. If you'd rather we file too, we can talk about that after you've seen the plan.

How long does it take?

Two to three weeks from the day we have your documents. If you're trying to make moves before December 31, book in October or November.

What do you need from me?

Your last two business and personal returns, the franchise agreement, a current P&L and balance sheet, and thirty minutes on the phone.
