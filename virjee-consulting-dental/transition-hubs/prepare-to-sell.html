Preparing to sell your dental practice

Some owners want five locations. Some want to practice six more years, sell, and stop before their back gives out. Both are fine plans. The second one just needs to start earlier than most people think.

The three steps

The three steps

Step 1. Find out what it's worth and what you need. What would the practice sell for today? What do you still owe? Is the difference enough to retire on? Most owners have never put those three numbers side by side.

Step 2. Move the levers. Collections, staff cost, variable cost, fixed cost and EBITDA. Omar calls overhead a control panel, because each knob you turn shows up differently on the P&L. Buyers price practices off profit, so every dollar of profit you add is worth a multiple of itself at sale time.

Step 3. Value it again before you go to market. You walk into the sale knowing your number and what drove it.

Clean books are worth real money

We see this constantly. A seller brings us P&Ls that were done cheaply and incorrectly: expenses in the wrong place, personal costs mixed in, things missing altogether. A buyer can't trust those numbers, so they pay less. In some cases the bookkeeping alone cost the owner a big piece of their sale price.

Two other things buyers check early: whether you pay yourself a market salary, and how many years are left on your lease. Both are easier to fix three years out than three months out.

Questions we hear a lot

How early should I start planning to sell my dental practice?

Ideally five or more years out. The changes that raise value need a couple of years of P&Ls to prove they stick, because that's what a buyer looks at.

What lowers a dental practice's sale price?

A heavy lease, flat collections, messy books, an owner who isn't paid a market salary, and a practice that only works when the owner is in the chair every day.

Will you sell the practice for me?

No. Brokers list and sell practices. We get the practice and the numbers ready, plan the tax side, and work with your broker through the sale.
