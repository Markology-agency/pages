Red flags: 4 to talk through

1
Dental ads cost $196 a lead, above the $150 goal
Why
The dental account has spent $589 for 3 form leads so far in November. From Oct 1 to Nov 15 the main dental search campaign sent 100 clicks to the homepage for 2 leads, and 51 clicks to service pages for no leads.
Fix
Test the three new landing pages against the homepage on that campaign, and stop sending ads to the service pages.
Owner: MarkologyBy: Nov 20
2
No blog has gone live in November
Why
The goal is 4 a month. Four drafts are in Asana for review, and the last post went live on Oct 5.
Fix
Approve the four drafts so they can be published this month.
Owner: OmarBy: Nov 20
3
Newsletter opens depend on who receives them
Why
The Nov 4 newsletter was opened by 25.6% of the 581 most engaged readers and by 2.9% to 6.9% of each of the other groups. Readers who have not opened in months pull the average down.
Fix
Send each newsletter to engaged readers first, and move readers who have not opened in months onto their own list.
Owner: MarkologyBy: Next newsletter
4
HubSpot and Shehzad's sheet disagree on 18 clients
Why
Matching July 30 to Oct 27, 10 clients are on the sheet but not Closed Won in HubSpot, 3 are Closed Won in HubSpot but not on the sheet, 2 have close dates more than two weeks apart, 2 won deals have contacts not marked Active Clients, and 1 name is spelled two ways. 3 more deals closed after the sheet's last entry on Oct 27.
Fix
Work through the shared list and correct each one.
Owner: Shehzad and EmilyBy: Ask in meeting
