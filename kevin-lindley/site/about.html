what makes us different

What Makes Working With Us Different

At a mid-size firm, you get one of two problems. Either there are too many cooks in the kitchen, or nobody takes ownership. Here, the answer to "who owns this?" is one person.

The work itself comes in three parts. First is compliance, which means getting the numbers right and filing on time. Second is tax strategy: figuring out what the IRS actually permits you to deduct, and doing it. Third is education. I want you to understand what you have and why, so none of it feels like it works by magic.

We reach out first

We're glad when you call

A reasonable amount of consultation is in the fee. You won't get a bill for every hour like an attorney would send you.

Every deduction has a reason behind it

If I can't justify it in an audit, it's off the table. I'll tell you plainly which is which.
