Just Get Me Leads.

A 30-day sprint that skips the theory and gets you to your first inbound lead. Same skills as the deep-dive, ruthlessly trimmed. No filler.

Who this is for

The solo accountant who just left corporate. The CPA who started their own firm last quarter and needs clients YESTERDAY. The fractional CFO who has the skills but not the leads. You are not here to "learn marketing." You are here to get a working pipeline. The deep-dive is for after you have leads.

The 30-day map

Four Weeks. Done.

Each week ends with a tangible outcome. Each day asks for 30 to 60 minutes. You can do this around tax season, around clients, around life.

01
Setup + Foundation
Days 1 to 7
  • Day 1
    Set up Claude Code on your computer. Connect Drive + email + meeting notes.
  • Day 2
    Build your Business DNA file. Long questionnaire. Who you talk to, what your business is, what you do, what you do NOT do, your voice, your no-go list. /build-business-dna
  • Day 3
    Install tracking infrastructure (Google Analytics, Search Console, Google Tag Manager). 30 minutes, one-time setup.
  • Day 4
    Marketing fundamentals: inbound vs outbound, how long marketing actually takes, which channels matter for accountants. Skip the lecture. Watch the 15-min recap.
  • Day 5
    Figure out your audience. The high-level ICP. /icp-starter
  • Day 6 to 7
    Deep messaging + positioning work. Run the workbooks. What makes you different, what your USP is, what happens if they don't choose you. /positioning /messaging-audit
Outcome by Day 7Claude knows your firm. You know your audience. You have a positioning statement that does not sound like every other accountant.
02
Fix Your Site
Days 8 to 14
  • Day 8
    SEO research, the short version. What you want to show up for. Free tools only. /keyword-research
  • Day 9
    Run the 5-tab test on your current site. Identify exactly what is wrong. /website-audit
  • Day 10 to 11
    Rewrite your hero + service descriptions. Pain-led copy. Specificity beats credentials. /hero-rewrite
  • Day 12
    Add the humans (real photos, real bios, real reviews). Trust signals above the fold.
  • Day 13
    Set up conversion tracking. Form submissions, phone clicks, booked calls. Now you can see what works.
  • Day 14
    Publish + sanity check. Open 5 competitor tabs alongside yours. Yours stands out, or you rework Day 10 to 11.
Outcome by Day 14A site that converts. Conversion tracking live. You can prove which channel sent which lead.
03
Local + Reviews
Days 15 to 21
  • Day 15
    Set up Google Business Profile completely. Photos, hours, services, categories, primary city.
  • Day 16
    Build your review pipeline. The ask script, the timing, the response templates. /review-pipeline
  • Day 17 to 18
    Ask your last 10 happy clients for reviews. One personalized message each. This is where leads come from for local accountants.
  • Day 19
    Set up monthly GBP posts on autopilot. /gbp-monthly-post
  • Day 20
    Citations + directories. The top 10 for accountants. NAP consistency check. /citation-list
  • Day 21
    Set up your referral ask script. Send to your last 5 clients. /referral-program-design
Outcome by Day 21You are findable on Google Maps. You have a growing review profile. Referrals are a system, not a wish.
04
Outreach + Content
Days 22 to 30
  • Day 22
    Pick your networking venues. BNI, Chamber, niche industry events. Pick 2, schedule the first month. Skip the rest.
  • Day 23 to 24
    Cold email outreach. The template, the list-build, the cadence. Send the first 25 emails. /cold-email
  • Day 25
    Set up LinkedIn personal (not business). Update bio, post your first thing. /social-monthly
  • Day 26 to 27
    Content calendar + first blog. The pain-led format. /content-calendar /blog-draft
  • Day 28
    Publish + index your first blog. Repurpose into 5 LinkedIn posts. /blog-publish-prep /repurpose-blog
  • Day 29
    Lead magnet draft + welcome email. /lead-magnet-ideas /welcome-sequence-draft
  • Day 30
    Review the week. Check tracking. Identify what's working. Plan Month 2. /monthly-metrics
Outcome by Day 30Pipeline live: site converts, GBP works, reviews coming in, referral asks out, cold email running, LinkedIn moving, first blog published, lead magnet in place. First leads should already be in motion.
What we deliberately skip in fast-track

The Tradeoff

Fast-track skips depth in service of speed. These topics live in the deep-dive ("Teach Me Marketing") and you can graduate to them in Month 2 or beyond, once leads are flowing.

Skipped

Practice Management Integration

Canopy / Carbon / Karbon / QuickBooks integration is the moat. Not needed for getting first leads. Comes in Month 2 deep-dive.

Skipped

YouTube + Video Production

Camera setup, editing, thumbnails, captions. Most solo accountants should not start here. Optional even in deep-dive.

Skipped

Webinars + Podcast Pitching

Speaking opportunities take 6+ weeks of relationship-build before they pay. Not the first 30 days.

Skipped

Google Ads

Most accountants should not DIY paid ads. Either hire Markology or skip until you have organic working.

Skipped

Schema markup + technical SEO

You install Rank Math (5 min). Set H1 + meta. Skip everything else until Month 8.

Skipped

Reddit + Quora + Communities

Slow channels. Useful long-term, not for first leads. Come back after Day 30.

Skipped

AI search visibility

Getting cited in ChatGPT and Perplexity is huge but takes content cadence to compound. Lives in Month 8 deep-dive.

Skipped

Partnership outreach

Finding tax-software ecosystems, payroll partners, etc. takes weeks of warm intros. Worth it later.
