Why We'd Like to See Your Books Every Month

When we're in your numbers every month, we can catch a problem in March, a full year before it would otherwise turn up the following April. It also means your tax return starts from books we already know, so nobody has to rebuild the year from a box of statements.

Say you're a consultant and your income jumped this year. With monthly books, the jump shows up in the statements you get by the 28th, while there's still most of the year left to decide what to do about it. With once-a-year books, the first anyone sees of it is when the return is due.

We can do more for a client whose books we see every month, and that is the honest reason we'd rather work this way. A fixed monthly fee also lets you budget your accounting costs and leaves no year-end bill to brace for.

What Owners Ask Before Hiring a Bookkeeper in Boulder, CO

How much does bookkeeping in Boulder, CO cost?

Our monthly bookkeeping starts from $275 a month. That covers your own staff accountant, an accounting manager who checks the work, up to 3 reconciled accounts, up to 25 transactions a month and financial statements by the 28th.

What if I have more than 25 transactions a month?

The starting fee covers up to 25 transactions a month and up to 3 accounts, so a busier business pays more than $275. We'll quote your monthly fee once we see your books, and it stays fixed so you can budget for it.

What if my business has very few transactions?

We have a quarterly plan from $500 a quarter for businesses with fewer than 10 transactions a month. It includes the books and a tax projection.

Will I work with the same bookkeeper in Boulder, CO every month?

You have your own staff accountant who does your books and gets to know your business. An accounting manager checks that work before your statements go out, and both of them are at our office on Table Mesa Drive.

Do you work in QuickBooks Online?

We work with QuickBooks files, including QuickBooks Online. If your file needs cleanup before monthly work can start, we'll quote that as a flat fee before we begin.

Can you run payroll too?

We run payroll for clients whose books or tax returns we already handle, from $81 a month for up to 3 employees. That includes direct deposit and checks, payroll tax filings and W-2s. Our payroll page has the details.

Is my business too small or too big for your bookkeeping?

We work with businesses doing from about $50,000 to $10 million a year in revenue. If you're near either end of that range, call us and we'll tell you honestly whether we fit.
