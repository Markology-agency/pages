New · Who it's for
Who it's for

Built for the way your winery actually runs

Estate wineries

You farm, make and sell. Your books have to tie vineyard costs to vintages and vintages to bottles, across years. I set that structure up once and keep it clean.

Custom crush clients

Someone else's tanks, your wine. Facility invoices, bulk purchases and bonded transfers all need to land in the right lot. I reconcile them so your cost per case is real.

Tasting room brands

DTC, wine club, events and a POS that never quite matches the bank. I reconcile Commerce7 or WineDirect to your books every month and keep club revenue honest.

New · How we work together
How we work together

Three steps to clean books

Most wineries are fully onboarded within 30 days, including cleanup of the current year.

Start with a call
01

Discovery call and file review

A 30-minute conversation about your winery, then I open your QuickBooks file and look at what's really there. You get a written summary of what's clean, what's not, and a fixed monthly quote.

02

Cleanup and setup

I rebuild your chart of accounts around wine, reconcile the year to date, connect your POS and bank feeds, and set up the monthly close. Usually two to four weeks.

03

Monthly close and check-in

Books closed by the 15th, financials in your inbox, and a short call to walk through anything that moved. You always know where you stand before the next decision.
