The essentials

Everything you need to know about content.

What is content marketing?

Creating and sharing valuable resources (blogs, videos, tools, calculators) that attract more leads to your business and get your firm found on Google and AI search.

Why does it matter for accountants?

Content lets your firm share knowledge, explain services, build trust with prospects, and create a sense of community around your brand, long before the first call.

How do you use the content?

Depending on your plan, the same piece becomes a blog, a reel, a YouTube video, a social post, and ad creative. Write once, repurpose many ways.

Who makes the content?

Our team does. How much input you have is up to you. The more you share as the subject-matter expert, the sharper the result, but you never have to write a word.

What kind will we create?

We decide together. We start with trending topics from our SEO tools, layer in what your clients keep asking, and build guides, quizzes, and calculators that genuinely help.

How do you track success?

By the traffic and leads it drives. The number-one driver of traffic to most sites is content, not the homepage, so we measure exactly what each piece brings in.
