Two cold email sequences

Markology LITE and HireMyAccountant, written the way you'd actually say them.

4 steps per trackv4

How these are written

Normal sentences, with joins in them

"but", "so", "whereas", "which is". The previous version was all short sharp fragments and it read as AI, which is the punchy-then-explanatory rhythm the house rules already ban.

The ask is a sample, not a meeting

Run the audit, send you a time. Every extra email between the pitch and the call is a chance for it to go cold.

Personalization comes from a check that ran

The live ChatGPT result, their real missing search terms, their real listing. If a check comes back empty the row gets dropped, never guessed.

Two things to fix before sending

Blocker

The spec and the live page disagree

The live page says $99 a month or $999 a year, a 15 minute interview, a decision within a week. spec.md says $999 a year only and a 30 minute interview. The copy follows the live page, because that's what they read when they click.

Blocker

A retired brand name is on the page these emails point to

The Get Vetted page labels the interviewer "Sam · FYA editor". FYA is Find Your Accountant, the name we retired.

Both tracks

Before every batch

  • Read every email out loud. Fragments and staccato pacing are the tell, so if a line doesn't sound like something you'd say, it doesn't send.
  • Every merge field traces to a check that ran. Spot check five rows by hand, send no blanks.
  • "Google and AI search", or "Google or ChatGPT", always paired.
  • No ranking language, and never the word guaranteed.
  • No em dashes, contractions throughout, US English, LITE always all caps.
  • CAN-SPAM footer: physical address plus a working unsubscribe, checked on the first test send.
  • Send from a warmed cold domain, never markology.io or hiremyaccountant.com.
  • Clients, former clients and the launch cohort deduped out before every import.
