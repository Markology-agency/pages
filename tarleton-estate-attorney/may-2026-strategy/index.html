Captures emails from in-market Texas residents at exactly the trigger life stages where estate planning becomes urgent. Builds owned list for ongoing nurture.

Idea 02

Allan video explainers (LinkedIn + YouTube)

One short video per week from Allan: 'What happens to your house if you die without a will in Texas', 'Will vs trust in Texas, explained in 90 seconds', 'How Texas community property changes estate planning for couples'.

Estate planning is a sensitive topic. Allan's calm-authority on camera builds the trust signal that text content can't replicate.

Texas attorney directory + association partnerships

Submit Allan to the Texas Bar Estate Planning Section directory, Avvo Texas Estate Planning, Super Lawyers, Texas Monthly Best Lawyers. Pair with one or two Texas legal podcast appearances (Texas Lawbook, ABA Legal Talk Network).

Authoritative legal directory backlinks plus direct exposure to Texas legal community. Worth a focused 30-day outreach push.

Texas estate planning seminar / workshop

Quarterly free 60-minute virtual seminar: 'Estate Planning Basics for Texas Families'. Promoted on LinkedIn, Facebook, and via email. Recorded version evergreen on YouTube and embedded on the site.

Generates 50-100 highly qualified Texas leads per session, plus evergreen content. The recording also builds video SEO authority on Texas estate terms.
