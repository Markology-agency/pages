What we think

  1. October reached the wrong people. Those campaigns ran under Meta's financial services setting, which doesn't allow targeting by job or education. Clicks were cheap, about 20 cents each, because most of the 72,000 people reached weren't dentists. That's why we paused both on October 28.
  2. The rebuild is reaching dentists, and it's early. Two leads in three days at $73 each is a good start. One lead owns a $3M to $5M practice. We want about two more weeks before we judge cost per lead.
  3. The year-end offer goes into this same campaign. The exit planning ads are a longer-term conversation. A year-end offer gives a dentist a reason to book now, before December 31. Once you pick the offers you're comfortable with, we'll add them to the dentist audience next to the exit planning ads and see which one brings in more practice owners.
  4. We need Full access to Core's Meta business to finish tracking. That lets us add the Meta pixel, so leads from the website calculators count too, not just the in-app form.
  5. We're watching how often the same dentists see the ads. The audience is about 8,000 to 9,500 people, so we'll rotate in the new offer ads before they get tired of the current ones.
