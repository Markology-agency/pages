Three questions to ask the seller before you sign the LOI

These come straight out of the lines above. If the answers are not clear, that is data too.

  1. Why is staff cost running 37.7% of collections when it should be closer to 25%?
    This is the biggest single gap on the page, about 8 points, and payroll is the biggest lever on future profit. You need to know whether it is genuine overstaffing, which you can fix, or an associate producing below what they cost, which is harder, before you underwrite a dollar of it as savings.
  2. Can we see the lab invoices for the last 6 months, not just the summary line?
    Lab at 12.0% is high on one of Omar's ranges and normal on another, depending on case mix. The invoices tell you which. A high crown to filling mix that will not repeat under you, a bad contract, and unbilled remakes all look identical on a summary line and have very different prices.
  3. What share of production came from patients the owner treated personally, and what is the transition plan for those charts?
    At 75.7% owner production, retention of those patients is the deal. Ask for the plan in writing. A short overlap and a warm intro protocol move the numbers by tens of thousands.
