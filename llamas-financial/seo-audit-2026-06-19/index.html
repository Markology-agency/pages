Why The June 1 Fix Didn't Stick

SCHEMA INTERACTION
PROBLEM.

The June 1 deploy added Service schema and built 8 blog-to-service links. The data shows that wasn't enough. The audit found three reasons why, all interacting with each other.

1. The Service page is sending Google three conflicting page-type signals

The /winery-accounting/ schema graph currently contains: Article, Service, and FAQPage. June 1 added Service. June 1 did NOT remove the legacy Article schema or the misused FAQPage block. Google's structured-data systems see three contradictory claims about what the page IS and demote it from rich results on the head term.

Action #1 + #2 this week: strip Article and FAQPage from the service page; leave Service as the sole page-type signal.

2. 8 of 35 blogs still missing in-body service-page links

Re-audit on 2026-06-19 found that 27 of the 35 blogs already carry in-body service-page links (June 1 was more complete than the initial scan showed). 8 posts still link blog-to-blog only. Less of a gap than first diagnosed, but still 23% of the cluster sending equity in the wrong direction.

Action #15 this week: add in-body anchors from the remaining 8 blog posts to the matching service page (varied anchor text).

3. llms.txt was drafted June 1 but never deployed

The June 1 plan included drafting /llms.txt and leaving it for SFTP install. /llms.txt returns 404 today. AI search engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude) that use the file for canonical discovery are invisible to Llamas.

Action #7 this week: ship /llms.txt to the web root.

All spokes link UP to the pillar with exact-match or close-variant anchor text. Pillar links DOWN to the most relevant spokes. Equity flow becomes one-directional toward the commercial page.

The architecture in three rules

  • Rule 1, every blog links up. Each of the 35 blogs gets 1-2 in-body anchors linking to /winery-accounting/ (or to whichever of the 6 service pages is most topically relevant). Anchor text variants: "winery accounting", "accounting for wineries", "winery accountants", "accountant for vintners". Spread the anchors so it isn't keyword-stuffed.
  • Rule 2, pillar carries the topical authority. /winery-accounting/ becomes the longest, most complete answer to "what is winery accounting" on the internet. Expand to 1,200-1,800 words. Cover the 6-8 subtopics each spoke discusses, with a link out to the relevant spoke for the deep dive. (Markology rule: pillar summarises, spoke explains.)
  • Rule 3, one page-type per URL. Service pages ship Service schema only. Blog posts ship BlogPosting only. Homepage ships Organization + AccountingService + WebPage. No Article on a service page. No FAQPage anywhere on a commercial accounting site.
