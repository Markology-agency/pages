01 · Decision

Tax Landing Page Direction

The audit flagged that paid traffic targeting "tax strategy" intent was hitting an inconsistent destination. Here's the call.

Decision

Use the existing /restaurant-tax-services/ as the dedicated tax LP for ad traffic. Refresh it to be ad-ready. Do not build a new /tax/ page yet.

Make this change first, run 30 days of paid traffic against it, and only fork to a separate ad-only LP if the on-page conversion rate from paid sits below 2% after a real sample.

Why this is the right call

  • Equity preservation. /restaurant-tax-services/ already exists in nav and has whatever organic equity it has accumulated. Spinning up a parallel /tax/ page splits link equity and dilutes the brand's tax authority cluster.
  • Speed-to-ship. Refresh ships this week. A new page is a 2-3 week build (design + copy + dev + QA) and would push live decisions out into June.
  • Single source of truth. One page to maintain, one page to monitor, one page to test.
  • Reversible. If paid conversion underperforms after a fair test, we fork to /tax/ with a stripped-down ad-only LP (no nav, single form, one offer). That decision is data-driven, not pre-emptive.

Ad-ready refresh checklist for /restaurant-tax-services/

  1. H1 must match ad copy intent: "Restaurant Tax Strategy & Planning" (or similar — confirm exact phrase off top-performing ad headline).
  2. Above-the-fold lead form OR primary CTA visible without scroll.
  3. One specific outcome example (range-based, not guaranteed): "Vast CFO clients typically uncover $15,000 to $40,000 in legal tax savings the first year." Soften the current "$25,000 average" claim into a range; "average" framing creates compliance risk for a Risk-flagged client.
  4. FAQ block (3-4 Qs) targeting the actual queries ad clickers have: when do I switch CPAs, what does year-round tax planning cost, S-corp election timing, Section 179 for restaurants.
  5. Single primary CTA. Strip secondary CTAs from this page when accessed via ad traffic (or build a dedicated ad-only version of the page if testing later).
  6. Page speed: target Lighthouse mobile ≥ 70. Current /start/ is 199KB; assume /restaurant-tax-services/ is similar and needs image compression.

What to tell Tanya

No client decision needed on this. Markology is making the call to refresh the existing /restaurant-tax-services/ page rather than build a parallel landing page. We'll send a follow-up if the conversion data after 30 days suggests a different approach.

Next step (Markology): Queue the /restaurant-tax-services/ on-page refresh as a separate SEO/Dev task on the June board. Tag as paid-LP optimization, not standard SEO refresh.

03 · Brief for Tanya

/start/ Landing Page Improvements

Concrete page-level changes to lift conversion from ad traffic. Audited the live /start/ page on 2026-05-26. The bones are good — these are surgical lifts, not a rebuild.

What's working today

  • H1 has a clear, concrete value prop: "We Save Restaurants Money Year-Round (Not Just in April)" — restaurant-specific, calendar-bound, differentiated.
  • "Most CPAs Disappear After Tax Season" angle directly differentiates from generic firms.
  • Strong objection-handling section: 5 specific operator objections paired with 5 specific responses. This is the best part of the page.
  • Real team intro with 4 named people — builds trust.
  • At least one named testimonial visible.

Recommended changes High impact

  1. Tighten CTA hierarchy. Above the fold today: "Sign In" + "Get Started Now" + "Book a Call" + "Read Success Stories" + "Start Your Tax Strategy". Five competing CTAs above the fold split decision-making. Pick one primary action ("Book a Call") and demote the rest to secondary visual weight or push below the fold.
  2. Soften the "$25,000 average" claim. The line "We uncover an average of $25,000 in missed tax savings for new clients" reads as an outcome promise. For a compliance-Risk firm, replace with a range and historical framing: "Vast CFO clients typically uncover $15,000 to $40,000 in legal tax savings the first year, depending on entity structure and prior CPA setup."
  3. Add a lead form above the fold. Right now the ad clicker has to scroll to find conversion. A 3-field inline form (name, email, phone) in the hero section beats a button that opens a modal.
  4. Add 2 more named testimonials. The one visible testimonial is strong but generic. Three rotating testimonials with restaurant name + city + named operator create stronger social proof for ad traffic that doesn't know the brand yet.

Recommended changes Medium impact

  1. Show a sample deliverable. "CFO services" is abstract. A screenshot of an anonymized monthly P&L dashboard or a sample of the weekly cash flow report makes the offer tangible. Add a "Here's what you actually get each month" mini-section.
  2. Add a "What we don't do" section. "We don't do generic small business accounting. We don't take clients outside restaurants, brewpubs, or wineries." Builds trust by demonstrating focus.
  3. Cross-link to the Vast Client Quiz inline. The quiz at /vast-client-quiz/ is a strong qualification tool — surface it as a secondary CTA below the objection-handling section ("Not sure if Vast CFO is the right fit? Take the 2-minute quiz.").
  4. Make the team intro work harder. Each team member gets a name and title today. Add: one specific restaurant experience credit per person (e.g., "Tanya: 20 years CFO experience across 50+ independent restaurant operators"). The "150 years combined" claim is currently abstract — distribute it across the team intro.

Recommended changes Low impact / nice to have

  1. Page weight. /start/ is 199KB. Most of that is likely hero imagery and Elementor JS. Run through TinyPNG / Squoosh — easy 30-40% weight cut.
  2. Mobile hero check. Confirm the hero CTA is visible without scrolling on iPhone 14 viewport. Long heroes lose ad traffic on mobile.
  3. Sticky bottom-bar CTA on mobile. Persistent "Book a Call" bar that stays visible as users scroll the page on mobile. Cheap conversion lift on long pages.

What we'd like Tanya's input on

  • Outcome claim wording. The "$25,000 average" line — okay with us softening it to a range, or do you have specific data backing the average that we can keep with proper attribution?
  • Sample deliverable. Can you share an anonymized monthly report or P&L dashboard we can mock up as a hero asset? Real beats stock.
  • Testimonial sourcing. Two more named testimonials with restaurant name + city. Even short quotes work better than long anonymous ones.
Next step (Markology): Once Tanya signs off on the outcome-claim softening + delivers sample deliverable + 2 testimonials, Markology ships the page updates in one sprint. Queue as a Development + Design task on the June board.
