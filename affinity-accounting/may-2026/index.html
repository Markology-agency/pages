WHAT'S NOT.

No movement in position deltas this cycle. Every tracked keyword shows position difference 0 vs. previous reading. The practical read is that May was a stability month, not a lift month — positions held, the work this period was footprint expansion, not top-3 wins.

GA4 sessions dipped 22% month over month. April 783 to May 612. Looking back twelve months, this is the lowest reading since August's anomaly (322). Two things to note before reading this as a loss: (1) GSC clicks ran 73 mid-month and project to ~90 by month-end, well above December's 56 baseline, so SERP traffic is not the problem. (2) Direct is now 58% of sessions, which is the AI-referral signature flagged in the channel breakdown above. The most likely story is a measurement-attribution shift, not real traffic loss — humans are increasingly arriving via AI answers that get logged as Direct, while raw click volume keeps climbing. Watch branded GSC impressions next month to confirm.

The opportunity is still position lift on the 12 top-15 Milwaukee-cluster terms, especially "milwaukee cpa" at 11 (volume 170) where a one-position move buys 6-9 clicks/mo at current CTR curves.
