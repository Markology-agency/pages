Webinar series

New pillar

This is the biggest addition from the call. Three Diligent-sponsored webinars on AI agents, announced as part of the launch, with ads pointing at Cherry Hill's site to register. The whole point is lead gen.

🎯The target

2,000 registrations across all three. Mike's number, "audacious" on purpose. Reference point: 1,200 came in with MindBridge, and he thinks these topics are stronger.

📅The dates

August, October, November. First one lands late August. All three focused on AI agents.

📏The guardrail

NASBA-eligible and educational on AI agents in general. Not a Diligent product pitch, and not a hard sell of our own services. Ads direct to our site → the webinar.

💰The ads

Ad spend drives registration. Budget is open to increase, Mike offered to support it, because strong Diligent numbers pull our own practice along.

The rules that apply to everything

✕No IIA logo

Off every public graphic, image and the newsletter, black it out where it appears. Allowed, but Mike wants it kept to proposals only. It adds friction, not value, on social.

✎Attribute the IIA

Say "the IIA released…" explicitly in copy. Never anything that reads as if Cherry Hill wrote the Topical Requirements. Mike is sensitive here from his former board role.

✂Shorter captions

Controversial hook first, then a checklist, not paragraphs. "For the good of the profession," not "buy our service." That framing is what gets the engagement.

81PRA number

Predictive Risk Assessment reads 81 external factors, not 45. It moved the morning of the call.

📋Publer

All launch content schedules in Publer. The team reviews and comments there; Mike tweaks his own captions in Publer.

🎨Fun + modern

Everything leans more forward and provocative. ChatGPT for image generation, the video's color-bar vibe as the reference, less stock-photo formality.
