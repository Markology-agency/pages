J. Tyler Thompson, CPA

Founder & President of TMA Accounting in Indianapolis

Tyler Thompson is the Founder and President of TMA, where he has spent 25+ years building an accounting firm designed specifically for small business owners.

His work centers on strategy, culture, and the systems that let the TMA team deliver consistent, high-quality service at scale. He is a believer in process-driven operations, continuous improvement, and the kind of relationships that hold up over time.
