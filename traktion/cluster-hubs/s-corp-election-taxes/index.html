S-Corp Election & Taxes

S-corp is the single highest-leverage tax move for a private practice once revenue crosses around $80K. Done right, it can save $5K to $15K a year on self-employment tax. Done wrong, it triggers IRS scrutiny and an expensive audit. This hub covers when to elect, reasonable compensation, estimated taxes, deductions, and the year-round tax playbook.
