Conversion tracking needs fixing

  • Biddable goals are right. Only SUBMIT_LEAD_FORM is biddable on every campaign, and no PHONE_CALL_LEAD goal is biddable at account or campaign level. BOOK_APPOINTMENT and QUALIFIED_LEAD are biddable at account level, but their actions ('Book appointment' 6867614806 and 'Qualified lead (HubSpot)' 7883211296) are Secondary with 0 conversions, so nothing bids on them today. If anyone flips 'Book appointment' to Primary, booking-button clicks would start training bids.
  • 'Book a Call - Form Submission' (6899123753) fires on a real HubSpot submission, not a click. The dentalcpausa.com homepage source has two separate listeners sending the same event (send_to AW-16673020652/3FkXCKmM4dkZEOy1qI4-). One is an inline script and the other is snippet 1827. Neither checks the form ID, so a second HubSpot form on the page would also count as a 'Book a Call' lead, and one submit can send two conversion events. Whether Google de-duplicates them depends on the action's count setting (One vs Every), which we did not pull.
  • Call actions ('Calls From Website', 'Calls from ads') were removed 10/30 but still sit in October's history. They added 1 conversion each to Brand and PMax in October, so the October 'Conversions' column overstates form leads by 2.
  • Two 'Search Exp' campaigns are ENDED experiments that still show ENABLED. They carry no spend since April. The API rejects edits to them (CANNOT_MODIFY_FOR_TRIAL_CAMPAIGN), so end or remove them in the UI so they stop showing up in reports.
