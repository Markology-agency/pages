WHAT WE
WORK NEXT.

Three focuses for the next 60 days.

01

Investigate the February keyword pullback

Keywords peaked at 2,731 in February and have come back to 2,516. An 8% decline over two months warrants a Search Console look to see which pages lost the most impressions. Refreshing those pages before they slide further is easier than recovering them after a larger drop.

Technical

Expand the Seattle small business accounting local footprint

Two Seattle terms at #1 and one at #2 show a real local search foothold. A location expansion strategy targeting Bellevue, Redmond, and Tacoma with city-specific service pages would compound the Seattle authority into a broader Pacific Northwest presence.
