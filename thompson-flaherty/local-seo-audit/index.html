THE LEGACY .PHP REDIRECT PROBLEM

Twelve old WordPress URLs from the pre-migration site are still in Google's index, ranking on positions 12-96 for the same terms the new homepage ranks #1 for. Every one of them currently 301-redirects to the homepage. That's link-equity-preserving but topical-relevance-losing — Google sees a redirect to home instead of a topic-matched destination, which dilutes the signal for keywords like "cpa tax prep," "personal tax preparers," and "tax due dates."

Fix: rewrite each .php redirect to a topic-matched destination (taxprep.php to /tax-business/, ourstaff.php to /about/, payrollservice.php to /payroll/, etc.). Equity stays consolidated; topical relevance gets restored. One-time fix via Rank Math Redirections.
