New Plays

FOUR NEW
DIRECTIONS.

Outside the standard SEO and content rhythm. Four bigger swings worth piloting.

Idea 01

Winery industry survey + annual benchmark report

Run a winery financial benchmark survey each spring: profit margins, COGS as percent of revenue, DTC versus wholesale split, average tasting room revenue per visit. Publish as a free PDF (gated by email) plus a public summary blog.

Becomes the anchor piece of content. Drives industry trade press coverage, builds an owned email list of winery operators, generates seasonal outreach hooks.

Idea 02

Octavio video series for winery owners

One short (60-90 second) video per week from Octavio explaining a winery-specific financial concept: 'Why your tasting room margin is misleading', 'How to read a winery P&L without getting lost', 'When does buying a barrel actually make sense'.

Niche audiences trust faces. Octavio's industry knowledge differentiates Llamas from generic accountants in a way text can't replicate.

Idea 03

Wine industry conference + association partnerships

Pitch Octavio onto winery industry podcasts (The XO Podcast, Wine Industry Network, NextWine), guest articles in Wine Business Monthly and Vineyard & Winery Management, and sponsor relevant winery association newsletters.

Direct access to the exact target audience plus authoritative backlinks. The winery industry is small enough that 3-4 placements substantially shift the brand presence.

Idea 04

Quarterly winery owner email newsletter

One quality email per quarter to the captured email list: seasonal tax reminders (estimated payments, year-end planning, harvest cost accounting), industry benchmark updates, new tax law changes affecting wineries.

Per the brand's lower-cadence, higher-quality voice. Builds recurring touchpoints with the in-market audience without overwhelming them.
