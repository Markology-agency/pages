Why this structure

Nav order: Services, AI Marketing for Accountants, How To, About, Book a Call (CTA button).
Services holds the channel-level retainers. SEO, GEO & AI Search, Content, Ads, Websites.
GEO page now dual-targets two keywords. "GEO for accountants" plus "AI search for accountants." Both anchors visible in nav so both keywords get nav-wide internal link equity. Page-level structure: H1 still leads with GEO, but H2 absorbs "AI search for accountants" as a separate ranking target with its own direct-answer section.
AI Marketing for Accountants is its own slot, not stuffed under Services. Headline keyword and master positioning. Now holds only Lite and Course (the two ways anyone buys Markology). Free Audit removed from this dropdown since it's a lead magnet, not a buying path.
How To pillar is "Digital Marketing for Accountants," not "Marketing for Accountants." Homepage already targets "marketing for accountants" so the pillar would have cannibalized it. Digital Marketing for Accountants captures a distinct ~720/mo direct keyword plus the channel-mix audience without competing with the homepage. ChatGPT for Accountants and Blog round out the dropdown.
Pricing removed from nav. If you want it back later, easiest spot is inside the AI Marketing dropdown next to Lite and Course.
Book a Call is the only nav CTA. High-intent action. Free Audit can still live on the page bodies as a softer secondary capture, but it doesn't need nav real estate.
