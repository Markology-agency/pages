Big Strategic Question

THE ATLANTIC PAYROLL
TWO-BRAND CALL.

Steve Ellard runs two Markology clients: Steven M. Ellard CPA (this site, Cape Cod accounting and tax) and Atlantic Payroll Partners (separate brand, payroll only). CLAUDE.md is explicit: never mix content between them. The data below is the conflict.

The Conflict

590 monthly searches for "atlantic payroll" are landing on the wrong domain.

SEMrush shows the highest-volume keyword in the entire Steve Ellard CPA ranking set is "atlantic payroll" at position #11. It's pointing at stevenmellardcpa.com/payroll-services-cape-cod/. That's a Steve Ellard CPA page ranking for the Atlantic Payroll Partners brand name. Whoever clicks that result is going to one site but expecting the other.

Brand Mismatch

What's happening underneath: Google sees the Steve Ellard CPA payroll service page as the topical authority for "Cape Cod payroll" plus a partial match on the Atlantic Payroll brand string. That's why a CPA page is ranking on a payroll brand query. The fix has to come from us, not Google.

Option 01
Redirect the demand to the Atlantic Payroll Partners site

Add the Atlantic Payroll brand name to the stevenmellardcpa.com payroll page only in a "Looking for Atlantic Payroll Partners? Visit their site →" disclaimer near the top. Build a parallel /atlantic-payroll/ landing page on the Atlantic Payroll Partners domain that targets the exact brand term. Let Google reassign the ranking signal over 60-90 days. Add cross-brand canonical hint via internal linking.

Recommended
Option 02
Keep the demand on stevenmellardcpa.com and target it harder

Treat "atlantic payroll" as a Cape Cod payroll discovery query and let Steve Ellard CPA convert that traffic into accounting clients. Add Atlantic Payroll context to the page, target the term explicitly, push the position from #11 to top 5. Note: this violates the two-brand separation rule in CLAUDE.md and risks brand confusion for the Atlantic Payroll Partners side.

Off-Brand Risk
Option 03
Let it ride at #11 and stop optimizing

Leave the page as-is. At #11 it's just below the page-1 fold. Click-through rate is minimal at that position. No active reinforcement of the brand bleed, but no fix either. The 590 monthly searches keep landing on the wrong domain at low volume.

Status Quo
Markology recommendation: Option 01. The current setup actively confuses the two brands every time someone searches "atlantic payroll" and clicks the #11 result. The Atlantic Payroll Partners brand is supposed to be the answer to "atlantic payroll," and Markology is the agency for both clients. Redirecting demand to the correct site protects the brand boundary, keeps Steve's CPA site focused on Cape Cod accounting search intent, and over 60-90 days lifts the Atlantic Payroll Partners domain on its own brand term. The "Target atlantic payroll more aggressively" task is currently in Internal Review pending this call.
