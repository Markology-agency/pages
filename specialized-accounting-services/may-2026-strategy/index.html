The Strategic Question

THE ROBS GAP
IS THE WHOLE GAME.

The strategic question for the July restart is whether Markology focuses the first month back on a deliberate ROBS cluster rebuild. The data says yes.

ROBS (Rollover for Business Startups) is SAS's defining niche. It is the highest-intent franchise topic, the most differentiated against generalist franchise accountants, and the cluster with the deepest existing content footprint on the legacy domain. Five ROBS keywords are still ranking through sas-cpas.com 301s today (positions #5, #14, #18, #42, #46). On the new domain, only robs accounting services has surfaced (#3).

This is a transferable signal problem, not a content problem. Google trusts the old URL because it has the historical link authority. The new URL exists, the redirects are clean, but Google has not finished moving credit. The two levers that close this gap are direct backlinks pointing at sas-acct.com/robs/ (full authority pass) and fresh, internally-linked ROBS content on the new domain (signal reinforcement).

Markology's recommendation: make the ROBS cluster the headline of the July National Monthly restart. Three of the four blogs already in the pipeline target this niche directly. The non-brand keyword cluster rebuild task is engineered to close exactly this gap. Add a targeted top-10 referring domain outreach campaign and the ROBS rankings should migrate to sas-acct.com over the following 60 to 90 days.

The restart frame: July is not "back to business as usual." It is "back with a focused ROBS push." Lead with content + links into /robs/, hold the franchise vertical position gains as the secondary track, and roll the Ace Hardware lead magnet in alongside as the third workstream.
