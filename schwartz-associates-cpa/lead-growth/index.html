Schwartz & Associates CPA · Lead growth diagnostic · September 30 to October 23, 2026

Where Schwartz's leads come from, and where we lose them

We read every enquiry the new website has received since it went live on September 30. We looked at them next to Google Ads, Google search, the Google Business listing and website visits for the same weeks, and compared them with the old site's form history.

The new website has received 7 enquiries since it went live. Two of them were real prospects, and both found Schwartz through the Google Business listing. The other five were sales pitches, spam and a job seeker. Google Ads spent $377 in October for 95 clicks and hasn't produced an enquiry yet.

The ads send most clicks to the accounting page, and that page has no form on it. The form people do reach asks eight questions. Phone calls and lead sources aren't being recorded either, so the true count is probably higher than we can see. The five steps below fix the tracking first. Then they put the ad budget and the map listing to work before tax season. Together they should add an estimated 3 to 6 real enquiries a month from January to March.

What the data says

  • The new site didn't cause a collapse. Real visitors from search, social and other sites were 254 in October so far, against 272 in all of October 2025. The old site's form took about 8 enquiries last October, and the new one has taken 7 in 23 days. The old form's analytics event fired twice per submission, so we halved its counts.
  • October is the quiet month. On the old site, form enquiries ran about 8 a month from October to December last year and about 14 a month from January to April. The next busy stretch starts in about ten weeks.
  • We can't yet compare lead quality before and after launch. The old site's enquiries sit in HubSpot, and we don't know how many of them were real prospects. Five of the seven on the new site were junk, so the spam share matters.
  • The Google Business listing is doing the work. Both real prospects came from it, it shows first in the map for "cpa near me" and "accountant near me" from the office, and it sent 51 clicks in three weeks. The review count has held at 89 since September.
  • Google Ads reaches few searches and gets few enquiries. The New Jersey campaign showed for 10% of the searches it could have shown for. It lost 65% to ad rank and 25% to budget. The South Jersey campaign is paused. Most clicks came from broad searches like "cpa near me" and from towns an hour or more from Turnersville, such as Clifton, Fair Lawn and Toms River. A few came from people searching for other firms by name.
  • The main ad lands on a page with no form. 61 of October's 95 ad clicks went to the accounting page. Visitors who want to get in touch have to find the contact page, where the form asks eight questions before they can send it.
  • The pricing page had 89 views from US visitors since launch, the third most visited page on the site, and it has no form either.
  • Organic search is steady. Google search clicks were 457 over the last 90 days against 436 for the same period last year. Most non-brand clicks still come from "near me" searches, which the map listing wins.

The plan

1Record every lead's source, and count phone calls Hours

Why
Every form submission arrives with its source fields empty, and the site has counted one phone tap since launch. Calls placed straight from an ad aren't counted at all. Until this is fixed, every channel looks worse than it is.
What we do
We'll fill the hidden source and ad-click fields from the visitor's first page, send phone taps to Google Analytics again, and add a Google Ads conversion for calls from the ad that last 60 seconds or more.
Expected impact
No new leads by itself. It tells us which channel earns each lead, so we can put the budget where the leads are.
Owner
Markology web and ads team
By 10/31
A test enquiry from an ad click shows its source in the email and counts as a conversion in Google Ads.

2Add a short form to the accounting and pricing pages, and trim the main form Days

Why
The accounting page gets most of the ad clicks and the pricing page is the third most visited page, and neither has a form. The current form asks eight questions, including two dropdowns about who the visitor is.
What we do
We'll add a four-field form (name, email, phone, what you need help with) to the bottom of both pages, with a call button beside it. On the main form we'll keep the "how did you hear about us" question and remove the two dropdowns.
Expected impact
About 1 to 2 more real enquiries a month, based on the ad and pricing traffic these pages already get.
Owner
Markology web team
By 11/7
Both pages carry the short form and it has been tested end to end.

3Stop spam reaching Ling and Rich Hours

Why
Five of the seven enquiries were sales pitches, spam or a job seeker. The forms have only a hidden trap field, and most bots now get past that.
What we do
We'll add Google's invisible spam check to every form on the site, so real visitors see no extra step. We'll also add a line that sends job applicants to the careers page.
Expected impact
No new leads, but nearly every email Ling and Rich receive will be a real prospect, so none get missed in the noise.
Owner
Markology web team
By 10/31
The spam check is live on all forms and a test enquiry still arrives.

4Point the ads at the searches and towns that turn into clients Hours

Why
October's $377 bought 95 clicks and no enquiries. The ads show for only 10% of eligible searches, and many of the clicks were "near me" searches from North Jersey or searches for other firms.
What we do
We'll send all ads to the page with the new short form and switch the main keyword to exact match. We'll add phrases for owners looking for a small business CPA or bookkeeper in New Jersey. We'll also exclude competitor names and towns more than about 45 minutes from Turnersville. The campaign runs to 11/30. If it hasn't brought 2 real enquiries by then, we'll pause it and put that budget into step 5.
Expected impact
About 1 to 2 real enquiries a month if the changes work. If they don't, we'll know by 11/30 and stop spending on it.
Owner
Markology ads team
By 11/30
At least 2 real enquiries are traced to Google Ads, or the campaign is paused and the budget moved.

5Hold first place in the map across South Jersey before January Weeks

Why
Both real prospects came from the Google Business listing, and map results take most of the clicks on "cpa near me" searches. We've only checked the map position from the office, and people in Cherry Hill, Voorhees or Glassboro may see a different order.
What we do
We'll check the map position from a grid of towns across Gloucester, Camden and Burlington counties. In the towns where Schwartz drops, we'll focus weekly Google Business posts. We'll also set up a short review request that the office sends after each return or month-end close, since the review count hasn't moved since September.
Expected impact
About 1 to 2 more real enquiries a month from January to April, plus calls we'll be able to count after step 1.
Owner
Markology SEO team, with the office sending the review requests
By 11/22
The town-by-town map check is done, the review request is going out, and we recheck the map every two weeks.

What we need from Rich and Ling

  • Roughly how many new-client calls and referrals came in during September and October. Phone is likely the biggest source and we can't see it yet.
  • An export of the old HubSpot form submissions for 2026, or a rough count of how many were real prospects. That tells us whether the new site's lead count is up or down once spam is taken out.
  • Whether the two real prospects from October became clients.

What 90 days could look like

Today the website brings about 3 real enquiries a month, plus calls we can't count yet. If steps 2, 4 and 5 land within the ranges above, that should rise to roughly 6 to 9 real enquiries a month from January to March, when demand is highest. These are estimates based on the last few weeks and last year's season, and we'll report the real count each month on the scorecard.
