Predictive Risk Assessment
𝗠𝗼𝘀𝘁 𝗿𝗶𝘀𝗸 𝗮𝘀𝘀𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁𝘀 𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝗹𝗮𝘀𝘁 𝘆𝗲𝗮𝗿'𝘀, 𝗿𝗲𝗵𝗲𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗱. The audit universe gets refreshed with whatever felt urgent last quarter. The fastest-moving risks, the ones from the last six months, miss the cut. That is how blind spots survive, and the whole profession knows it. Predictive Risk Assessment breaks the loop: → Three AI models run the analysis independently, then reconcile → 81 external factors weighed against your internal data → Measures risk velocity, not just risk presence → Forecasts where risk concentrates next A starter audit plan your committee can engage with, interpreted and signed by a practitioner. Part of Cherry Hill's AI Toolbox. #InternalAudit #RiskAssessment #CherryHillAdvisory

Calibr is the opposite: → Inventories the AI already in use across your org → Adversary-scans your LLMs, audits your MCP servers and agent skills → Scores the program against our Trust methodology, mapped to the frameworks your board already knows → Builds what is missing: register, control map, remediation roadmap Findings come back interpreted and signed by a practitioner. AssureLens

AssureLens closes it: → Inputs from each line, mapped to your risk universe → Every provider graded 1 to 5 on quality, factored into the map → Finds coverage that is thin, doubled up, or missing → Returns a gap register and a board-ready view Interpreted and signed by a practitioner. Segra
𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗳𝗹𝗶𝗰𝘁𝘀 𝘁𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗺𝗮𝘁𝘁𝗲𝗿 𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝗯𝘂𝗿𝗶𝗲𝗱 𝗮𝗰𝗿𝗼𝘀𝘀 𝘀𝘆𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗺𝘀. Segregation of duties gets tested one system at a time, on a sample. Segra covers it: → Normalizes user permissions across 26+ systems → Generates a new system profile on the fly for anything new → Analyzes the full access population for SoD violations → Per-user findings with compensating controls → Describe a rule in plain English and it builds it Reviewed and signed by a practitioner. Part of Cherry Hill's AI Toolbox. #ThirdPartyRisk #InternalAudit #CherryHillAdvisory

The FAQ, on the company account.

𝗙𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗾𝘂𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀 𝘄𝗲 𝗴𝗼𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗶𝘀 𝘄𝗲𝗲𝗸, 𝗮𝗻𝘀𝘄𝗲𝗿𝗲𝗱 𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗮𝗶𝗴𝗵𝘁. We announced our AI-enabled team. Here is what people actually asked. "Is this software we buy?" No. Nothing to license, nothing to install. It works inside the systems you already run. "Do we have to run it?" No. You never log in. It is how our team works, not a tool we hand you. "Are the agents real people?" No, they are AI, and we say so. We named them because you check a colleague's work, not a model's. "Who signs the finding?" A practitioner. Every time. AI drafts, sorts, and flags. The conclusion is still ours. "How do we know our data is safe?" It comes down to right-to-audit provisions and the vendor's control environment. Reasonable assurance is the goal, and we hold ourselves to the standard we would test you against. The bigger point: this is where the profession is going. Better to lead it than inherit it. #InternalAudit #AIGovernance #CherryHillAdvisory
