May / June Game Plan

WHAT WE
WORK NEXT.

Three focuses for the next 60 days.

01

Investigate the traffic decline with Search Console

Four #1 rankings and stable keyword counts but 57% less traffic since December. This pattern usually means impressions are up but CTR is down — possibly from featured snippets, AI Overviews, or competitors winning the click. A Search Console click-data analysis would confirm the cause.

Technical
02

Push RSU and AMT cost basis terms from #3 to #1

'Do RSUs have a cost basis,' 'calculate cost basis for RSU,' and 'AMT cost basis' are all at #3 with consistent search demand from tech employees. These are already on page 1 and close to the top — a title tag test and fresh supporting content could flip them.

Quick Win
03

React faster to tender offer opportunities

The Databricks tender offer piece at #1 shows KB Financial can win timely, high-value searches. Building a template for tech company tender offer articles and publishing within 48 hours of announcements would compound this traffic source.
