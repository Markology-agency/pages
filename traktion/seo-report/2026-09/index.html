The short version

Over July to September you showed up in Google for 1,432 different searches and were in the top three for 513 of them. That is smaller than the June to August window (1,805 searches, 851 in the top three), so the footprint shrank this quarter.

Visits held roughly level: 115 in September against 125 in August. Most of the drop in reach came from two older articles, Average Profit of a Therapy Practice and When Can You Retire as a Therapist, which drew fewer readers than in the spring.

On your core service searches, CFO for therapists is still in the top three. The accountant for therapists searches reached the top five in August and slipped back to page two in September. No new articles went live in September, and fresh, on-topic articles are what pushed those searches up earlier in the year. The finished articles now in review are the quickest way to regain that ground.

How this is measured

Position is your average place in Google's results across every time you appeared. An average of 8 means you typically sit around eighth on the first page.

Times shown counts how often a Traktion page appeared in someone's results, whether or not they clicked.

Visits are clicks through to the site from an unpaid Google result.

Figures run to 29 September. Google reports with a two to three day delay, so the final September numbers will settle slightly higher. Comparisons use three full months on each side so that a single busy week does not distort the picture.
