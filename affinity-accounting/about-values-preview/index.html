Who will you be working with?

We’re a team of experienced Milwaukee accountants with a passion for helping our clients succeed. Each of us brings something unique to the table, but we share a common goal: to make tax season (and everything else) easier for you.

Victoria Haas, CPA

Principal

With over 18 years of public accounting experience, Victoria has served as a tax professional for both large and small CPA firms. She specializes in a variety of solutions including tax planning, tax preparation, outsourced accounting, and financial forecasting. She uses her expertise and insight to help her clients achieve their goals so they can get back to doing what they love. She fuses accounting with technology and helps her clients realize the benefits of having real-time financial metrics to make better business decisions.

Victoria earned her Bachelors of Science in Accountancy from the University of Illinois in Urbana-Champaign and her Masters in Taxation from DePaul University. She currently serves on the Board of Directors for TEMPO Milwaukee as the Treasurer.

When she’s not providing accounting or tax advice, she enjoys spending time outdoors with her husband and three children, traveling, skiing, and exploring the culinary scene.

She loves watching the Premier League and traveling with her partner and spending summer weekends with her family at their lake house.

Are We a Good Fit?

At Affinity, we believe the best results come from partnerships built on trust, clarity, and shared expectations. Here’s how we approach every client relationship:

What You Can Expect from Us:

  • Upfront pricing and timelines, so there are no surprises. (Most clients start at $950/month.)
  • Secure and respectful handling of your information—your privacy is a priority.
  • Clear, consistent communication with regular updates, so you always know where things stand.

What We Expect from You:

  • Send us your documents on time and be transparent about what's going on in your business.
  • Make timely payments and respect our process—we’re all about efficiency and mutual respect.

When It Might Not Work Out:

  • We focus on service-based businesses, so businesses like e-commerce, retail, and manufacturing may be outside our wheelhouse.
  • We want every partnership to be a win. But sometimes it doesn’t work out—and that’s okay. If there’s ongoing miscommunication, late or missing payments, or expectations that don’t align with the scope of what we offer, we may need to step back.
