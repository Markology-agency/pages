What we suggest for the ads

SuggestionWhy
1. Put the extra $500 into Google search.Google is the only ad channel with booked buyer calls we can name, and it runs out of budget on three of every four searches.
2. Leave the priced forms off Meta.43 people clicked and nobody sent the form. The form opened on a $7,500 or $4,000 price and asked for a phone number.
3. If Meta comes back, lead with the free deal screen.It is the only Meta offer that brought in people with a live deal. The QoE and tax plan prices can follow on the thank-you screen and in the welcome emails.
4. Run that test on Facebook only.Instagram took $130.91 across two campaigns and has not produced a form.
5. Keep the inventory story ad."Books said $1.2M. The count said $490K." drew more clicks than any other ad we ran.
6. Fill in the lead source on every call.The November 11 call has no source yet, so we can't credit it to a channel.
