Critical finding

The audit pulled every blog and service page, parsed every internal link, and built the directed link graph. Two structural problems jumped out.

1. /winery-consulting/ is fully orphaned

Zero pages on the site link to it: not the homepage, not the global nav, not any blog. The other five service pages each get 75-169 blog inbound links (mostly via nav). winery-consulting gets nothing. Twelve blogs that should be in its cluster currently have no path to it.

2. Service-page equity is nav-driven, not body-driven

Blogs show 18-21 inbound links per service page — but those are the same five nav links repeated three times (header, footer, secondary footer). Outside of nav, almost no blog has a contextual body anchor pointing into its topically-relevant service page. Nav links carry diluted weight; Google reads body anchors as the stronger relevance signal.

Three-phase action plan

Sequenced for impact. Phase 1 is the one-day structural fix. Phase 2 is the content-anchor rebuild. Phase 3 is the optional cross-link breadth pass.

Day 1 — Structural

Add /winery-consulting/ to nav

  • Add to footer service column

  • Add contextual link from homepage hero or services grid

Week 1 — Content anchors

Insert contextual body anchors

  • 12 winery-consulting cluster blogs get a contextual paragraph link to /winery-consulting/
  • Anchor = exact service keyword ("winery consulting" / "winery business consulting")
  • 23 other blogs get a contextual body anchor to their assigned service hub (currently only have nav links)

3
Week 2 — Breadth

  • Each blog gets one secondary internal link to a related blog in a different cluster (topical breadth)
  • Each blog confirms homepage link via "winery accountant" anchor in body

  • Pillar slug /pillar/ either repurposed as cluster hub or 301'd
