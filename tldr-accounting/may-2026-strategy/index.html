Strategic Question

FEBRUARY PULLBACK:
DEFEND OR LET DECAY?

Traffic peaked at 1,765 in February and settled to 1,039 in April. The investigation closed Internal Review last week. The takeaway is that the dip is concentrated in non-niche legacy pages, not the therapist-niche cluster.

What we know

The therapist-niche pages held their positions: "psychotherapist accountant" at #2, "accountant for therapists" at #2, "bookkeeping for therapists" at #3, the entire Seattle local cluster at #1. The pages losing ground are the older non-niche traffic drivers: EFTPS / federal-tax-payment-system, IRS payment plan, RSU box 14 (legacy tech-employee content from earlier years).

The W4 follow-up Map the session decline to specific top pages (due May 29) will pin the exact URLs and the page-level impression deltas.

The strategic question

Two paths, both defensible:

  • Defend the legacy pages. Refresh the top 3 to 5 URLs that lost the most positions. Update for current tax year, freshen the H2 structure, add internal links to the niche pages. Cost: roughly two weeks of writing/optimization time that would otherwise go to therapist niche.
  • Let them decay, reinvest in niche. Accept that the therapist-only direction means non-niche pages will keep slipping. Put the same time into expanding the Seattle local cluster and pushing the therapist-niche commercial pages from #2 to #1.

Markology recommendation: Lean toward decay-and-reinvest. The high-volume non-niche pages (RSU, EFTPS, payment plan) bring traffic but not therapist leads. The niche pages convert. We will confirm the page-by-page numbers in the W4 report and bring a final read after that data is in.

Documented Seasonal Decay

TWO PAGES ARE
CALENDAR-DRIVEN, NOT BROKEN.

The W4 session decline map isolated two pages whose April drop is seasonal, not algorithmic. We are not chasing them. They will rebound on the tax calendar.

The two seasonal pages
  • /what-does-it-mean-when-you-sign-your-8879/ — Form 8879 is the IRS e-file signature authorization. Traffic peaks in March and April when therapists are e-signing their returns. Falls off a cliff May through December. Expected rebound: January 2027.
  • /restricted-stock-unit-taxes-and-your-w-2/ — Same pattern. RSU vesting and W-2 reconciliation are tax-filing-window queries. Peaks in March and April, then drops. Expected rebound: January 2027.
Decision

No refresh, no rewrite, no internal-link surgery. These pages are doing their job in the months that matter. Treating them as ranking losses would burn writing hours on a calendar we cannot beat.

Cross-reference: The W4 map ranked all top URLs by impact. Salary page (Action 1) is the single fixable miss; EFTPS payment-plan family is the decay-or-defend decision tied to the Strategic Question above; these two are pure seasonality.
