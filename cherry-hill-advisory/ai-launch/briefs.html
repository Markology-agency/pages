Rules for every graphic

Brand colors, no greenNavy #011E51 / #0B2C60 and electric blue #089BFF. Mike killed the green screener band by name.
Sora + InterSora for headlines, Inter for body. Already on the site.
No IIA logoBlack out the "Authorized Licensee" mark on every public graphic. Proposals only.
Less textShort headline, message in the caption. "Will I read this scrolling in bed? No." No text galore.
Fun + modernThe explainer video's look, color bars, motion, a data feel. Not stock boardroom photos.
Attribute the IIA"The IIA released…" in copy. Never anything that reads as if Cherry Hill wrote the Topical Requirements.
ChatGPT, not GeminiMike's call. ChatGPT's latest image gen beats Gemini and hallucinates less.
No em dashes, use %Client copy rules. Commas or colons, and the % symbol, never the word.
01

Homepage hero

temporary · one week

Cherry Hill grew: its human team, joined by a visibly-AI agent bench. The current image is too formal and reads too close to the old hero. Push it forward-leaning and a little provocative.

The concept

"Cherry Hill just grew by 19." Human practitioners on one side, a larger bench of clearly-AI agents on the other. Two cuts: a buttoned-up version for the company homepage, a fun version for Mike's personal.

Must fix
The figures must read as AI, not real colleagues. "They're not part of the real team." Give the agent figures a clearly synthetic treatment, humanoid but obviously digital: a subtle wireframe or particle edge, a cooler blue tint, an "AI" signal, ideally with names surfacing (Helena, Renata). The real practitioners stay human. The contrast is the whole point.

Hero copy (company):

Eyebrow: NOW LIVE: AI-ENABLED SERVICES H1: Cherry Hill just grew by 19. Sub: A new AI agent team, working alongside the practitioners who lead every engagement. It's time to move our profession forward. Buttons: Meet the team → · Introductory Call
ChatGPT image promptA wide, modern hero image for an internal-audit firm launching AI services. Left side: a small group of real human professionals in business attire around a table, warm and credible. Right side: a larger group of clearly synthetic AI "agents", humanoid figures with a subtle digital wireframe/particle treatment and a cool blue glow, visibly not human, arranged like a growing team. Deep navy background (#011E51) with electric-blue (#089BFF) accents and soft data-visualization light bars. Cinematic, forward-leaning, premium, not cheesy. No text, no logos.

Specs: match the existing hero container + a 1200×627 announcement cut.

02

Screener graphic

on hold until the tool is live

The current one is too wordy and doesn't convey what the tool does. It must sell: AI-powered, free, live on the site, automatically screens your organization for IIA Topical Requirement conformance.

  • Cut the copy to one short line. Lead with AI + free + instant, not a paragraph.
  • Keep the tool screenshot minimal or drop it if it adds text noise.
  • Attribute the IIA. Brand navy/blue, no green.

Headline options: "Free, AI-powered Topical Requirement screening" · "See where you stand on the IIA Topical Requirements, free, in minutes."

Fix
The current graphic gives two different times, "about 15 minutes" and "2 minutes to complete." Pick one.

Specs: 1200×627 + 1080×1350.
