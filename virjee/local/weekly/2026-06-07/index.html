The pivot is working. Since the January 2026 healthcare focus launched, the SEMrush ranking-keyword footprint has grown from 458 to 1,074 (+134%), the medical-segment GSC impressions have grown +382% (570 in January → 2,746 in May), and the average search position has improved from 6.6 to 5.0 (+1.6 positions) — the domain has moved from mid-page-1 to the top of page 1 across its entire query base.

Houston presence is holding. Local Houston rankings did not collapse during the pivot. Houston-segment impressions held steady through the year (+87% Jan → May) and Virjee retains 170 ranking keywords in the Houston cluster, including #1-#5 positions on core business queries like "houston bookkeeping services for small business" and "houston tx payroll service".

Medical national footprint is the growth engine. 34 medical and healthcare-practice keywords now ranking nationally, top positions including "healthcare accountant houston" (#1), "medical accountant houston" (#2), "optometrist accountant" (#4), and "tax deductions for doctors" (#13). The next 90 days should push more of these into the top 10 as the cluster matures.
