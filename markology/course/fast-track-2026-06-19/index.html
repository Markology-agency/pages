The three badges

EveryoneEveryone does this, regardless of local or national
LocalDo this if you serve a local geography
NationalDo this if you serve a national niche

Local accountants (CPA serving the Atlanta metro, small-firm partner targeting Cape Cod, etc.) need Google Business Profile, local citations, local networking, direct mail. National-niche accountants (dental CPA serving practices nationwide, marketing-agency accountant, restaurant CFO across 30 states) need content velocity, LinkedIn personal brand, podcast guesting, niche-industry partnerships. Pick what applies. If both, do both.

Not in fast-track

What's in the deep-dive that fast-track skips

Fast-track is the trimmed path to leads. The deep-dive ("Teach Me Marketing") goes WIDER and DEEPER. Once you have leads flowing, you graduate to the chapters below. Same subscription. Same Claude skills compounding.

  • Entire Month 2: Integrate with Your Existing Tools (Canopy, Carbon, Karbon, QuickBooks, CRM, workflow). The moat, but not required to get first leads.
  • Entire Month 11: YouTube + video production. Optional even in deep-dive. Skip unless on-camera is your thing.
  • Marketing fundamentals chapters 3.2 through 3.5: How marketing works, inbound vs outbound, channel deep-dives. Useful context but not required to ship.
  • Messaging chapters 4.8 and 4.10: AI tools deep-dive, content tool roundup. Skills handle this in fast-track.
  • SEO research chapters 5.2, 5.7, 5.8: Metrics deep-dive, free tools list, analysis templates. Skills cover the doing.
  • Website conversion chapters 6.2, 6.3, 6.5 through 6.8, 6.14: Platform comparisons, hosting options, build-vs-hire. Useful if you're starting from scratch, skippable if you have a site.
  • Tracking + reporting chapters 7.6, 7.7, 7.9, 7.10: GSC deep-dive, weekly check-in routine, quarterly review, when to pivot. Strategic chapters that come after the basics.
  • Monthly SEO + content chapters 8.1, 8.6, 8.11, 8.12, 8.13, 8.15: Schema, SEO tools deep-dive, blog rewrite framework, back-end SEO, AI search visibility. Layer in once you're publishing weekly.
  • Email chapters 9.6 through 9.11: Lead magnet placement, sales automation, newsletter strategy, subject lines, getting subscribers. Comes after basic email is working.
  • Social chapters 10.3, 10.5, 10.7: Posting frequency, scheduling tools deep-dive, the social hater routine. Refinements after you start posting.
  • Channels chapters 12.1 through 12.5, 12.9, 12.10: Google AdWords, webinars, Reddit, communities, podcast guesting, partnerships, where to find them. Long-tail growth.
