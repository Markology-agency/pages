The two options, restated

B and C share the same homepage-as-master-hub spine. They differ on how many sub-hubs sit underneath.

Option B — Two-Tier

Default · Recommended for all 20

Homepage as master hub for the head keyword. Service pages as sub-hubs. Each blog spokes into its one most-relevant service page using the exact service keyword as anchor text, plus links up to homepage.

  • Concentrates equity on the pages that convert
  • Matches Markology's link-equity ladder exactly
  • Templates cleanly across 20 clients
  • Per-blog decision is binary: which service page is most relevant?
  • Lower writing budget required

Option C — Multi-Pillar

Phase-2 promotion for select clients

Same spine as B PLUS 2-4 long-form pillar guides as additional non-commercial hubs. Blogs cluster under whichever hub fits — service or pillar. More ranking surface area, more writing work.

  • Activates info-intent search demand
  • Pillar pages are great LLM extraction targets
  • Needs service pages already ranking top-5 to justify spread
  • Requires 2-4 net-new long-form pages first
  • National-tier budget only
