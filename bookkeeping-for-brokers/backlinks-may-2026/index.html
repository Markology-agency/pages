SEND. TRACK.

Standard outreach cycle: send Monday or Tuesday morning eastern time, follow up once after 10 business days, drop pitches that get no response after the follow-up. Track every send in the agency CRM.

  • Send first round (Monday/Tuesday AM ET) from a real human inbox, not a no-reply.
  • Track each send in CRM: publication, editor email, date sent, status.
  • Follow up exactly once, 10 business days after first send. Short reply, no guilt.
  • If accepted: agree to deadline, deliver draft early, submit clean Word doc with bio + headshot link + anchored backlinks already in body.
  • After publish: confirm backlink renders as do-follow (right-click → inspect → check for rel="nofollow").
  • Log final placement URL in the agency backlink tracker so next month's report shows the new referring domain.
