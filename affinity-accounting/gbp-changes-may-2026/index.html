Week 2

Manufacturing & Agriculture Credit

Title: Wisconsin's most-missed tax credit

Body: If you produce or sell tangible goods in Wisconsin, the Manufacturing and Agriculture Credit can wipe out almost all of your state income tax. We see qualifying businesses miss it because their generalist CPA never flagged it. 90-minute review tells you if you qualify.

CTA button: Book consultation → https://affinitymke.com/contact/

Week 3

S-Corp election timing

Title: When the S-corp election actually saves money

Body: Most Wisconsin business owners hear about the S-corp election from a friend and ask if they should elect. The honest answer: usually yes above $90K in net profit, usually no below $60K, depends in between. The salary reasonableness math is what most CPAs get wrong.

CTA button: Learn more → https://affinitymke.com/tax-accountant-milwaukee/

Week 4

Bookkeeping > software

Title: QuickBooks isn't a bookkeeper

Body: The single most common mistake we see: business owners running on DIY QuickBooks, assuming the software is doing the work. QuickBooks records transactions. It does not categorize them correctly, catch reconciliation gaps, or build a tax-strategy-ready P&L. That's what a bookkeeper does.

CTA button: Get started → https://affinitymke.com/bookkeepers-milwaukee/

Week 5

CFO services threshold

Title: When a Milwaukee business needs a CFO

Body: Most Milwaukee businesses don't need a full-time CFO until $5M in revenue. Most need fractional CFO support starting around $1M. The signal: you're making capex decisions without a clear payback model, or running payroll without knowing your cash runway. Both are CFO problems.

CTA button: Learn more → https://affinitymke.com/cfo-services-milwaukee/
