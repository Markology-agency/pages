Questions and answers

Before you book

Do we have to change our booking or point-of-sale system?

No. We work from the systems you already use and set up your books so each service, product line and provider can be seen on its own.

What do we look at each month?

Profit by service after product and provider pay, payroll against revenue, package money still owed in treatments, and the next 13 weeks of cash.

Which level fits a med spa?

We'd start with Growth. It includes the monthly CFO partnership, the 13-week cash flow forecast and client and project profitability, which is where profit by service sits.

What about taxes?

Business and personal tax planning and preparation are available alongside any level, so your tax work and your monthly numbers come from the same team.
