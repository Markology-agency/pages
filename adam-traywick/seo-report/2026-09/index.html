Work Completed in September

  • Fixed homepage competition for "fort worth cpa". Three pages (About, Blog and the best-CPAs list) were telling Google they were copies of the homepage. Each now points to itself, so the homepage is the one page competing for the term.
  • Google Business Profile brief. New category, description, Q&A and service area prepared to push the Maps listing toward the local top 3.
  • 5 new internal links across the trades cluster: year-end tax planning, job costing for plumbers, field service software, the mid-year contractor check-in and bookkeeping for electricians.
  • Sitemap resubmitted to Google Search Console so the cleaned-up site gets recrawled.
  • Citation and link targets identified, led by the Fort Worth Chamber and the TXCPA directory, the two pages that rank #1 and #2 for "cpa fort worth".

What's Working

  • Year over year, search clicks are up 181% (83 to 233) and organic sessions are up 81% (186 to 336) for the same September dates.
  • Core Fort Worth terms hold page one: "fort worth accountants" #2, "small business accountant" #2, "accountant fort worth" #4.
  • The real estate page broke onto page one in Fort Worth and page two nationally within the month.
  • Trades content (S corp, truck write-offs, barbers, salons) keeps sitting in positions 4 to 6 and brings in most of the clicks.

What to Watch

  • Clicks are down 8% against the same August dates. The drop tracks the loss of low-ranking impressions rather than lost positions. We check it again in October.
  • "which is a disadvantage of s corporations?" showed 823 times at position 9.4 for 1 click. A stronger title on that page is the fastest win on the site.
  • "cpa fort worth" sat at 5 for most of the month and ended at 9. Reviews and the Maps listing are the levers for the top 3.
  • "quickbooks cleanup", "construction accountant", "fort worth payroll" and "fort worth cfo services" are tracked but not ranking. Each needs a dedicated page before it can rank.

October Plan

  • Rewrite titles on the highest-impression pages, starting with the QBI guide, the $60K S corp post, the truck write-off post and the S corp disadvantages page.
  • Submit the Fort Worth Chamber and TXCPA directory listings.
  • Keep the weekly Google Business Profile posts going and push toward 75 reviews by December.
  • Scope one new service page for the QuickBooks cleanup and payroll searches.
