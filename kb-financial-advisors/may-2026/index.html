The Story This Month

AI ENGINES ARE
DOING THE SELLING.

This is the model working as designed, but it doesn't look like traditional SEO performance.

The AI-Discovery Funnel

KB's deep equity-comp content (RSU cost basis, AMT, QSBS, IPO planning, Databricks tender offer) earns citations inside AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude answers. A founder asks an AI engine "how does QSBS work" or "what is my RSU cost basis." The AI quotes KB Financial Advisors and Landon by name as the source.

The founder then searches "KB Financial Advisors" or "Landon Loveall" directly on Google. That's the branded GSC search. From there they land on the homepage and convert.

The proof: branded impressions went from 226 in February to 371 in April. That's +64% in two months while KB ran zero brand-awareness ads or PR campaigns. The only thing that changed: deep equity-comp posts kept ranking and got cited more.

This is also why direct clicks on deep posts look low. The /databricks-tender-offer-2026/ page pulled 17,117 Google impressions but only 29 clicks (0.17% CTR) last 30 days. AI engines are extracting the answer and showing the citation, but readers don't click through, they search the brand instead. Don't kill that page. It's earning brand search.
