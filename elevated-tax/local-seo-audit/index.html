SHIP LOCALBUSINESS JSON-LD SCHEMA + EMBEDDED MAP

The single highest-ROI fix. Add LocalBusiness (or Accountant) schema to the homepage and contact page with full NAP, geo coordinates, openingHoursSpecification, areaServed (Missoula, Bozeman, Hamilton), sameAs (LinkedIn, Instagram), and the "Best of Missoula 2025" as an award/credential. Embed the Google Map of 101 E Front St on the contact page. Schema gives Google explicit signals about the local entity — currently it's inferring from on-page text only.

Est. impact: 5-15% local-pack visibility lift within 30 days. Foundation for every other local signal.
