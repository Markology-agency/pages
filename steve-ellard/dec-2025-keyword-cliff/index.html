THE DECEMBER 2025
KEYWORD CLIFF.

A read-only investigation into why SEMrush showed Steve Ellard's keyword count drop from 66 to 29 between November and December 2025. The short version: it was not a ranking loss. It was a site migration, and the most valuable keywords got better, not worse.

Domain: stevenmellardcpa.com Window: Nov 2025 → May 2026 Source: SEMrush US + live URL checks Asana Task: [SEO] Investigate the December keyword drop
The headline finding

The keyword count dropped because Steve's site went through a structural migration in late November 2025. The old /resources/blog/ and /what-we-do/ URLs were replaced with a flatter /blog/ and root-level service-page structure. SEMrush re-indexed under the new URLs in December. The brand terms ("steven ellard", "steve ellard") jumped from position 4-8 to position 1, traffic 8x'd from 17 to 140 estimated visits per month. This was a migration win that looked like a cliff in the raw count.
