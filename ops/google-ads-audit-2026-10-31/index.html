Every Google Ads account

21 accounts. Every ad, keyword, location, angle and conversion goal, read from the accounts on Oct 31. Nothing was changed. Form leads only; brand reported separately.

Across all 21 accounts

  • Only one account has conversion tracking we can trust. Adam Traywick is set up correctly. Fifteen need fixing and five are broken. In most accounts Google still bids on calls, phone-number clicks, page views or local actions, so it chases cheap calls instead of form leads. Several "form" goals are really button clicks (KEEN, Aladdin, Core, Vast, Training Loft).
  • We can't confirm the form tag fires on most sites. Before any account restarts, someone should send a test form fill and see it land in Google Ads: U-Nique, Llamas, Cherry Hill, Ashford, SAS, Tarleton, Traktion, Core and Virjee Local.
  • Most ads land on service pages. Every account has landing-page fixes listed, and Thompson Flaherty's pages have no consultation form at all. The landing page briefs for Adi are in the Drive folder "Ad Briefs (Google Ads audit 2026-10-31)".
  • Two angles are proven. Adam Traywick's "Top-rated Fort Worth CPAs" has 22 form leads at $51, and Tarleton's Dallas probate ads have 13 at about $95. Both lose impressions to budget.
  • Brand is carrying some accounts. Traktion's brand PMax brought 24 of its 26 bookings. Virjee Dental's brand search brought 25 of its 63 form leads.
  • Demand Gen lookalikes. None of the accounts are ready today, but 8 could be once tracking works: Virjee Dental, SAS, Tarleton, Affinity, Traktion, Ashford, Adam Traywick and Cherry Hill. Virjee's seed list from HubSpot is pulled and has not been uploaded.
  • Accounts to confirm before any work: Training Loft, Aladdin and Elevated (no current work), plus BizBud and KEEN.
