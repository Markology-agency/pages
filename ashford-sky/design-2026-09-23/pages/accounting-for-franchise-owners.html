The franchise-specific problems we handle

A generalist bookkeeper can close your month. They usually can't produce what your franchisor asks for, or tell you which of your locations is quietly losing money.

01

Royalty and ad fund tracking

Royalties, national ad fund, local co-op, and technology fees each land in their own account, reconciled to what the franchisor actually drafted. When their statement doesn't match your sales, we find out why.

02

Books in the franchisor's format

We build your chart of accounts to the franchisor's required structure and produce the monthly and annual reports in the format they ask for. No re-keying, no late submissions, no scrambling before an audit.

03

One view across every location

Each unit keeps its own P&L, then rolls up into a consolidated view with location-by-location margins, labor percentages, and royalty burden side by side.

Tax for franchise owners

Franchise fees, territory rights, buildout, and equipment all get treated differently for tax. The initial franchise fee alone has its own write-off rules, and the multi-entity structures that come with a second or third location add complexity a generalist preparer won't plan around.

We handle your business and personal returns as one picture, and plan ahead of year end rather than reporting on it after.
