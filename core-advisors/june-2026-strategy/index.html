Where the leads are leaking

The keyword work is doing its job. The conversion path from impression to form submit is not. Three breakpoints, in order of leverage.

Breakpoint 1: CTR is the wall

The top five lead-relevant pages get 24,011 impressions every 90 days and convert 24 of them to clicks. The average is 0.10%. Title and meta description rewrites can plausibly take this to 1.5% to 3.0% within four to six weeks. That alone produces an extra 280 to 700 organic clicks per quarter, on top of what is happening today.

Breakpoint 2: PMax is paying for button clicks, not leads

In the last 90 days, the PMax campaign drove 24 "Schedule a Call" button clicks but only 6 actual lead-form submits. That is a 4-to-1 ratio of intent to completion. The button-click tracking is correctly excluded from the primary conversions metric, so PMax is not optimizing against those button clicks. But the gap itself tells us the form is the friction: people click the CTA, see what the form asks for, and bail. Trimming the form fields or moving to an embedded scheduler is the move.

Breakpoint 3: Search ads are off, so the high-intent terms are unfunded

Search campaigns are all paused after CPCs ran $7.18 average last cycle. Meanwhile PMax holds only 9.99% Search impression share with 90% lost to ad rank. That means the bottom-funnel terms ("dental cfo", "financial advisor for dentists", "wealth management for dentists") get almost no paid pickup, and the SEO traffic has to do all the conversion work. A tightly scoped exact-match Search campaign with a $5 CPC ceiling on a $300 monthly cap reopens that lane.

The game plan

Seven plays. Sequenced by leverage and dependency. Each one is also queued in Asana on the Core Advisors June board.

01

Title-rewrite sweep on the top six leaking pages

Rewrite the title tag and meta description for /best-dental-financial-advisors, /cost-to-buy-dental-practice, /free-dental-practice-valuation-calculator, /profit-margin-for-dentists, /dental-cfo service page, and /dental-payroll service page. New titles lead with the search term, include the year, end with the value promise. Conservative model: 1.5% CTR lifts these six pages from 29 clicks per quarter to 360. Even at half that we are adding 50 organic clicks per month immediately.

This Week
02

Audit and simplify the lead-form fields

In 90 days PMax sent 24 people to click "Schedule a Call" but only 6 finished the form. That is a 75% drop after click. Audit Gravity Form #1 (homepage / contact) for field count, conditional logic, and validation friction. Hypothesis: cutting from current state down to name, email, dentist-or-not, and one open question moves completion rate from 25% to 50%-plus. Test it on the homepage CTA for two weeks before rolling out.

This Week
03

Rebuild the calculator's conversion path

Calculator page gets 4,187 impressions per quarter and almost no leads. Add a soft email capture before the result reveal ("get your benchmark range emailed plus a one-page interpretation"), trigger a three-email follow-up sequence, and only ask for a Calendly booking after step three. The result is a calculator that captures most of the visitors and warms them before the call ask.

30 Days
04

Re-author the "best dental financial advisors" blog as the Core Advisors POV

The current page is a listicle that ranks competitors next to Core. With 8,003 impressions per quarter at position 13.3 it is the single biggest organic asset. Rewrite as "How dentists should evaluate a financial advisor" with the Core integrated-team thesis as the spine and the comparative table as supporting context. Goal: move from 0.12% CTR to 1.5%, capture the click, and convert at the page's CTA.

30 Days
05

Reactivate Search ads on three exact-match terms only

Relaunch a single Search campaign with three keywords on exact match: [dental cfo], [financial advisor for dentists], [wealth management for dentists]. Max CPC bid $5.00 with a $300 monthly cap. Tight ad copy that names dentists in the H1 and points to a dedicated landing page (not the homepage). This reopens the high-intent lane without the $7 CPC bleed that paused the old campaign.

30 Days
06

Rewrite the three service pages that already rank but never click

/dental-cfo (1,334 impressions, 0 clicks at position 26), /wealth-management-for-dentists (1,330 impressions, 1 click), and /dental-payroll (1,317 impressions, 1 click). All three rank but the pages are not converting attention to clicks or clicks to leads. Rewrite each opener with a direct one-sentence answer to "why dentists choose Core for X", add an above-fold lead form, and use the new content to inform the Search ad landing pages from play 5.

60 Days
07

Set up monthly tracking on real lead-form submits, not button clicks

Build a single Looker Studio dashboard that pulls real lead-form completions (Submit lead form_Form 1 + Form 2 in Google Ads, plus any organic-attributed submits from GA4) into one view, segmented by source. This becomes the lead-volume number we report on monthly. Without this we keep flying blind on whether the seven plays are working.

60 Days

New plays worth testing

Four ideas outside the core remediation track. Each one targets a gap the current funnel has.

Dentist-specific city pages

"Dental CFO Phoenix", "Dental CFO Austin", "Dental CFO Charlotte" templated pages targeting the top 10 dental-density US metros. Each page localized with state-specific tax notes and a city-tagged form. Pure long-tail capture play that the integrated-team positioning is unusually well-suited for.

The "DSO vs solo" calculator

A second interactive tool: input practice revenue and EBITDA, get an estimate of what a DSO offer might be vs. holding for an independent sale. This is a buyer-magnet for the practice-owner audience Core most wants to reach, and there is no good free tool in market.

Quarterly dental finance email digest

A four-page PDF every quarter: industry margin benchmarks, tax law updates, one practice owner case study, one investment positioning note. Email-gated download. Pulls the long-tail "average dental practice profit margin" / "best banks for retirement planning for dentists" searches that today get zero clicks.

LinkedIn outreach against the calculator

Once the calculator is gated, run a LinkedIn DM sequence to dental practice owners offering the "12-point dental practice valuation" as a no-pitch resource. Brand-aligned with the integrated-team thesis and converts the calculator's discoverability into a sales channel.

What we are doing to drive more leads

Rankings opened the top of the funnel. These are the workstreams we are running now to convert that traffic into actual lead-form submits. Top four are in motion this month; secondary plays follow in July.

Hotjar install + 14-day funnel diagnostic

Heatmaps and session recordings on /contact/, the homepage, and the top five landing pages. 32 organic clicks per week is real traffic landing on real pages, and zero of them are submitting the form. The diagnostic tells us where the drop happens (form field, scroll depth, CTA visibility) so the fixes target the right thing instead of guessing.

Meta retargeting to past-180-day site visitors

Pixel-based audience of everyone who has visited coreadvisors.com in the last 180 days, served light testimonial / value-prop ads on Facebook and Instagram. Cheap CPM, high relevance, perfect channel when the funnel leaks at the form stage. Starting at $15/day (about $450 per month) with a 30-day creative refresh cadence.

Contact form redesign + lead magnet

Two changes. First, test a stripped-down three-field form (name, email, "what's on your mind") against the current form to see if conversion rate triples. Second, gate the printable output of the goals calculator behind an email capture so visitors who are not ready to talk still leave a contact we can nurture.

Conversion tracking audit + PMax / Search split

Two adjacent fixes. We verify the Google Ads conversion gtag actually fires on form submit (a gap we caught on several portfolio clients this quarter). We also split the PMax USA campaign, which is delivering 3,128 impressions and 68 clicks a week with zero conversions, into a Search-only campaign with named keywords so we control match types instead of trusting the PMax black box.

Secondary plays queued for July

Activate the Dental USA Search campaign

The new "Search - Core Advisors - Dental USA - 2026-06" campaign is currently delivering zero impressions. Diagnose the bid / geo / eligibility issue and relaunch so it actually carries traffic against the dental-focused city pages.

Once the Meta retargeting baseline is in market, layer LinkedIn matched audiences (best for the business-owner ICP) and Google Demand Gen (YouTube + Discover + Gmail inventory) so the same warm audience sees Core across every surface they spend time on.

Negative keyword sprint on Search and PMax

Same playbook we just ran for Steve Ellard this month: block named competitor CPAs, free-tax-aid searches, and software-research queries that drain budget without converting. Typically frees up 15 to 25 percent of monthly spend to redirect into the high-intent terms.

Blog visitor email capture and nurture

The organic blog traffic is climbing but has no capture mechanism. Add a topic-aware newsletter pop-up that auto-tags subscribers by article subject, then drip a four-email sequence ending in a soft consultation CTA. Turns the SEO win into a long-term email list, not just a one-shot session.
