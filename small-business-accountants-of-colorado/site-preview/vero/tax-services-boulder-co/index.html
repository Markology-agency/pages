How Tax Work Went From 80-Hour Weeks to a Year-Round Job

When some of us started in this work, tax season meant 80-hour weeks until April and then a skeleton crew for the rest of the year. Back then we filed extensions for a couple of people, and that was about it.

FAQ

What Should You Know Before Booking Tax Preparation in Boulder?

How much does tax preparation in Boulder cost with your team?

Personal returns start at $750, and S corporation and partnership returns start at $1,350. Those are starting fees, so we'll talk through what your own returns involve when you call.

Do your tax services in Boulder cover both my business and my personal return?

We prepare business returns for S corporations, partnerships and C corporations, and we prepare the owner's personal return alongside. Having one team do both means the two returns are built from the same numbers.

Will you help with tax planning if someone else prepares my return?

Tax planning is something we do only for the clients whose returns we prepare. A plan works when the same people carry it through to the return, so if you'd like our advice, we'd prepare your return too.

Do I have to live in Boulder to work with you?

We work with clients across Boulder County and in Denver. Our one office is in Boulder, so Denver clients usually work with us by phone and email, and they're welcome to visit us on Table Mesa Drive.

Is my business too small or too big for your firm?

We work with businesses doing from about $50,000 to $10 million a year in revenue. If you're unsure where you fall, call us and we'll tell you honestly whether we're a fit.
