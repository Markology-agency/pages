THE TESTIMONIALS PROBLEM

"Hear what our clients have to say" appears as a homepage section header. The actual testimonials section is empty (or so subtle the WebFetch couldn't surface them). For an accounting firm targeting therapy-practice owners — an audience that takes ~3 months to evaluate a new accountant — testimonials are the conversion driver. Heard Accounting (Traktion's biggest direct competitor in this niche) anchors their entire site on video testimonials from real therapists. Closing this gap is the single biggest lift, ahead of any schema or AI fix.

Action: Get 3-5 video or text testimonials from current Traktion therapy-practice clients. Include practice name, location, "before Traktion / after Traktion" framing. Add to homepage and service pages. Encode in Review schema.
