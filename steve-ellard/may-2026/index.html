December Cliff

THE DECEMBER 2025
KEYWORD DROP.

Single open investigation that explains 75% of the year-over-year story.

What SEMrush showed: domain_rank_history reported 92 keywords in September 2025, 88 in October, 66 in November, then 29 in December. Partial recovery to 31 in January, holding 29-31 through March, climbing to 49 in April and 47 in May.

What Google Search Console actually shows: total monthly impressions grew from ~860 in November to ~2,400 in December (3x). 91 queries returned impressions in Nov vs 115 in Dec (more, not fewer). The ten big winners in December were Cape Cod long-tail tax-prep queries that surged 150-230 impressions each ("payroll service company cape cod ma" 231 imp, "tax prep for small businesses in cape cod" 182 imp, "tax preparation company in cape cod" 182 imp). Only 8 query-level losses, totalling ~190 impressions: "atlantic payroll" -112 imp (still ranking pos 5, likely post-tax-deadline payroll seasonality), "payroll" generic -36 imp (position fell 5 to 14), "atlantic payroll brewster ma" -16 imp, plus 5 tiny losses.

Verdict: the SEMrush keyword count drop reflects its own ranking-position threshold, not a real Google ranking loss. The site's actual Google performance grew through the period we'd been calling a cliff. The W3 investigation task can close as resolved.
