How we think about it

Three things we tell every client about equity planning

i.

Plan against the marginal rate, not the withholding rate

Default federal withholding on RSUs is 22%. Your marginal rate is usually 32% to 37%. The gap shows up in April. The whole RSU playbook is closing that gap before it surprises you.

ii.

The window decides the strategy

Pre-IPO, IPO, lockup, post-IPO. Each window has different moves available. The biggest mistakes come from running pre-IPO plays after the bell rings.

iii.

Founders compound advantage with structure

83(b) at incorporation. QSBS at five years. Spousal stacking before a liquidity event. The exemption math works when you start before the upside is obvious, not after.
