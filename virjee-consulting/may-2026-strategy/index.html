Idea 01

Houston physician + dental practice email series

Capture emails on the medical accountant and dental CPA pages with a 'Houston Medical Practice Tax Checklist' lead magnet. Then a 5-email educational series specific to physician/dental practice owners.

Quarterly 'Houston Small Business Tax Brief'

A quarterly email or PDF synthesizing Texas/Houston-specific tax updates: state filing deadlines, local incentive programs, federal changes affecting Texas businesses. Distributed via email and posted to the site.

Builds recurring marketing asset, positions the firm as the Houston small business tax authority, generates seasonal organic content.

Local Houston content partnerships

Co-published articles with the Houston Business Journal, Greater Houston Partnership, Texas Society of CPAs, and similar local publications. Mix of guest posts, expert quotes, and survey-based industry reports.
