Title TagHigh Lift

Current: 3 Best Dental Financial Advisors We Recommend - CoreAdvisors
Proposed: Best Dental Financial Advisors of 2026: 3 Firms Dentists Trust | Core Advisors

Adds the year qualifier (signals freshness, captures "2026" intent), keeps "dental financial advisors" as the leading anchor, and adds "dentists" as a secondary keyword. Stays under 60 characters of visible width.

H1Medium Lift

Current: 3 Best Dental Financial Advisors We Recommend
Proposed: The Best Dental Financial Advisors for Dentists in 2026

Drops the "We Recommend" closer (sounds promotional), adds "for Dentists" as natural keyword padding, and pulls in 2026 to match the title. Keeps the H1 short enough to render on mobile in one line.

New H2 Section to AddHigh Lift

The current outline jumps from intro straight into the three firms. Google and AI engines need an "answer-first" block to confirm topical depth. Insert this H2 as the second section, before the firm breakdowns:

What Does a Dental Financial Advisor Do?
150-200 word section. Opens with the direct quotable answer ("A dental financial advisor helps dentists coordinate practice income, tax planning, retirement, and personal wealth as a single financial picture, instead of bouncing between a CPA, an advisor, and a 401(k) provider.") then lists the four core service areas a real dental financial advisor handles. Uses the exact phrase "dental financial advisor" twice and "dentist advisors" once.
How To Choose a Dental Financial Advisor
Already exists as "How To Choose Your Dental Financial Advisors" - rename to the singular, question-form variant. Headings phrased as direct questions get pulled into AI Overviews more reliably than possessive phrasings.

Content Depth AdditionsMedium Lift

The page never uses the exact phrase "dental practice value calculator" - the tracked, ranking keyword - even once. Every reference says "valuation calculator." Adding "value calculator" three to four times in title, H1, intro, and at least one H2 should be enough to push past Henry Schein and Practice Concierge.

New H2 Section to AddHigh Lift

The page currently has only 3 H2s and ~475 words. That's too thin to defend a head-term ranking against deeper Henry Schein and Practice Concierge content. Add this H2 between "How the Calculator Works" and "What Could Your Practice Achieve":

What Is a Dental Practice Worth? (2026 Benchmarks)
200-300 word section. Opens with: "Most general dental practices in the U.S. sell for 65-85% of annual collections, with high-margin specialty practices (ortho, endo, pedo) clearing 100-150%." Then breakdown by practice type, region note, and the role of EBITDA multiples for larger practices. Pulls in the exact "dental practice value calculator" anchor at least once. Sources: ADA Health Policy Institute, Henry Schein practice transitions data.
How Accurate Is a Free Dental Practice Value Calculator?
100-150 word section. Direct quotable opener: "A free dental practice value calculator gives a ballpark estimate within 10-15% of a formal valuation - close enough for planning, not close enough for a sale or a buyout." Frames the tool honestly, sets up the CFO services upsell naturally.
