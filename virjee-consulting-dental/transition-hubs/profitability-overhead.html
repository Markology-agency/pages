Busy but not profitable?

We had a client with about a million in collections, a full schedule and solid collections who was taking home far less than you'd expect. Nobody had ever asked her what success looked like.

Book a call with the Virjee Consulting team

Start with what you want to take home

Work backwards. What do you want to deposit in your personal account after taxes? What margin does that take? What collections does that margin need, and how many days a week do you want to work to get there? It sounds simple, and almost nobody does it.

Overhead is a control panel

Staffing, marketing, lab, supplies and rent are separate knobs, and each one shows up differently on your P&L. We look at each against practices at your stage, so a startup isn't judged like a 20-year practice.

Talk it through with us

Every practice and every deal is different. If you want us to look at yours, you can book a call with the Virjee Consulting team and we'll start with your numbers.
