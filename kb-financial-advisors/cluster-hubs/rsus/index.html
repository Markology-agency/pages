I Have RSUs Vesting

RSUs are simple to read on a pay stub and brutal to plan around. Default federal withholding is 22%, your marginal rate is 32-37%, and the gap shows up at tax time as a five-figure surprise. This hub covers the RSU planning playbook: cost basis, withholding gaps, double-trigger taxes, concentration risk, sale timing, and the 10b5-1 trading plan that lets you actually sell without insider concerns.
