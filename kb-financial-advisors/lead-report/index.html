How this is measured

Channel — taken from the hidden attribution fields the form captures at submit. A gclid means a paid click even when a redirect stripped the UTMs; otherwise utm_source wins, then the referrer. Nothing here is self-reported.
Category — keyword-matched from what the person typed, so an ambiguously worded submission may need reclassifying by eye.
