We don't form our instincts, fears, and loyalties in a vacuum. We inherit them from the systems we belong to, especially our families of origin. Out of deep, unconscious love, we often carry the unresolved grief, unspoken loyalties, and protective defenses of generations before us.

Constellation work doesn't analyze these patterns on a whiteboard. It brings them into the room so you can see them, feel them, and gently set down what was never yours to carry.

what actually happens in a workshop

What to Expect: Demystifying the Circle

If you’ve never experienced a constellation, it can sound abstract. In practice, it is a quiet, grounded, and deeply respectful process.

01
Naming the Inquiry

A client (the "Seeker") sits with the facilitator and names a specific life dilemma, a relational block, an organizational hurdle, or an internal freeze.

02
Mapping the Field

Instead of just talking about the people or elements involved (a parent, a partner, an ambition, an obstacle), other participants in the room are invited to stand in as "Representatives" in physical space.

03
The Unspoken Comes to Light

Without role-playing or acting, the physical arrangement in the room immediately reveals the hidden emotional landscape: who is looking away, where the distance lives, and what has been excluded.

04
Resolution and Ease

Through simple acknowledgments and respectful repositioning, the system finds its natural balance. The tension in the room softens, and the seeker experiences a tangible, internal shift: returning to their rightful place with fresh perspective and deep relief.

Ways to participate

Attend as a Seeker
Working Your Question

You bring a personal, creative, or relational question to the center of the circle for a full constellation. You observe the field unfold and step into the resolution. (Limited spots per gathering).

Attend as a Representative or Witness

You participate in the circle, with the option to stand in as a representative for others or simply witness the field. Most people find that standing in someone else’s constellation provides astonishing clarity on their own life patterns.

1:1 Private Constellations

A focused, private session using tabletop markers, floor anchors, and somatic tracking. Perfect if you prefer a 1:1 container or are exploring a sensitive life or leadership crossroads.

Organizational & Team Constellations

Tailored facilitation for founders, creative partnerships, and leadership teams going through role ambiguity, mission drift, or founder transitions.
