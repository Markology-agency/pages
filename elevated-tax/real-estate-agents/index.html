Frequently Asked

Common questions from Montana real estate agents.

Do I really need a CPA if I'm just a single agent with a brokerage?

If you cleared more than $30,000 in commissions last year, almost certainly yes. Self-employment tax alone is 15.3%, on top of federal and Montana state income tax. A few well-tracked deductions and the right structure (often an S-corp election once your net profit is consistently above $50,000-$60,000) can save you several times what a CPA costs.

When should a real estate agent consider an S-corp election?

The general benchmark: when your net profit (after business expenses) is consistently above $50,000-$60,000 a year. At that point, the savings from electing S-corp status usually outweigh the extra payroll and filing costs. Run your numbers in our S-corp calculator, then book a call to confirm whether it makes sense for your situation.

Do you handle quarterly estimated taxes?

Yes. As part of every year-round engagement, Andrew calculates your quarterly estimates and reminds you when they're due so you avoid IRS underpayment penalties and the cash crunch of a big April surprise. The cash flow forecaster above is the first step in seeing those payments coming.

What deductions do real estate agents commonly miss?

The big ones we see agents leave on the table: mileage (often tens of thousands a year), home office, MLS dues and broker fees, lead-gen subscriptions, CRM software, professional photography, staging costs, and the business-use portion of cell phone and internet. Andrew goes line by line on the first engagement to build a deduction baseline you can carry forward each year.

What do you charge?

Pricing depends on what your business looks like (single agent vs. team, S-corp vs. sole proprietorship, bookkeeping needs), but every engagement is flat-rate and communicated up front. Book a 20-minute call and we'll quote you on the spot.

I'm not in Missoula. Can you still work with me?

Yes. We work with Montana real estate agents across the state. Everything is handled by phone, video, and secure document sharing. The Montana-specific tax rules apply statewide, so your zip code doesn't change the work.
