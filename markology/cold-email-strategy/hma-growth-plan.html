Track 1: Consumers (people looking for an accountant)

Goal: rank the way Thumbtack ranks for "accountant near me" type searches, and in the meantime buy the clicks in the metros where a Vetted firm already shows.

SEO

  • Now Get the core pages indexed: homepage, 21 industry hubs, top 25 city pages, via Search Console and the Indexing API. Nothing is indexed yet; the domain is one week old in Google's eyes.
  • Now Blog, one post a week, extremely conversational, each one ending on a Browse or industry page. Five approved topics ranked by search demand:
    1. Bookkeeper vs. Accountant vs. CPA (published Sept 16)
    2. CPA vs. EA: which one should do your taxes
    3. How to find a good accountant (the featured post)
    4. Questions to ask an accountant before you hire one
    5. Do I need an accountant for my small business, and when
    Every post carries title tag, meta description, featured image with alt text, FAQ block with schema, Article schema, and links to the directory.
  • Next Backlinks from the vetted firms: a "Vetted on HireMyAccountant" badge on each firm's site linking back, plus a link from markology.io. Twenty real accounting sites linking to a new domain is the fastest authority we can get.
  • Next Thin-page control: city pages with fewer than three firms stay out of the index until they have content, so they don't drag the rest down.
  • Later Organization and ItemList schema on the hubs and an llms.txt so AI engines can cite the directory.

Track 2: Accountants (firms who pay to be Vetted)

Goal: firm owners see HireMyAccountant everywhere in the same week: an email naming their position, and the brand in their feed. The offer is the badge, the full profile and the top of their city's results. The video interview is an optional $299 add-on and is never described as the vetting.

What Vetted means now

A firm is Vetted after it pays, confirms its services and specialties with our editors, and has its public record and client feedback reviewed. If it does not pass, full refund. We do not claim to verify licenses (Terms, Will's wording); the email says "if you don't pass vetting", not "credential check", until Will approves a license check.

Cold email (Instantly)

Modeled on a TrustAnalytica email a client received, which works because it states three facts the reader can check: their position, the firm above them, and the reason for the gap. What we do not copy is "placement is determined by your profile level". Our ranking is scored on specialty, vetted status and location, and paying never buys the badge.

Subject: you're #14 of 218 in Fort Worth

Hi {{first_name}},

Your firm is currently #{{position}} of {{firm_count}} on the {{city}} results page on HireMyAccountant. Here's the page you're on: {{city_url}}

{{top_firm}} holds the #1 spot. It's the only Vetted firm on the page, so it has a full profile: the Vetted badge, services and specialties, reviews, and a direct link to book. Your row shows your name, your credential and a link to your site.

Ranking is scored on what a firm actually does, whether it's local, and whether it's been vetted. Vetted is the one you can change this month. It's $999 a year, about $83 a month, with a full refund if you don't pass vetting.

Reply and I'll get your profile started.
