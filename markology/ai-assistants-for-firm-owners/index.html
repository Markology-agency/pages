Questions firm owners ask us

Straight answers about how custom AI assistants work.

What is an AI assistant for a firm owner?

An AI assistant is software built around your firm that takes on recurring work such as intake, routine client questions, follow-ups and drafting emails. Ours are custom built, so each one works from your processes and writes in your firm's tone of voice.

How is this different from using ChatGPT myself?

A general tool starts from a blank page every time you open it. Your assistant is set up ahead of time with your processes, your wording and your way of handling common situations, so you do not have to re-explain your firm each time you use it.

Will it sound like my firm?

Yes. We set the tone from examples of how your firm writes, and you review what it drafts until you are happy with how it sounds.

Does it replace my staff?

It takes repetitive work off your team's plate so they can spend more of their time on the client work that needs a person.

Is it really cheaper than hiring someone?

Yes. An assistant costs much less than a salary, benefits and the management time an internal hire needs. It is also easier to train, because we set it up around your firm for you. We will give you a clear price on the call.

What about client confidentiality?

We settle this before anything is built. On the call we agree on what information the assistant can see and what stays out of its reach.
