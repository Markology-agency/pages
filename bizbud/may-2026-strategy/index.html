Google Ads

THE NICHE CONVERTS.
THE MEASUREMENT DOES NOT.

Both Search campaigns are producing real conversions in a niche where most accounting firms struggle to get any. The bigger story is what's hiding in the conversion setup: two of the three lead form conversion actions are silently excluded from the conversion metric, which means the bidding algorithm is optimizing against the wrong signal. Fixing that alone should improve cost-per-real-conversion within 14 to 30 days.

Monthly Conversions

Primary (book appointment) vs. all (includes lead forms).
The hidden issue: Three conversion actions live in the account, but only "Book appointment (page load on /thank-you/)" is included in the conversion metric the bidding algorithm uses. The two lead form actions are marked excluded from conversions. That means every lead form submission counts as a "secondary" conversion (visible in all_conversions, ignored by bidding). The April drop in all_conversions (15 to 9) suggests the lead form pixel may not even be firing reliably. Fix this first, then re-evaluate CPLs.

May / June Game Plan

WHAT WE WORK
NEXT.

Six items, ordered by impact. Items 1 to 3 ship this week and unlock everything downstream. Items 4 to 6 are the 30-day moves that compound on top.

01

Fix Google Ads conversion tracking on lead forms

Two of the three conversion actions are flagged "exclude from conversion metric," so the Maximize Conversions bidding strategy never sees lead form submissions as real conversions. Toggle both lead form actions to "include in conversions" and verify the thank-you page is firing the pixel reliably (April's all-conversion drop suggests it may not be). Once fixed, the bidding algorithm gets clean signal and CPL should drop meaningfully within two weeks.

This Week
02

Rewrite the Tennessee Franchise Tax blog title and meta

The single highest-leverage SEO move on the site. The blog ranks for 35 keywords and pulls 4,000+ monthly impressions, but the click-through rate is 0.39% (a strong blog at that position should be at 2-3%). Sharper title (something like "Tennessee Franchise & Excise Tax: 2026 Rules for LLCs and S-Corps") and meta description capture 100+ additional clicks per month with zero new content.

This Week
03

Pause the underperforming influencer search terms

"Influencer accountant," "content creator cpa," "content creator tax accountant," and "accountants for content creators" have spent $273 over 90 days with zero conversions. Add them as negatives or pause the matching keywords until the lead form tracking is fixed. Recovers roughly $90 per month of pure waste while preserving the converting terms ("bookkeeping for content creators," "international tax accountant").
