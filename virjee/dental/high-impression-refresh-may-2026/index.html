02 — Valuation calculator visible FAQ block (Task 2)

Edit in Elementor: https://dentalcpausa.com/wp-admin/post.php?post=694&action=elementor
Add an FAQ Elementor widget (or HTML widget with question/answer markup) directly below the calculator tool. Once a visible FAQ block exists, the Rank Math FAQPage schema (already saved) will automatically render the wrapper. Use these 5 Q&As exactly:
Q1. How is a dental practice valued?
Most dental practices are valued between 60% and 85% of trailing 12-month collections, or 2.5x-3.5x adjusted EBITDA, whichever produces the higher figure for the seller.
Q2. What multiple do DSOs pay for dental practices?
DSOs typically pay 5x-7x adjusted EBITDA for dental practices with $1.5M+ in collections and clean financials, with a portion paid as illiquid DSO equity.
Q3. How is goodwill allocated in a dental practice sale?
Goodwill typically accounts for 70-85% of a dental practice's purchase price, with the remainder allocated to equipment, supplies, and patient records via IRS Form 8594.
Q4. What's the difference between an S-corp election and asset sale for a selling dentist?
An S-corp asset sale gives the seller long-term capital gains treatment on goodwill (currently 20% federal max) versus ordinary income rates on equipment recapture.
Q5. How long does a dental practice valuation take?
A defensible dental practice valuation takes 2-4 weeks with three years of cash-basis P&Ls, tax returns, and a current production report by provider.
Also add a short "How the dental practice valuation calculator works" H2 paragraph immediately under the tool, explaining the EBITDA-to-multiple methodology and the typical 60-85% of collections range. One paragraph is enough.
