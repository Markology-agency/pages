What the report covers

Every engagement includes the same full scope. There are no tiers to pick from and no hourly billing.

Proof of cash

We tie the seller's reported cash, revenue and expenses to their bank statements.

Tax return reconciliation

We match the books to the filed returns and explain the differences.

Add-backs and adjustments

We document owner pay, one-time items and related-party costs, with the support behind each one.

Trends and margins

We look at several years of results to separate seasonality and one-time swings from the real run rate.

Customer concentration

We look at how much revenue depends on a few customers and how likely it is to stay after the sale.

Working capital

We give you a working capital target to use in the purchase agreement.

CIM comparison

If the broker marketed the business with a CIM, we compare its earnings to ours and explain the gaps.

Projections

We build a monthly model you and your lender can use to test different scenarios.

Risks and opportunities

We write down what could help or hurt the earnings after you own the business.

Management Q&A

We record every question we asked the seller and every answer we received.
