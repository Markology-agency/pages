How the links should work

In the article

Instead of
If your payroll runs through a separate company, your accountant may not see it until year-end. To learn more, click here.
Write
If your payroll runs through a separate company, your accountant may not see it until year-end. Our Indianapolis payroll services are part of your monthly accounting, and we explain how TMA’s payroll service works here.

Sample wording. The words you link should tell people where the link goes.

What to do

  1. Add 1 or 2 service, industry or location links to every post, starting with the top 20.
  2. Add 2 or 3 related posts to every service and industry page.
  3. Every time you publish a post, go back to 2 older posts and link to the new one.

Make the Pages Load Faster

People leave a slow page before they read it, and most of your ad visitors are on phones. When we tested the dental checklist page for your ads report, it took 8.9 seconds on a phone.

On HubSpot (your site)

HubSpot already handles caching and the delivery network. What slows a page down is usually what gets added to it: large images, video, pop-ups and tracking scripts.

On WordPress

We use NitroPack, which handles caching, images and code for you. It’s the first thing we’d add if you ever move the site to WordPress.

What to remove first

1Images over about 200 KB
2Videos that load before someone clicks
3Extra pop-ups and widgets
4Old tracking scripts in Tag Manager

What to do

  1. Run the homepage, checklist page and playbook page through pagespeed.web.dev on mobile.
  2. Aim for the main content to load in 2.5 seconds or less.
  3. Fix images first, then video, then scripts.
  4. Test again after each change.

If you pay a company

An agency usually does this for you, and you can also pay a company like The HOTH directly. Ask to approve each site before it goes live, choose sites with real visitors in your field, and go slowly. Google can ignore or penalize links that look bought, so skip cheap packages with hundreds of links.

What to do

  1. Claim the free directories first, using the same name, address and phone number on each.
  2. Ask 5 partners or associations for a link this quarter.
  3. Point links at your homepage, service and industry pages, plus your best tools and posts.
  4. Vary the linked words. Mostly use your name, and the keyword only some of the time.
