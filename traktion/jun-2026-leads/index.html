The leak is holds, not demand. Of 24 booked calls, 10 cancelled or no-showed — a 42% fall-off before the conversation even happens. Demand into the top of the funnel is healthy; the fastest win is getting more booked calls to actually hold (reminder sequence, tighter scheduling window, a pre-call value email).

Net New Leads, Trailing 12 Months

June's 21 net-new leads sit right in the healthy band Traktion has held all year, and well above where the funnel was a year ago (Jun '25: 14).

Meetings Held vs. Signed

Meetings stayed steady through Q2 but signings paused from March to May. June's two signings restart the conversion line.

Year Over Year — June

Compared with June 2025, the funnel is materially stronger at every stage that matters: net-new leads up 50% (14 → 21), signings doubled (1 → 2), and the meeting-to-signed rate moved from 8% to 18%. Meetings held dipped by one (12 → 11), driven entirely by the higher cancel/no-show count this June.
