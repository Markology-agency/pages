Before You Book

What do you need from me to get started?+

Your tax documents and a copy of last year's return. I look it over for anything unusual that carries into this year. You'll get a link to upload everything, so please don't email your W-2.

Can you take over mid-year?+

Yes. We set the engagement to start when it makes sense for you, catch up the books if they need it, and build the road map for the rest of the year. If last year's return is still open, we can handle that too.

Do you work with clients outside Texas?+

Yes. I have clients all over the country. Even a lot of my Dallas clients meet with me over video and I rarely see them in person.

What if I already have a preparer I like?+

That can work. Sometimes the right setup is that your preparer keeps the return, and we handle the monthly books and the planning. We'll talk through what makes sense on the call.
