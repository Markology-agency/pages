What's Working

  1. Insurance accounting software cluster is performing well. The QuickBooks-vs-Xero blog holds 4 top-10 positions simultaneously across "accounting software for insurance agencies/brokers/agency", good topical authority building.
  1. Loan officer commission content is dominant. The commission calculator and the calculation blog together hold 8+ keyword positions, including #2 for "loan officer commission calculator." This is BFB's clearest traffic driver.
  1. Keyword count grew 47% over 6 months (51 to 76). New content is entering the index and building organic footprint, even if traffic hasn't scaled proportionally yet. This is normal for a site at this domain authority level and this early in a content program.

---

What to Watch

  1. "Broker accounting" (880 searches/mo) is ranking position 48 on the homepage, this is far too broad a keyword for a homepage to rank for competitively. A dedicated pillar page or hub content for "brokerage accounting" could capture meaningful traffic. Priority action.
  1. "Accounting software for insurance" (320 searches/mo) is at position 11, one page-1 push away. The QuickBooks-vs-Xero page already exists; update the meta title and add a strong H1 targeting this exact phrase to move it to page 1.
  1. Domain authority is low (ascore 16). The content program is building the keyword footprint, but link acquisition is the lever that will move the dial on traffic. The monthly backlinks work should prioritize relevant industry publications (insurance trade press, real estate association blogs, mortgage industry sites) over generic directories.
  1. "Bookkeeping for real estate brokers" (140 searches/mo) is at position 58, well below where it should be given BFB's clear service offering. The new real estate trust account blog and quarterly tax blog in the August content calendar are the right calls to improve this cluster.

---

Recommended Actions for September

  1. Refresh /blogs/quickbooks-vs-xero-for-insurance-agencies, retitle meta to target "accounting software for insurance" (320/mo, pos 11) and add an H2 section directly answering that query. Quick-win to page 1.
  1. Create or refresh a hub page for "brokerage accounting" to capture the 880/mo keyword at a higher position than the homepage currently holds.
  1. Continue the August blog calendar (trust account, quarterly taxes, QuickBooks setup, 1099 reporting), these directly support the real estate and insurance keyword clusters that are showing growth.
  1. Submit new blogs to GSC for indexation immediately upon publishing.
  1. Prioritize backlink outreach to insurance and real estate trade publications to raise the domain ascore above 20, which is the threshold where ranking velocity starts to improve meaningfully.
