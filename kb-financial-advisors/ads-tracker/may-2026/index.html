Decision For Tomorrow

Pause SF Search, refocus it, or hold the line.

Three options for Landon and Jackie. Recommendation: Option B — refocus the campaign around the audience the rest of KB's strategy already targets. The keyword set is the problem, not the budget.

Option A · Pause

Kill SF Search, reallocate $6/day.

Move the $180/month into Databricks Pre-IPO (which also produces 0 booking_complete but at least matches the focus) or hold it back to fund a /founders-tax-planning/ landing page build first.

Pro: Stops the bleed immediately. Con: Gives up a foothold in SF metro-area search where KB has physical presence.

Option B · Refocus

Rebuild SF Search around the focus.

New ad groups: "san francisco qsbs cpa", "san francisco ipo tax advisor", "san francisco founder financial planning", "san francisco tech executive cfp". New copy that leads with founder / IPO / QSBS language. Point at a new /founders-tax-planning/ LP (build needed).

Pro: Keeps the geo signal, adds the focus filter. Con: Requires a 2-3 week rebuild + new LP. Volume will be lower.

Option C · Hold

Keep running with the 26 negatives.

Trust the negatives to filter the bad traffic over the next 30 days. Recheck in late June.

Pro: No engineering work. Con: Another month of $165+ spend with high probability of 0–1 bookings. The structural keyword-set issue is not solved by negatives alone.
