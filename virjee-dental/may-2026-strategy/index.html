New Plays

FOUR NEW
DIRECTIONS.

Outside the standard SEO and content rhythm. Four bigger swings worth piloting, each one strictly dental-focused per brand rules.

Idea 01

Dental practice acquisition diligence checklist (lead magnet)

The Practice Buyer persona is the highest-value audience landing on the cost-to-build and starting-a-practice articles. Build a downloadable PDF: 'Dental Practice Acquisition Tax Diligence Checklist' covering goodwill allocation, entity structure, financing tax treatment, working capital adjustments, and post-close planning.

Captures emails from in-market dental practice buyers at the most expensive transaction of their career. Builds owned list of high-LTV prospects.

Idea 02

DSO partnership tax decoder

The DSO-Adjacent Dentist persona has limited dental-specific resources to evaluate DSO offers. Build a recurring content series (one piece per month): 'How to evaluate a DSO offer', 'DSO equity rollover tax mechanics', 'DSO earn-out structure tax implications', 'When DSO partnership beats independent ownership'.

Limited competition, high commercial intent, exact match for the highest-value persona in the index.

Idea 03

Quarterly dental practice owner tax newsletter

One quality email per quarter to the owned list: federal and state dental tax law changes, year-end planning reminders timed to dental practice cash cycles, IRS updates affecting dental practice deductions. Plain-spoken, no general business content.

Per Omar's brand discipline (every piece must be dental-specific or rewrite). Builds recurring touchpoint without overwhelming the practice owner audience.

Idea 04

Dental industry podcast guest tour

Pitch Omar onto dental practice owner podcasts. Short list: Shared Practices (Richard Low), The Dentalpreneur (Mark Costes), Dental Practice Heroes, ACT Dental, Dentistry Uncensored. Two to three appearances per quarter target.

Direct access to the exact target audience plus authoritative dental industry backlinks. Each appearance can drive a dedicated landing page on the site for that podcast's audience with a relevant lead magnet.
