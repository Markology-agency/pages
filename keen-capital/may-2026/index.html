The Chapin Refresh Story

A national site that ranked for nothing became a local SC HNW practice that ranks for 69 things.

The site spent most of 2025 ranking for 7 to 25 keywords on the SEMrush US database. Pages were written for a generic national HNW audience. Ads ran on broad terms like "investment companies." Sessions were flat at around 400 a month, and the few conversions the ad account reported were button-click events on the homepage CTA, not real form submissions.

The May 2026 refresh changed three things at once. First, the homepage and Carolinas Wealth Advisors page were rewritten around Chapin, Lake Murray, Columbia, and the Midlands — locking in the geographic frame that matches the firm's actual book of business. Second, a new $7/day Search campaign launched on a 30-mile proximity radius around 29036, with city-anchored keywords and 42 negative terms keeping it inside South Carolina. Third, conversion tracking was rebuilt: the Let's Chat button-click action was demoted so it no longer drives Smart Bidding, and the AW gtag was installed directly on the /thank-you/ confirmation page so a tracked conversion now equals a real form submission.

The combined effect is what the keyword count chart shows. The site moved from invisible to indexed across 69 terms, with the Safe Withdrawal Rates 2026 post anchoring a retirement-income cluster and the rewritten service pages picking up Chapin and Midlands queries. June is the first month of clean ad data on top of all of it.

June Game Plan

What we ship next.

Three priorities built on the May momentum. Each one is queued in Asana with a finished decision attached.

01

Run the E-E-A-T sprint on the homepage, the About page, and the top blog posts.

The site currently has no Schema.org structured data. For an SEC-regulated RIA, that is the easiest credibility upgrade Google has on offer. The sprint adds Organization schema on the homepage with a knowsAbout array (HNW investing, safe withdrawal rate, retirement income planning, fee-only fiduciary), Person schemas for the four advisors with credentials (CFP, CFA, CPA, EA), Service schema on the four service pages, and FAQPage schema with visible Common Questions blocks on the ten most recent blog posts.

The sprint is the same playbook we ran on KB Financial Advisors and Affinity Accounting in May. On KB, FAQ schema lifted the equity-comp posts into a sustained AI-citation pattern; same compounding effect is the expected outcome here for retirement-income queries.

02

Expand the Safe Withdrawal Rate cluster from 1 article to 4.

The SWR 2026 post is currently ranking for 14 distinct keywords across positions 28 to 89. That is a hub asset earning traffic on its own. Around it we are publishing three sibling posts: sequence-of-returns risk, the 4% rule revisited for 2026 conditions, and dynamic withdrawal strategies for HNW retirees. Each post links into the hub and into the Retirement Income Calculator, which is the conversion path for retirees searching this topic.

Topical authority compounds when a site has a hub plus three to five siblings, not when it has a single deep page. The cluster build moves the site from "one viral retirement post" to "the SC firm that owns retirement income content."

03

Launch the fee-only financial advisor city pages for Chapin, Columbia, and Lake Murray.

The Carolinas Wealth Advisors page is the broad SC LP. Beneath it, three dedicated city pages capture the "fee-only financial advisor Chapin SC" and "fiduciary advisor Lake Murray SC" intent the new Search campaign is bidding on. Each page mirrors the Chapin refresh voice, links into the retirement income calculator, and carries LocalBusiness schema with the Chapin HQ NAP.

City pages serve two compounding purposes: they give the ad campaign a more specific landing page per ad group (lifting Quality Score), and they give organic search a topical hook for branded-plus-city queries that the homepage alone is too broad to capture.
