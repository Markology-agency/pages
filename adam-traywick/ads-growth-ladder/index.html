WHERE YOU STAND.

Your campaign already shows across all of Texas. It runs at $6 a day, and that budget runs out early most days. In September, 42% of the searches you were eligible for went unanswered because the money was gone. That's the cheapest growth available to you, since those are the same searches that are already producing leads at $12 to $25 each.

Another 54% of searches are lost on ad rank. Winning those means bidding higher, so each lead after that point costs more. That's where the returns start to shrink.

Where we'd stop

Steps 1 and 3 are where the value is. Going to $30 a day should roughly double your ad leads at about $30 each. Going to $60 a day adds around a dozen more at about $70 each. Past $60 a day, every extra lead costs over $130, so we'd only go there if your close rate on the leads from steps 1 to 3 says it pays.

We'd run step 2 alongside step 1 as a small test, since the trades are where we're taking your positioning. We'd skip step 6 unless you want clients you'll never meet in person and you're ready to compete on price with national bookkeeping companies.

THE FOUR MARKETS.

1. Texas, general accounting

Searches like "cpa near me", "accountant fort worth" and "bookkeeping services". About 22,500 searches a month in Dallas-Fort Worth and about 70,000 across Texas.

Best value Clicks cost $2 to $5 today and $5 to $25 at full coverage.

Search volume isn't the limit here. Budget runs out long before the searches do. Most of the volume is DFW, so going statewide adds reach without a big jump in leads.

2. Trades, Texas only

"Accountant for contractors", "construction cpa", "hvac bookkeeping". About 300 searches a month across all of Texas.

Too small alone Clicks cost $6 to $45.

On its own this would bring 1 to 3 leads a month for around $400. It works as part of a combined trades campaign. It can't carry a campaign by itself.

3. Trades, nationwide

The same searches across the US. About 3,000 a month with real buying intent, most of it contractor and construction terms. Plumber, HVAC and electrician terms are small.

Good clients, few of them Clicks cost $15 to $60.

This tops out around $1,500 to $2,000 a month. After that there aren't enough searches left to buy. We sized the organic side of this in the national trades report.

4. Small business bookkeeping, nationwide

"Outsourced bookkeeping", "small business accounting services", "virtual cpa". About 26,000 searches a month.

Expensive Clicks cost $15 to $90.

There's plenty of volume, but you'd be bidding against Bench, Pilot and 1-800Accountant for price shoppers. Expect $400 to $600 per lead.

Why we didn't size Canada or North America

A Canadian business needs a Canadian accountant to file its returns, so those leads wouldn't turn into clients for you. The US is as far as the map goes.

WHAT THESE NUMBERS ASSUME.

  • Today's numbers come from your own account. Over June to September, 12% of ad clicks became a lead (September was 19%). We assume the rate drops to 8% to 10% as we reach for searches you don't win today.
  • Market sizes and click costs come from Google's Keyword Planner, pulled through your account on 6 October 2026. Search counts are monthly averages over the last 12 months.
  • Leads aren't clients. Some ad leads are people who want a personal tax return done. That share grows as spend grows, especially from January to April, when tax-prep searches and click costs spike. Telling us which September leads turned into clients is the most useful thing you can do before we scale.
  • These are planning estimates and not a guarantee. Google sets click prices in a live auction, so we'd move up one step at a time and check the real cost per lead after two to three weeks before taking the next step.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT.

  • Week 1: Texas budget goes from $6 to $30 a day. The trades campaign goes live at $20 a day with its own ads and the HVAC, plumbing and electrician pages as landing pages.
  • Weeks 2 and 3: We review search terms twice a week and block personal tax-prep searches so the extra budget goes to business owners.
  • Week 4: If the cost per lead holds under about $40 and the leads are business owners, Texas moves to $60 a day.
