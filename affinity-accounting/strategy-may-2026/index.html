Diagnostic

What's broken under the hood.

Four findings from the May 2026 audit. Two are conversion-tracking + ad-copy fixes (high impact, fast to deploy). Two are E-E-A-T gaps that need WordPress Application Password access before Markology can deploy.

1. Google Ads conversion attribution is mis-configured

High — explains 0 conversions

What's happening: 11 conversion actions exist in the account. The CTA Affinity actually optimizes for, Book a Call (Button), is not marked primaryForGoal. Neither is Contact form Submission. The actions Google currently uses for bid optimization are Local actions - Website visits and Local actions - Other engagements — both inflate counts and don't represent leads.

Why it matters: Google's Maximize Conversions algorithm is bidding for the wrong signal. Real leads (booked calls, form fills, phone clicks) aren't counted, so spend optimizes around website-visits noise.

Fix (queued for Whitney auth): Demote Local actions - Website visits and Local actions - Other engagements from primary. Promote Book a Call (Button) and Contact form Submission to primary.

2. Active campaign is bleeding budget to consumer tax-prep

High — 25%+ of spend is off-ICP

Evidence (search terms, last 30d): 45 impressions on tax preparers near me, 47 on cpa milwaukee, queries for hr block, jackson hewitt, tax preparer, where's my refund. These are individual W-2 filers, not $1M–$15M owner-led businesses.

Fix (queued for Whitney auth): Add 25 negative keywords at the campaign level — consumer tax-prep brands (TurboTax, H&R Block, Jackson Hewitt, Liberty Tax, TaxAct), DIY intent (free file, tax software, where's my refund), job-search intent (jobs, career, internship), and tax-debt intent (tax resolution, back taxes, tax lien).

3. Ad copy doesn't reflect the new $1M–$15M owner-led ICP

Medium — opportunity cost

Current state (8 enabled RSAs): Generic headlines: "Accounting Services Milwaukee", "5-Star Rated On Google", "Trusted Milwaukee Accountants". Zero references to the brand's central differentiator (proactive-not-reactive), the productized-advisory shift, the $1M–$15M revenue band, or Chicago. Reads as a generic Milwaukee CPA, not Affinity.

Fix (queued for Whitney auth): Add 2 new RSA variants per ad group that lead with proactive positioning, owner-led-business framing, and Chicago geo. See "Ad copy refresh" section below.

4. E-E-A-T author signal is empty across 30+ blog posts

Medium — AI discovery + Google quality

Evidence: The WordPress REST API returns 2 user accounts (affinitymke, wpvalet) both with empty description fields. Every blog post is bylined "Affinity Accounting" with no Person schema, no credential signals, no jobTitle, no knowsAbout fields. Same gap on /about/.

Fix (blocked on WP App Password): Inject Person schema for Victoria Haas + team on each post, append visible Author Bio HTML, add Organization + Service schema enrichment, draft llms.txt. Same playbook Markology completed on KB Financial Advisors May 12 — see KB scorecard.
