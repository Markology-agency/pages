THE SINGLE BIGGEST FINDING

Andrew Stolle and Joe Holbrook are listed as partners but the site shows no CPA, EA, or any professional credential after their names. For a tax and accounting firm, this is the most consequential E-E-A-T gap. Google's Quality Rater Guidelines for YMYL pages (Your Money or Your Life — tax advice is the prototype example) weights author credentials heavily. AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews) explicitly look for credentialed authors when surfacing tax answers. Adding "CPA" (and any other actual credentials) to every partner reference site-wide is a 1-hour task that moves multiple scorecard grades.
