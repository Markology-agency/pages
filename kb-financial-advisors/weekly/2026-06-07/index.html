The story this week. KB's content footprint reached 43,154 weekly impressions at average position 12.8. Same week last year was 36,024 impressions at average position 32.9. That is a 20.1-position improvement YoY (from page 4 into the top of page 2) on a 20%+ larger query footprint.

Why direct clicks dropped. Average position improved sharply, but click-through rate fell because AI Overviews are answering KB's high-value informational queries in-place. The "AI Overview Likely-Cited" table below shows top-10 queries with hundreds of impressions and near-zero CTR — those are AI Overview impressions, not failures. They drive trust and downstream brand search.

How conversion actually happens for KB. AI engines cite KB's deep equity-comp content, founders see the brand, and brand searches land on the homepage. Brand-search impressions are healthy (5.6% CTR on brand queries this week vs 0.15% on non-brand) and the SEMrush footprint sits at 1,727 ranking keywords — discovery is working as designed.
