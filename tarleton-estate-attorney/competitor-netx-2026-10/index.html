What we recommend

Test 1 · Ads

Run probate ads just for Hunt and Rockwall counties

NETX stopped Google advertising on August 25, and no Greenville estate firm is advertising right now. Today the five-county budget gets used up in Dallas County before it reaches Hunt. We'd build a separate small campaign for Hunt and Rockwall counties. It would cover probate and muniment of title searches and send people straight to the consultation form.

  • Measure: form leads from Hunt and Rockwall over 60 days.
  • Decision for Allan: the budget. The account is set up around a $200 monthly cap today.
Test 2 · Offer

Publish starting prices for flat-fee work

Allan already offers flat fees on some estate planning work. Putting real "starting at" prices on the fees page and the wills page would answer the first question people compare firms on. It would also take away NETX's main selling point.

  • Measure: consultation requests from the wills and fees pages, 60 days before and after.
  • Decision for Allan: which services and prices. Any published fee has to meet Texas Bar advertising rules, so we'd check the wording with him.
Test 3 · Content

Add a muniment of title page

About 720 people a month search "muniment of title texas" and 1,600 search "muniment of title". NETX has a page with a flat price and Tarleton has none. It's a quicker, cheaper alternative to full probate when there's a will and no debts. That fits the probate work that already brings Allan leads.

  • Measure: rankings and form leads from the page after 90 days.
Fix now

Reply to reviews and clean up old listings

  • None of Allan's 47 Google reviews has a reply, including a 1-star on the Dallas listing from September 19. NETX replies to half of theirs. We'd draft the replies for Allan to approve.
  • Yelp, BBB, Lawful and Birdeye still list "Tarleton + Boswell." We'd update them to Tarleton Law Firm PLLC so Google sees one consistent name.
  • Allan's bio says "30+ years" and the homepage says "more than 16 years." We need the right figure from Allan.
Test 4 · Bio

Make Allan's bio do more of the work

NETX's About page tells Chris's personal story and names his town. Allan's bio lists strong credentials but doesn't say why he does this work or mention Terrell. We'd add a short section in Allan's own words, taken from a quick call and never written for him. We'd also link his board certification and State Bar profile and add Terrell to the page title.

  • Measure: clicks from the bio to the consultation form.
Allan's call

Showing up in the Greenville map results

The Greenville map box goes to firms with an office in Greenville. Tarleton can only appear there with a real, staffed office in Hunt County. A mailbox or virtual office breaks Google's rules and can get the listing suspended. If Hunt County is a market Allan wants long term, that's the conversation to have. Until then, Tests 1 to 3 reach Hunt County without an office.

Questions for Allan
