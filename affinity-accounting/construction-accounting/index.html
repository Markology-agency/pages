Questions and answers

Before you book

Do I have to change how I bid or track jobs?

No. We start with how you work today, set up job costing around your existing jobs and estimates, and adjust only what gets in the way of seeing each job clearly.

What does job costing look like each month?

Every open job, with what you bid, what you have spent and what is left, compared with how far along the work is. We go through it together in your monthly meeting.

Which level fits a construction company?

Most fit Growth. It includes the monthly CFO partnership, the 13-week cash flow forecast and client and project profitability, which is where job costing sits.

What about taxes?

Business and personal tax planning and preparation are available alongside any level, so your tax work and your monthly numbers come from the same team.
