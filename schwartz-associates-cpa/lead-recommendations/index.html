How we get you more local enquiries

These are our recommendations after watching how visitors used the new website over the last four weeks. Each one says what we'd do, why, and what we need from you.

Most visitors leave before they ever see a form. The recommendations below put a way to reach you in front of them sooner, and bring in more of the right local people.

What the visits show

24 of 87US visitors opened the Pricing page. It is your second most viewed page.

These numbers come from recordings of visits to your website between October 16 and November 12, 2026.

Our recommendations

1

Take Pricing out of the menu

The page stays live, so you can still send the link to anyone who asks. It just stops being one of the first things a new visitor clicks.

Why: people work out a price on their own and leave before they've spoken to you. Nobody who used the calculator went on to get in touch.

We need from you: a yes to take it out of the menu.

2

Show Rich's calendar right after the form

Someone sends the form, and the next page lets them pick a time with Rich. You still get every enquiry by email and in HubSpot, whether they book or not.

Why: Rich said you're often one of five firms a person contacts. The first one to get a call on the calendar usually wins.

We need from you: Rich's Calendly link, and a yes to people booking directly.

3

Send an instant reply with the calendar link

Everyone who sends a form gets a short email from you within a minute, with the link to book a call. Nikki still calls the same day.

Why: it covers evenings and weekends, when nobody is at the phone.

We need from you: a yes, and we'll send the wording for you to check.

4

Focus on three industries first

We suggest childcare centers, beauty salons and landscapers, with home service contractors next. Each one gets its own ads and Google Business Profile posts, and they point to the industry page already on your site.

Why: an owner who sees their own kind of business named is more likely to get in touch than one who sees a general accounting ad.

We need from you: confirm the three, or swap one.

5

Test ads inside ChatGPT

We run two ads side by side in South Jersey and Philadelphia ZIP codes. One offers the no-cost QuickBooks assessment and the other leads with an accountant who picks up the phone. We keep the one that brings enquiries.

Why: ChatGPT already sends you visitors without any ads, and most of them stay to read.

We need from you: nothing. We'll report which ad wins.

6

Post on your Google Business Profile every week

We start with the no-cost QuickBooks assessment, then one post for each of the three industries.

Why: your recent real enquiries found you through the profile.

We need from you: access to the profile so we can schedule the posts.

7

Record one QuickBooks session with Ling and Rich

It's a 20-minute recording on getting QuickBooks ready for tax season. People fill out a short form to watch it, as Rich asked, and it ends on the no-cost assessment. If it books calls, we add one a month by industry.

Why: people get to meet you before they call.

We need from you: 30 minutes in the week of November 30, and the link to Rich's older Q&A video.

8

Show a follow-up ad to people who looked at Pricing

Visitors who opened Pricing and left see a Google ad for the no-cost QuickBooks assessment over the next few weeks.

Why: they were interested enough to check the price, so they are worth a second try.

We need from you: nothing.
