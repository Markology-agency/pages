The Branded Search Story

Branded search demand is the strongest growth signal on the site.

The headline number on this report is the branded search trajectory in Google Search Console. Branded impressions doubled year-over-year. Branded clicks grew 28%. The number of distinct branded queries grew from 8 to 13 — meaning more search variations are surfacing the firm, not just more volume on the same queries.

The new branded queries are the most useful detail. Twelve months ago, branded search for KB was dominated by the canonical "kb financial advisors" lookup. May 2026 GSC shows people searching "kb advisors," "kb financial," "kb financial advisors llc," "kb financial services," and "landon loveall" by personal name. That shift — from a single brand-search lookup to a fan of related variants including the lead advisor's name — is the upstream demand pattern that compounds for a knowledge firm. People are encountering KB somewhere and coming back to find the firm with whatever search term feels natural to them.
