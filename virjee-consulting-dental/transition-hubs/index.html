Questions for Omar

  1. Written valuation: price, what's included, turnaround.
  2. Practice Purchase Review: free, paid, or part of the new-owner package?
  3. New Practice Owner package: contents, and can we show a price?
  4. Consulting: confirm it runs through the monthly advisory program, how it is priced, and whether it is open to non-clients.
  5. Benchmark sign-off: one range per category for the KPI Checker and scorecard.
  6. Fee data source for fee schedule analysis.
  7. 10 minutes on camera about selling (when to start, what kills a sale price, one real example) to fill the seller pages in your words.
  8. Partners to name (PPO negotiation, marketing, hygiene coaching) and PNC referral timing.
  9. Listings: we recommend no for now. Agree?
