WHY YOUR SEARCH LOOKED LIKE THAT.

  • Ten medical pages compete for the same searches. /medical-accountants/, /medical-accountant-houston/, /medical-practices/, /medical-practice-cpa/ and six more all target doctors. Google can't tell which one is the main page, so for "medical accountants texas" it showed /medical-accountants/, which has Rank Math's default title "Medical Accountants - Virjee Consulting". Google then cut that down to "Medical Accountants".
  • None of the medical titles say Houston or Texas, so Google has nothing that ties the result to a Texas search.
  • The business panel on the right was Medical Auditing Solutions LLC, an unrelated company. Google showed it because its name partly matches the search. Nothing on the site causes that panel. What's missing is AccountingService schema on the service pages and a Google Business Profile service that links back to the medical page. Both are in this plan.
  • The schema on every page except the homepage says "Article", which is the blog post type. Google reads the service pages as articles rather than services offered by a Houston firm.
  • Eight pages contain hidden "Lorem ipsum" placeholder text. It's hidden on every screen size, but it's still in the page source, so Google reads it.
