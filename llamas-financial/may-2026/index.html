Geography

NAPA, OR USA?

Per Octavio's question. Llamas Financial is Napa-anchored but serves wineries nationally. GSC traffic confirms this: there is no geographic clustering toward California or Napa in the query data. The top non-branded queries are generic ("accounting for wineries", "winery accountant", "accounting for vineyards") with no state or city modifier. Every commercial query above is a national-intent search, not a local one.

Read: Llamas should keep positioning as a national winery accountant, not a Napa local CPA. No local SEO investment needed (no Google Business Profile push, no "Napa CPA" pages). The winery niche is the moat. Geography is open. The new homepage hero copy could lean into "winery-only accounting, anywhere in the US" to convert that national intent.
