# Ashford Sky CPA

Ashford Sky is a Utah-based CPA firm providing tax, accounting, bookkeeping, and fractional CFO services to growing businesses, acquisition entrepreneurs, e-commerce sellers, and SaaS companies. The firm combines clean monthly bookkeeping, year-round tax strategy, and CFO-level guidance under one team.

## Services

- [Tax Preparation Services](https://ashfordsky.com/tax-preparation-services/): Tax preparation, planning, and strategy for Utah and multi-state businesses, including acquisition entrepreneurs.
- [Salt Lake City Bookkeeping](https://ashfordsky.com/salt-lake-city-bookkeeping/): Monthly bookkeeping with CPA-led close, clean reconciliations, and reporting tuned for owner decisions.
- [Fractional CFO Services Salt Lake City](https://ashfordsky.com/fractional-cfo-services-salt-lake-city/): Cash flow forecasting, KPI reporting, acquisition modeling, and strategic financial leadership for $1M+ Utah businesses.
- [Salt Lake City Accounting](https://ashfordsky.com/salt-lake-city-accounting/): Monthly close, financial reporting, tax planning, and CFO-level guidance under one team.
- [Accounting for Acquisition Entrepreneurs](https://ashfordsky.com/acquisition-entrepreneurs/): Diligence, quality of earnings, post-close accounting, and tax strategy for searchers and ETA graduates.
- [Buying a Business in Utah](https://ashfordsky.com/buying-a-business/): Diligence, financing model, asset allocation, and tax-optimized purchase agreements for Utah acquisitions.
- [Outsourced Tax Department for SaaS](https://ashfordsky.com/saas/): Multi-state nexus, ASC 606 revenue recognition, SaaS KPIs, and tax strategy across the growth stack.
- [Utah S-Corp Tax Strategy](https://ashfordsky.com/utah-s-corp-tax-strategy/): S-corp election analysis, reasonable compensation modeling, and S-corp tax strategy for Utah owners.
