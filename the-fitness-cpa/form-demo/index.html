How it works in HubSpot

HubSpot's own multi-step form only creates the contact on the final step, and there's no setting to change that. So the fix isn't one multi-step form, it's two ordinary forms chained together.

  1. Form 1 stays as it is on /get-in-touch/ (six fields). Its "after submit" action redirects to a new page, /get-in-touch/step-2/, passing the email in the URL.
  2. Form 2 is the existing longer form (business type, locations, revenue, employees, challenges). Its email field is prefilled from the URL and hidden.
  3. Because both forms carry the same email, HubSpot writes form 2's answers onto the contact form 1 created. One record, two submissions.
  4. Leads who stop after form 1 are still in HubSpot with name, email, business name, website and source. Today those leads would be lost if the form were built as a single multi-step form.
