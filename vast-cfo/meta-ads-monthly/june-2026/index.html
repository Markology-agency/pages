July Priority Actions

1

Keep the Webinar campaign running, scale the budget gradually

Scale what works The On-Demand Webinar at $0.93 CPC, 2.50% CTR, and $41.56 CPL is the proven unit. Worth a +50% budget bump to test whether CPL holds at higher daily spend, then +100% if it does. Stay on the same creative until performance degrades.

2

Evaluate the Awareness campaign's role honestly

Spend efficiency The Awareness campaign spent $227.10 (52% of June budget) to reach 55,902 restaurant operators with 0 attributable leads. If the goal is brand recall before later retargeting, fine — but the retargeting layer needs to exist to capture those warmed-up audiences later. If retargeting isn't set up yet, the Awareness spend is being burned without compounding.

3

Build a retargeting audience from Webinar campaign viewers + Awareness reach

Compound the spend Two custom audiences worth setting up: (1) anyone who clicked the Webinar ad but didn't register, (2) anyone in the Awareness audience who engaged with a post in the last 30 days. Run a small retargeting campaign at $5-10/day with a direct "Book a discovery call" CTA. Cheap clicks, warmer audience.
