1. Replace PMax Brand KW + Remarketing with a small exact-match Search brand campaign ('kb financial advisors', 'kb financial', 'kb advisors') at no more than the current spend, Bay Area, landing on the homepage.

Problem and data

PMax put $273 of $426 (64%) into Display for 3 all-conversions. Its October spend was $42 against a $201 budget, with 89% impression share lost to rank.

Other explanations

Display remarketing may help people who later book through a brand search, and 8 leads in a year is a small sample. Brand is not growth, so this saves money; it adds no new demand.

How it helps
