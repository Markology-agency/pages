Paste-Ready Pitch Email

To: editor@clubindustry.com · Subject: Op-ed pitch: Instructor classification (1099 vs W-2) for fitness
Hi, I'd like to write an op-ed for Club Industry on the 1099 vs W-2 question for group fitness instructors. The stakes for gym owners are high. A reclassification audit on three years of instructor pay can wipe out a small studio. Most owners default to 1099 because it is easier; the IRS rules on behavioral control, financial control, and relationship type frequently push these workers into W-2 territory whether owners realize it or not. The piece would walk through the IRS 20-factor test with three fitness-specific scenarios: a freelance Pilates instructor who teaches at three studios (likely 1099), a CrossFit coach who runs the morning programming and only coaches your members (likely W-2), and a sub who fills in monthly (likely 1099 if structured right). 1,200 words.
