What Somatic Coaching Actually Means

Most of us try to solve everything by thinking, which means our intellect tries to do the work of our heart and our body as well.

In a session we slow down and pay attention to all three. The exhaustion that lives in your nervous system starts to ease when they’re talking to each other, and the next step often becomes clear from there.

Somatic[soh-mat-ik]adj.

Anything that includes what your body is doing while you talk, where you tense up, when you hold your breath, shifts you don't even notice. You don't have to believe in anything for it to work.

"I have no idea, but I don’t feel like myself anymore."
