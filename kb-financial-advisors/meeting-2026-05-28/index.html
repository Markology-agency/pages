3. The AI-Discovery Story

WHY DEEP POSTS
STILL MATTER.

This is the story we want to walk Landon through. It explains why direct organic clicks on deep posts look modest while overall demand is rising.

The Funnel

Step 1. A tech founder asks ChatGPT or Perplexity "how does QSBS work" or "what is RSU cost basis." The AI answers, citing KB Financial Advisors / Landon Loveall by name as one of the authoritative sources.

Step 2. The founder doesn't click the AI link. They open Google and search "KB Financial Advisors" directly.

Step 3. They land on the homepage or /contact-us/ and book a consultation.

The evidence is in the impression data: /databricks-tender-offer-2026/ pulled 17,117 Google impressions in 25 days but only 29 clicks (0.17% CTR). That's the classic AI Overview pattern. Engines are surfacing the answer with the brand citation, and readers either get the answer right there or pivot to brand search.

The other half of the proof: 88% of homepage organic traffic is branded query traffic. The homepage isn't ranking for deep equity content. The deep content earns the citation; the brand search converts.
