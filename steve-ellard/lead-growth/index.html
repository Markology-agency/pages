Where Steve's leads come from, and where we lose them

We looked at every inquiry from the website and the Facebook and Instagram ads over the last 90 days, next to Google Ads, Google search and website visits for the same period.

The website turns visitors into inquiries at a healthy rate. The gap is earlier in the process. Steve's ads reach about one in five local tax and CPA searches, and most people who find him on Google were already looking for him by name.

The quickest fix is moving $10 a day of ad budget into the tax campaign, which has brought in every paid Google lead. Together the five steps below should add an estimated 3 to 7 inquiries a month going into tax season. We'll measure them against the goal of 10 a month.

What the data says

  • When people search for Steve by name or for Atlantic Payroll, they find him. Those name searches made up 81 of the 103 clicks where Google shows the search term. Everyday searches like "cpa cape cod" brought only 22 clicks in 90 days.
  • Searched from Brewster, Steve's Google Business listing is first in the map results for "cpa cape cod", "cape cod cpa", "tax accountant cape cod" and "tax preparation cape cod", and first for payroll searches as Atlantic Payroll. The map listing links to the homepage, so rank tools credit the homepage. Below the map, the website's best ordinary listing is the "top accounting firms on Cape Cod" blog post.
  • Map results depend on where the searcher is standing. A check from Brewster doesn't tell us whether Steve shows first for someone in Hyannis, Falmouth or Plymouth. Many map clicks also turn into calls or directions, which never reach the website.
  • The tax ad campaign showed for 10% to 29% of eligible searches each month. In October it missed 55% of searches because the daily budget ran out.
  • Payroll search ads cost $13 to $39 a click to reach page one in this area. The payroll campaign's $10 a day has bought no clicks since 10/19.
  • Payroll searchers clicked the old Atlantic Payroll web address 75 times. It currently sends them to the homepage, not the payroll page.
  • October has been quiet, and that matches last year. There were 3 inquiries from October 1 to 22 this year and 3 in the same days of 2025. Last December and January brought 10 to 14 form entries a month, so the next peak is close.
