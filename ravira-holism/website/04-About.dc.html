From operational architecture to human alignment.

For over a decade, I worked in fast-paced startup operations, growth, and executive leadership. I built systems, managed complex workflows, and solved operational puzzles.

I also saw the steep personal toll our culture exacts on high-functioning people. We are rewarded for living completely in our heads, suppressing fatigue, overriding boundaries, and treating life like an optimization problem. When I hit my own crossroads, I learned that logic alone couldn't fix an internal disconnection.

That led me to train in somatic coaching, the Hakomi method, nervous system regulation, and the Enneagram. What I know now is that real clarity isn't something you force through analysis; it is what naturally emerges when your mind, heart, and body are working together.
