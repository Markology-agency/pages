Do You Need Bookkeeping, Accounting or Both?

Most of our clients get both from us for one monthly fee, starting from $275 a month. The two lists below show which part of the work goes by which name.

Bookkeeping

  • Entering and categorizing your transactions
  • Reconciling your bank, card and loan accounts
  • Keeping QuickBooks current from month to month

Accounting

  • Monthly financial statements, delivered by the 28th
  • An accounting manager looking over the numbers for anything that seems off
  • Questions answered when you call
  • Books that are ready when your tax return is due

Say you own a dental office and you bought a new chair in the spring. The bookkeeping side records the purchase and each loan payment as it happens. The accounting side is the statement you get by the 28th, where you can see what the chair did to your cash and your profit while there's still most of the year left to plan around it.

That is also our answer when an owner asks why they'd pay for accounting every month. When we see your books every month, we can catch a problem in March, long before the following April, and your tax return starts from books we already know.

FAQ

What Owners Ask Before Hiring an Accounting Firm in Boulder, CO

What does monthly accounting in Boulder, CO include?

You get your own staff accountant and an accounting manager who checks the work. Each month we process your activity, reconcile up to 3 bank, card or loan accounts, and send your financial statements by the 28th. The starting fee covers up to 25 transactions a month.

How much does an accounting firm in Boulder, CO charge each month?

Our monthly bookkeeping and accounting starts from $275 a month. For a business with fewer than 10 transactions a month, we have a quarterly plan from $500 a quarter that includes the books and a tax projection. We'll tell you which one fits once we see your books.

Can you take over from my current bookkeeper?

We can. We start by looking at how your books are set up today, and if they're current we pick up from the last month that was closed. If they've fallen behind, we catch them up first for a flat fee that we quote before the work starts.

Do you work in QuickBooks Online?

We work with QuickBooks files, including QuickBooks Online, and we'll look at yours on the first call.

Is my business too small or too big for you?

We work with businesses doing from about $50,000 to $10 million a year in revenue. If you're near either end of that range, call us and we'll talk it through.

Do you prepare the tax return from the same books?

We prepare business and personal returns for many of the clients whose books we keep, and those returns are billed separately from the monthly fee. Our tax team is in the office 12 months of the year, so you can reach someone well after April 15th. Our business tax returns page explains how that part works.
