Accounting Questions We Get Asked

Do I need a CPA if I already have a bookkeeper?
−

Not always. A good bookkeeper keeps the transactions recorded and coded. A CPA looks at what those numbers mean for your tax bill, your owner pay, and your next decision. Some clients keep their bookkeeper and have us review the books and do the planning. Others hand us both.

What's the difference between bookkeeping and accounting?
−

Bookkeeping makes sure every transaction is recorded and categorized. Accounting turns those books into answers, like what you can pay yourself, what to set aside for tax, and whether the business can afford the next hire.

Can you take over mid-year?
−

Yes. We catch the books up from January, check them against last year's return, and build the plan from there. The earlier in the year we start, the more room there is to act before December 31.

Who will I actually be working with?
−

Me. I have a few part-time people who help behind the scenes, but they won't be emailing you much. I'm the one you'll talk to.
