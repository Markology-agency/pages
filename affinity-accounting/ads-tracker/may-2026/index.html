SIGNALS.

The Search Campaign is delivering volume, but the search-term mix is misaligned with the productized-advisory ICP. "tax preparers near me," "hr block near me," "jackson hewitt tax service near me," and "tax places near me" are all 1040 retail-tax intent — that's a different buyer than the $1M-$15M owner-led service business Affinity wants. Spend on those terms is bleeding budget.

"cpa milwaukee" at $9.22 CPC with 47 impressions is the most relevant high-intent term in the report. One click cost $9.22 with no conversion. That's not necessarily an ad problem; it's a tracking-or-LP problem.

Performance-Max-Brand has only 15 impressions in 30 days at $1/day budget. Either bid up or pause; right now it's neither serving nor saving budget.

FIX LIST.

1.

Verify conversion tracking is firing

TRACKING

Test the Financial Health Assessment + book-a-call CTAs end-to-end. Inspect the gtag/GA4 conversion pixel on the thank-you page. If GA4-import path is broken, drop a direct AW pixel in. Same playbook we used for Ashford Sky and KB in May (see project notes). Until tracking is verified, no Google Ads campaign decisions should be made on conversion numbers.

2.

Add negative keywords for retail-tax intent

ADS

Add as negatives: "hr block", "jackson hewitt", "tax places", "tax preparer", "tax preparers", "preparers near me." These bring 1040 retail intent into an advisory-firm campaign. Affinity targets $1M-$15M business owners, not retail filers.

3.

Align landing pages to ad-group intent

DEV

"cpa milwaukee" and "accountants near me" should route to /accounting-milwaukee/ not the homepage. "bookkeeping" should route to /bookkeepers-milwaukee/. "tax services" terms route to /tax-services-milwaukee-wi/. Currently the campaign mostly drops on the homepage, which dilutes message match and tanks Quality Score.

4.

Decide on the Performance-Max-Brand campaign

ADS

15 impressions in 30 days at $1/day is essentially off. Either raise daily budget to $5 and let it work as brand-defense, or pause it. Don't leave it half-running.
