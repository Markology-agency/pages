The AI-Discovery Thesis

Deep equity-comp content earns AI citations.
Brand search converts.

For founder/IPO/QSBS/HNW prospects, the discovery path is not "person searches RSU cost basis, lands on KB blog, books a call." It is: person asks ChatGPT or Perplexity about Databricks tender offer math, the engine cites KB's deep post, the person searches "kb financial advisors", lands on the homepage, books. That is why 88% of homepage organic traffic is brand-search, while deep posts pull massive impressions with low direct-click CTR.

The May data validates the thesis. Branded GSC impressions held steady year-over-year while non-branded impression volume cycled with seasonal demand. The Databricks Tender Offer 2026 post pulled 18,654 impressions over 30 days at #1 — those are queries where the searcher's intent is research, not click. The work is being absorbed upstream by AI engines.
