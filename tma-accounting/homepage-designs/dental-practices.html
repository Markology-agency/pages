What Dentists Ask Before Hiring a Dental CPA

What does a dental CPA firm like TMA do for my practice each month?

Every month we record your transactions, check them against your bank statements, and send you reports you can read. We also run your payroll and file the payroll taxes. At year-end, we prepare the practice's tax return from those same books.

Do you work with dental practices outside Indiana?

We do. Our office is in Indianapolis, and we also work with business clients in other states. You and your TMA team work in the same QuickBooks Online books.

Do you work with dental specialists as well as general dentists?

We work with both. General dentists and specialists are among the professional practices we've worked with since 2000.

Can a dental accountant at TMA prepare my personal return too?

Usually, yes. We prepare personal returns for the owners of the practices we work with, as an add-on. The same team prepares it, so your practice and personal numbers agree.

What does a dental CPA cost?

You pay a set monthly fee that we agree on before we start. It depends on how many people you pay, how busy your accounts are, and how many locations you run. Our Price Estimator gives you a ballpark in a couple of minutes.

My practice's books are behind. Can you catch them up?

We can. Catching up is part of getting started with us, and we tell you what it will cost before we begin. After that, we keep your books up to date every month.
