The headline: the drop since March is one page, and it was never buyer traffic

Clicks went 187 in March to 111 in August, and impressions 35,943 to 15,602. That reads like a sitewide collapse. It isn't. /when-can-you-retire-as-a-therapist/ alone went from 25,084 impressions to 4,593 — that single page is most of the fall. It ranks for what age do therapists retire, which is a career-curiosity search, not someone looking for an accountant. It produced 96 clicks and no visible pipeline.

The page that grew over the same period is the one that matters: /accountant-for-therapists-reviews/, up 38 to 85 clicks and from position 10.0 to 6.3, ranking on people researching Heard, the market leader. That is the highest-intent traffic on the site.

So the honest read is: vanity traffic fell, buyer traffic rose, and the total went down. The problem to solve isn't recovering March. It's that the commercial keywords were never won in the first place.

The single biggest item

/s-corporation-tax-calculator/ is 222 words.

It currently ranks for 14 calculator variants worth roughly 2,600 searches a month, all sitting between position 27 and 63 — page 3 to page 7. Nobody sees page 3.

Your own blog is outranking your own service page

bookkeeping for therapists is 140 searches a month at a $20.05 cost-per-click — the most commercially valuable term in this niche. Google is answering it with /best-bookkeeping-software-for-therapists-2025/ at position 34, not with /bookkeeping-for-therapists/, the actual service page. The blog is absorbing the signal meant for the page that sells. That is a fixable internal problem, not a competitive one.

Authority is the ceiling on all of it

SEMrush Authority Score 8. 843 backlinks from 291 referring domains (699 follow, 129 nofollow).

That is enough to win low-competition, specific, long-tail therapist terms — which is exactly what the plan above targets, and why it will work. It is not enough to take broad competitive terms from a venture-funded competitor by writing more posts.

Link acquisition needs to run as its own track alongside the content: therapist podcasts, practice-building communities, and the SimplePractice / TherapyNotes ecosystem. Without it, every cluster above has a hard ceiling around position 8-15.
