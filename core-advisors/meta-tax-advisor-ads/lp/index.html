Before you book

What most owners ask first.

I already have a CPA. Is this a sales call?

It is 30 minutes about your practice and what is not getting planned. If Core is not a fit, Jeremiah will say so. You will leave with at least one thing to fix either way.

Do I have to move everything to Core at once?

No. Most owners start with tax planning or the books and add the rest when it makes sense. The point is that whoever does each piece sits on the same team.

We are not in the Carolinas. Does that matter?

No. We have clients in most states and meet on video. The offices are for the dentists who like to come in.

What do I need to prepare?

Nothing. If you have last year's return and a recent P&L handy, bring them. If not, come anyway.
