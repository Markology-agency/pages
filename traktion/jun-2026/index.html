July Game Plan

1
Lock in the new #1s

Cement the therapist-software rankings before they settle

Several software keywords hit #1 in late June, but they are still volatile week to week (a couple have already eased in early July as Google settles the new positions). Reinforce the software and reviews pages with fresh internal links, an updated publish date, and a tightened title so these hard-won positions hold rather than drift back to page two.

2
Own the comparison searches

Build out the competitor-review page into a real comparison hub

Traktion already ranks page one for Heard and GreenOak review searches, and "heard bookkeeping" (70 volume) sits at #13 just outside it. Expand the reviews page with fair, structured comparisons and clear internal links into the services and CFO pages, so comparison-shoppers have an obvious next step instead of a dead end.

3
Recover the churn

Win back the tracked keywords that slipped in June

The tracked set saw normal SERP churn this month: the top-10 count moved from 16 to 11 as some terms fell while others rose. Pull the keywords that dropped out of the top 10 during June, and push the highest-volume page-two terms ("profit first for therapists" at #16, 260 volume; "therapeutic accounting" at #17, 90 volume) with a content refresh to move them onto page one.
