Does your advertising have any of these red flags?

We rarely see digital advertising done well. Wasted spend, no tracking, bloated fees. Here's how to tell your ad dollars could be working harder.

Red flag

You're measuring likes and shares, rather than leads.

Red flag

You don't have a dedicated landing page for each ad and audience.

Red flag

Your cost-per-click is over $1, or your click-through-rate is under 1%.

Red flag

You're paying for clicks without getting anything in return.

Get your house in order first

The ad is only half the job.

The best design and copy in the world won't convert if the ad leads to a weak landing page. We never spend on ads until your site is in conversion-worthy shape.

Attract quality, not quantity

It's one thing to attract thousands of people. It's another to attract your ideal lead and have them convert. We track how long visitors stay, what they do, and what happens after they leave.

Track the metrics that matter

We skip the vanity metrics and watch the ones tied to results. Our click-through-rate runs consistently above 4% and our cost-per-click below $1. If a campaign isn't hitting ROI, we end it before real money is spent.
