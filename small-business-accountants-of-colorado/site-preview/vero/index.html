Am I too small or too big for your accountants in Boulder, CO?

Our clients do from about $50,000 to $10 million a year in revenue, so most small businesses that call us are well inside the range. If you're above $10 million, a larger firm is probably a better fit, and we'll tell you that.

How much does monthly bookkeeping cost?

Monthly bookkeeping starts at $275 a month. That covers your own staff accountant, an accounting manager who checks the work, up to 3 bank, card or loan accounts reconciled, up to 25 transactions a month and financial statements by the 28th. If your business has fewer than 10 transactions a month, our quarterly plan starts at $500 a quarter and includes the books and a tax projection.

My books are behind. Can you catch them up?

We catch up and clean up QuickBooks files for a flat fee, and we quote that fee before any work starts. Most catch-up clients move to monthly bookkeeping afterward.

Do you prepare both my business return and my personal return?

We prepare returns for S corporations, partnerships and C corporations, and we can prepare your personal return alongside the business one. S corporation and partnership returns start at $1,350, and personal returns start at $750. Tax returns are billed separately from monthly bookkeeping.

Will you help with tax planning if someone else prepares my return?

Tax planning and advisory are for clients whose tax returns we prepare. The advice and the return need to line up, and that works when we're the ones preparing the return.

Can an accountant in Boulder, CO work with my Denver business?

We work with Denver businesses from our Boulder office by phone and email, and you're welcome to visit us on Table Mesa Drive. Boulder is our only office.
