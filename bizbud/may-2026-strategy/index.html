New Plays

FOUR ADJACENT
DIRECTIONS.

Outside the standard service-page rhythm. Four bigger swings that compound on top of the target-keyword work.

Idea 01

Athlete-to-business-owner vertical

Cameron and Jared are both former pro athletes. There's a real and undertargeted audience of athletes transitioning into business: NIL collegiate athletes, retiring pros, athlete-creators. Adjacent to creator-economy taxes but with a sharper hook.

One blog ("How NIL athletes should structure their income") and one LinkedIn campaign tests the niche cheaply. Cameron's background does the heavy lifting on credibility.

Idea 02

Creator economy podcast tour

Pitch Cameron onto two or three creator-economy podcasts per quarter. Short list: Trapital, The Creator Economy Live, Creator Now, Made It In Marketing.

Each appearance gets a backlink and a CTA back to the site. Three or four guest spots per quarter compound both authority and direct traffic on the target-keyword pages.

Idea 03

The 'Creator Tax Estimator' tool

A free interactive calculator that takes platform earnings (YouTube, TikTok, brand deals, Patreon) and outputs a quarterly estimated tax payment. Solves the single biggest creator pain point and earns links + shares.

One-time engineering cost, ongoing lead capture. Tools like this typically convert at 3-5x a standard contact form. Lives as the highest-converting page on the site.

Idea 04

FBAR / FATCA Calculator for digital nomads

Same play, different audience. A simple tool that takes foreign account balances and outputs whether the user needs to file FBAR, FATCA, or both. Anchors the new /expat-tax-services/ page and the digital nomad cluster.

Backlink magnet for expat communities. Modest engineering effort, evergreen utility.
