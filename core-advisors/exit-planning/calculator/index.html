Build notes for Claude Design

Native replacement for the Outgrow calculator. This file is a working prototype, not production code.

What changed from the live Outgrow version

  • The live version asks 5 financial questions and returns one number. It measures money only.
  • Questions 1 to 5 here are the same five, reworded slightly to explain why each is being asked (the add-back logic in particular).
  • Questions 6 to 10 are new, drawn from Jeremiah's Lifestyle vs. Best-In-Class list in his 2026-07-01 email. They measure transferability, which is what actually moves a dental multiple.
  • Those five answers drive the transferability score, the gap bar, and the personalized drivers. The result explains itself and earns the call.

The math (real, verifiable)

  • SDE = collections − (staff × 12) − (opex × 12) + personal add-backs. Owner compensation is collected then added back, so it cancels out. This mirrors the live calculator's inputs.
  • Transferability score = sum of Q6 to Q10 (0 to 3 each, 15 max) as a percentage. Thresholds are EPI's: 50% or below is discounted, 58% to 72% is above average, above 72% is best-in-class.
  • Drivers are the lowest-scoring answers, surfaced worst-first. Score 3 on everything and the section flips to a strengths message rather than inventing problems.

Still blocked on sourcing

  • MULT_TODAY MULT_BIC GAP_$ (no sourced dental multiples yet). EPI's 6× vs 3.5× is manufacturing. Until real dental/DSO comps land, these stay tokens. Do not fill them with a plausible guess.
  • The gap bar's proportions are currently driven by the transferability score, which is honest and computable, rather than by a fake dollar gap.
