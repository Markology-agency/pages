WHAT TO TALK
ABOUT WITH KB.

Three observations from the data heading into the 5/28 meeting.

1. PMax Brand is the only converting campaign right now

Performance Max - Brand KW + Remarketing produced the single booking_complete in the window plus 2 lead form submits. At $25 of spend. It's the lowest-budget campaign in the account (currently $1/day budget) and it's outperforming. Recommend doubling budget to $5-$10/day.

2. Databricks Pre-IPO is a top-of-funnel discovery play

$232 of spend, 50 clicks, zero bookings. That's expected. The keyword "databricks ipo" is informational intent, not commercial intent. The right benchmark for this campaign is "did it produce qualified branded search later?" Branded GSC impressions went from 226 in February to 371 in April. Some of that lift is attributable to Databricks-spend awareness, especially among Databricks employees seeing both the SEO post AND the ad.

3. The SF search campaigns need a closer look

$154 on 15 clicks at $10.28 CPC and zero booking_complete. The audience strategy says HNW tech founders nationally, not "anyone in San Francisco searching for a tax accountant." Recommend: either narrow the SF keyword set to founder/IPO/RSU-adjacent intent (cut "tax preparer", "tax filing"), or pause the campaign and redeploy spend to the QSBS content cluster going live in June.
