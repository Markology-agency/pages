The three-source workflow holds:

  • Founder-voice library tells us WHAT Mebea has substantive material on. Every H2 outline is built from his actual takes and vocabulary.
  • Sitemap + Asana full-board dump tells us WHAT NOT to cover. v1 missed an in-flight "First 90 Days" blog because the cannibalization check used keyword search; v2 dumps every Traktion task title and compares semantically. Zero conflicts remain.
  • SEMrush tells us WHICH KEYWORD each topic should target. 9 of 12 blogs have direct keyword + volume + difficulty data attached. 3 are flagged as pillar/thought-leadership pieces (high practical value, low search volume) and earn their slot via brand authority.

Titles read like real search queries, not internal Markology jargon. The reference transformation: "Forecasting Revenue When Your Income Looks Like a Roller Coaster" became "How To Plan Your Therapy Practice Finances When Months Are Rocky." Every other v1 title got the same treatment.
