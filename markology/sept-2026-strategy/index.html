THE RANKINGS
ARRIVED.
THE CLICKS DIDN'T.

Twelve months ago markology.io sat at an average search position of 58. Today it sits at 17, and it holds page one for keyword clusters it was nowhere near a year ago. That is the hard part of SEO, and it is done. What has not caught up is click capture: over the last 90 days, 10,395 non-branded impressions on named queries produced one click, and even on the most generous reading of the anonymized remainder the non-branded click rate lands under 0.4%. The ranking asset is built. This memo is about converting it.

WHAT A 3% CTR WOULD BE WORTH

Non branded clicks on named queries, last 90 days1
Non branded clicks at a 3% CTR on the same impressions103
Implied monthly organic clicks, all sourcesRoughly 71 vs 37 today
Branded CTR today, for comparison16.6%

Three percent is a deliberately conservative target for pages sitting at position 3 to 8. The branded CTR of 16.6% shows the site converts a search result fine when the searcher already wants Markology. The gap is on discovery terms.

One caveat, stated plainly. Google withholds rare queries for privacy, so named query rows account for 56 of the 114 total clicks in this window. The other 58 cannot be split between branded and non branded. Even assuming every single one of them was non branded, the non branded click rate is 0.375%, against a reasonable expectation of 2 to 5% for the positions this site holds. The finding survives the worst case.

Game Plan

SEVEN MOVES,
IN PRIORITY ORDER.

1
This Week

Rewrite titles and metas on the ten pages in the Next Unlock table

Every one of these pages is already visible and none of them are earning a click. Write the title to answer the query and give a reason to click that an AI summary cannot satisfy, such as a real number, a case study result, or a named tool comparison.

Trigger: 3,419 non branded impressions at position 15 or better produced one click in 90 days. At 3% CTR this alone roughly doubles total organic clicks.

This Week

Check whether an AI Overview is firing on the top six zero click terms

Manually search best website builder for accountants, accounting website builder, ai marketing services accountants, ai marketing for accountants, seo case study accountants and content strategy for accountants. Record whether an AI Overview appears and whether markology.io is cited inside it.

Trigger: a 0.01% non branded CTR across 479 queries is too uniform to be a titles problem alone. This test separates "our snippet is weak" from "the click is being absorbed upstream."

30 Days

/post/best-ai-marketing-for-accountants, /the-best-ai-marketing-for-accountants-course/ and /best-ai-marketing-for-accountants/ all rank for the same two terms at positions 3.5, 3.5 and 12.5. Pick the strongest, redirect the other two into it, and consolidate the internal links.

30 Days

30 Days

Build the AI citation play around /geo-for-accountants/

Trigger: one of the last three leads arrived from chatgpt.com, and the newest lead asked specifically about AI search optimization. Demand is arriving through AI assistants before it arrives through Google.

It is the strongest ranking asset on the site and it attracts DIY searchers. Either add a clear "or have us build it" conversion path partway down the page, or accept it as a top of funnel awareness play and stop measuring it on leads.

Trigger: 2,064 impressions, more than four times the agency hire intent terms combined, pointed at a page whose visitors are self identifying as not hiring an agency.
New Plays

FOUR IDEAS
WORTH A LOOK.

Play 01

Ask every booking where they heard about you

Add one optional field to the Calendly intake. Branded search and direct traffic both mean somebody else did the introducing, and analytics can never tell you who. One question closes the gap that no amount of UTM work will.

Play 02

Run the E-E-A-T sprint on yourselves

Markology ships this for clients. The site that sells AI visibility should be the most cited one in the category. The geo-for-accountants page at #2 is the natural anchor for it.

Play 03

Publish the zero click finding as content

A first party case study showing 10,395 impressions and one click, with the diagnosis and the fix, is exactly the proof accounting firm owners are searching for. It doubles as a sales asset and as bait for the seo case study cluster already ranking.

Play 04

Track AI assistant referrals as a named channel

One of the last three leads came from chatgpt.com and one earlier lead came from the same source in August. Split AI referrals out in the leads sheet now, while the volume is still small enough to read every row.
