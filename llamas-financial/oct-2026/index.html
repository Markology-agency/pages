What is working

  • The profit margin post holds position 1 for vineyard business profit margins, which is the biggest single source of visits.
  • The winery accounting basics post ranks 2 and 4 for winery bookkeeping and winery accounting.
  • The winery software post reaches position 3 for tasting room inventory software and position 6 for winery accountants.

What to watch

  • Our winery accounting and winery bookkeeping service pages rank at 61 and 87 for those same terms, while blog posts hold the top spots. Google is choosing the blogs over the service pages, so we want to point the service pages' internal links and titles at those searches.
  • Winery tax (position 17) and winery CFO (position 16) are the closest service searches to page one.
  • Search volumes in this niche are small, usually 30 to 90 a month, so growth will come from covering more winery searches, not from any one term.
