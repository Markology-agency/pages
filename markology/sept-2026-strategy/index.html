THE SITE MIGRATION
IS STILL BLEEDING.

markology.io is not being found on Google and AI search for the services it sells. The cause is not content and it is not competition. Search Console URL inspection shows the two highest-impression pages on the site redirect visitors to the homepage instead of to the article they clicked, and the SEO service page points Google at a canonical URL that Google has never crawled. Six of six primary service keywords sit outside the top 100. All of it is plumbing, and all of it is fixable this month.

Game Plan

SEVEN MOVES,
IN PRIORITY ORDER.

The first three are plumbing. Nothing else on this list is worth doing until they are done, because every other improvement will be split across two URLs or redirected to the homepage.

1
Today

Repoint the two blog redirects away from the homepage

Redirect /post/facebook-vs-google-ads to /facebook-vs-google-ads/, which already exists and is found at position 7.2. For /post/best-website-builder-for-accountants, first establish whether the article survived the migration. If it did, redirect to it. If it did not, republish it, then redirect.

Trigger: URL Inspection returns googleCanonical / for both. 5,599 impressions over four months, and every clicker lands on the homepage instead of the article they chose.
2
Today

Fix the SEO service page canonical

Either make /seo-for-accountants/ real, crawlable, linked and in the sitemap, then 301 the old address to it, or change the page's declared canonical to the address Google already has indexed. Either works. The current state, pointing at a URL Google has never seen, is the one option that cannot.

Trigger: /seo-for-accountants is indexed and declares a canonical to /seo-for-accountants/, which Search Console reports as "URL is unknown to Google". 12 keywords in search a year ago, zero today, 4,936 impressions and no clicks.
3
This Week

Audit every URL for the duplicate address problem and clean the sitemap

Crawl the site, list every page that exists at both a trailing slash and a non trailing slash address, pick one form, 301 the other, and regenerate the sitemap so it contains only live canonical URLs. Search Console shows the current sitemap still referencing old addresses that redirect.

Trigger: at least 12 pages are duplicated across two addresses, including contact, about-us, faqs, terms, blog, and four of the five service pages. Google is splitting every signal across both.
4
30 Days

Rebuild the SEO service page around the terms it used to hold

Once the canonical is fixed, target the twelve terms the page held in September 2025: accountant seo, accounting seo, accountancy seo, seo for accountant, seo accounting, cpa website seo and the rest. Pair Google and AI search in the copy throughout, since that is now the actual buying question.

Trigger: SEO for Accountants is a named service on the pricing page, and zero of the six primary keyword themes in the brand file are in the top 100 today.
5
30 Days

Consolidate the three competing AI marketing pages

/post/best-ai-marketing-for-accountants, /the-best-ai-marketing-for-accountants-course/ and /best-ai-marketing-for-accountants/ all compete for the same two queries at positions 3.5, 6.9 and 14.7. Keep the course page separate because it sells a different product, merge the other two.

Trigger: 484 impressions split across three addresses for one query set. The same split exists on the content strategy pair.
6
30 Days

Put UTM parameters on every booking link and add a "how did you hear about us" field

Hard coded UTMs make attribution survive cross device journeys. The intake field catches what analytics structurally cannot, which is who introduced the person before they typed the brand name into Google.

Trigger: 40 of 43 bookings in the leads sheet have no recoverable source, and the most recent lead arrived through a branded search that no tracking can explain on its own.
7
60 Days

Rewrite titles and descriptions once the URLs are clean

Every page currently visible and earning no clicks needs a title that answers the query and gives a reason to click that an AI summary cannot satisfy. Do this after the plumbing, not before, so the work lands on the address that will survive.

Trigger: 3,419 non branded impressions at position 15 or better produced one click. Some of that is snippet quality, but most of it is Google serving an address that redirects or competes with itself.
