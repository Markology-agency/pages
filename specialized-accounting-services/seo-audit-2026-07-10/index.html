1 · The migration is the whole story

Diagnosed 2026-06-22, re-tested today. Nothing has changed. This is the single highest-cost issue on the account and it is a hosting-config fix, not a content problem.

The 301 path-preserving redirect still has not shipped

Every SEO-bearing inner URL on sas-cpas.com (every franchise page, ROBS page, service page, the /blog/ index, and every ranking post) still returns a hard 404. Only the bare root forwards. GoDaddy basic domain forwarding catches the root and nothing else. The equivalent pages on sas-acct.com all return 200 — this is purely a redirect rule that was never added.

What this is costing

SEMrush still shows 30 keywords and the bulk of the referring-domain equity sitting on the orphaned old-domain URLs (≈175 referring domains on the new domain vs ≈240 on the old). Google reads sas-acct.com as a brand-new site: no sitelinks, no knowledge panel, non-brand head terms (franchise accounting, ROBS) absent from the top 30. The fix is a path-preserving 301 wildcard; Apache + Nginx snippets are already written in ~/Desktop/SAS-redirect-fix-kit.txt. Deploy is ~30 minutes of hosting work.

The plan — sequenced by impact

Everything below priority 1 is throttled by priority 1. Ship the redirect first.

1

Deploy the path-preserving 301 wildcard

sas-cpas.com → sas-acct.com, path-for-path. Snippets already written. This is the unlock for every stranded ranking and referring domain. Needs hosting access to the old GoDaddy domain (or move its DNS to a host that supports rewrite rules).

Effort: ~30 min hosting · Impact: highest on the account · Blocker: access to old domain host

2

Build the real structured-data graph

Replace the stub with an AccountingService/LocalBusiness + Organization graph: NAP (888-600-0075, Pleasant Prairie WI), geo, hours, logo, sameAs (LinkedIn, GBP, Top Workplaces award). Strip Article schema from all non-blog pages and set Service/WebPage.

Effort: 1 pass via Rank Math/REST · Impact: sitelinks, knowledge panel, local

3

Reclaim the non-brand near-wins

Expand the thin vertical/service LPs that already rank pos 10–38 (restaurant, exit-strategies, CAT, specialty accounting), add internal links from the blog library into them, and target the WI restaurant-franchise angle. Franchise accounting + ROBS head terms follow once equity returns.

Effort: rolling content + internal linking · Impact: non-brand recovery

4

On-page brand + hygiene cleanup

Align titles/metas to "Specialized Accounting Services / SAS" (drop the legacy "SAS CPAs"), fix the British "specialises", resolve the two double-H1 pages, tidy the 2 short / 1 long meta descriptions.

Effort: quick REST pass · Impact: consistency + CTR

5

Local SEO layer

SAS is national-franchise-first, but the Pleasant Prairie office is ranking (weakly) for SE-Wisconsin queries with no local signals. LocalBusiness schema (priority 2) plus a GBP tune-up is low-effort incremental reach.

Effort: low · Impact: incremental local capture

Ship the redirect. Everything else follows.

The site is not the problem. The handoff from the old domain is. Priority 1 is a half-hour of hosting work that has been sitting for 18 days, and it gates the entire non-brand recovery.
