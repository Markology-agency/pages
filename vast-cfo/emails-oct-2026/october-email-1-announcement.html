Build a 2027 restaurant budget you’ll actually use

We’ll cover:
•The five layers of a restaurant budget, from gross revenue to profit.
•Labor budgeted fully loaded, not just wages.
•A month-by-month budget that expects your slow season.
•The cash forecast and reserves the P&L budget misses.
