The Real Story Behind the Session Drop

Q4 2025 was not the baseline. The Q4 spike was direct, not search.

The pattern is clean in the GA4 data. From Aug 2025 through Dec 2025, Direct sessions exploded from 250 a month to 1,491 a month, while Organic Search stayed in a 137-251 band the whole time. Total sessions hit 1,830 in November on the back of that direct surge. From January 2026 forward, Direct started normalizing — 841, 518, 539, 423, 371. The fall-off in total sessions is almost entirely the Direct channel returning to mid-2025 levels.

Organic search is a different story. Through the same period it ranged 137 to 251 sessions per month and never broke trend. May 2026 organic (188) is within 12% of January (214), and the tracked-keyword footprint is up 3x year-over-year. Translation: the SEO program is steady, but the firm was getting a brand-attention boost in late 2025 from somewhere outside search (podcast, press, ABM push, paid social spillover, or a tracking artifact) that didn't carry into Q1.

June Game Plan

What we ship next.

Three priorities going into Q3. Each is a Markology-side build with measurable output by month-end, not a recommendation.

01

Push three top-10 keywords into top-3.

"dental practice value calculator" (vol 110, #6 → target #3), "dental financial advisors" (vol 140, #8 → target #3), "dentist advisors" (vol 210, #9 → target #3). Each gets the same playbook: title and meta refresh, FAQ schema, three internal links from sibling content, body answer-first restructure. Same recipe used to push "dental practice value calculator" from #9 to #6 this cycle.

The math is meaningful. Top-3 CTR runs 25-30% versus 5-10% for positions 6-10. Pushing all three from page-one-bottom to page-one-top approximately quadruples organic click volume on the target queries.

02

Ship the E-E-A-T schema sprint.

The dental-financial-advisor space rewards verifiable credentials and named expert authorship more than any other professional vertical. Adding the schema layer is mechanical: Organization on the homepage with knowsAbout (dental tax planning, practice valuation, retirement planning, dental CFO), Person schema for each advisor with credentials and areas of expertise, Service schema on the seven commercial pages, FAQ schema plus visible Common Questions HTML on the top 10 recent blog posts, and an author-bio block on those same posts.

The schema sprint runs on Markology authority via WP REST. The scorecard with before/after snapshots will live at /core-advisors/eeat/.

03

Build the dental practice acquisition content hub.

The Cost to Buy a Dental Practice post and the Free Dental Practice Valuation Calculator are already the firm's strongest non-brand performers (#3 and #6). The acquisition cluster has natural breadth: associate-to-owner transition, financing options, due diligence, partnership buy-ins, valuation methods, transition planning. A dedicated hub at /dental-practice-acquisition/ ties those existing posts together with internal links and a fresh hub page targeting "buying a dental practice" and "dental practice acquisition" head terms.

The pattern compounds. Once a hub exists, every new blog in the cluster links to the hub and to siblings, which raises the topical-authority signal Google reads across the entire acquisition lane.
