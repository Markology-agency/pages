The Four Patterns

WHERE THE LOSS IS COMING FROM.

Every plateau has a cause. Traktion's drift is not random churn. The pages losing positions group into four clean patterns. Each has a different fix.

01

S-corp calculator long-tail collapsing

/s-corporation-tax-calculator/

The primary keyword "s corp tax calculator" (1,300 monthly searches) is stable at #47. But the entire long-tail cluster around it is dropping out:

  • "s corp distribution tax calculator": #36 → #83 (−47)
  • "tax calculator for s corp": #49 → #73 (−24)
  • "s corp payroll tax calculator": #69 → #89 (−20)
  • "s corp tax calculator excel free": #68 → #83 (−15)
  • "s corp savings calculator": #76 → #90 (−14)
Likely cause: Page is calculator-heavy with thin educational content. Google has been demoting thin tool pages in 2026 unless they have substantive content alongside the tool.
02

Dallas service pages lost local visibility

/dallas-bookkeepers/ + /dallas-payroll-services/

Both Dallas pages lost almost every Dallas geo keyword they held in January:

  • "bookkeepers in dallas": #10 → dropped out of top 100
  • "bookkeeper in dallas": #65 → dropped
  • "payroll companies dallas": #16 → dropped
  • "dallas payroll services": #93 → dropped
  • "bookkeeping dallas": #89 → dropped
Likely cause: March 2026 local pack reshuffle. Pages may be too thin compared to Dallas-local competitors with stronger LocalBusiness signals and citation depth.
03

Content decay on the 2025-labeled software guide

/best-bookkeeping-software-for-therapists-2025/

The slug literally says "2025" in May 2026. Multiple high-volume keywords dropped:

  • "quickbooks for therapists": #13 → #39 (−26)
  • "accounting software for therapists": #9 → #21 (−12)
  • "best bookkeeping software for therapists": #32 → dropped
  • "best accounting software for therapists": #12 → dropped
  • "mental health accounting software": #29 → dropped
Likely cause: 2025 stats, pricing, and tool versions read as stale next to 2026-fresh competitor content. The dated slug compounds the signal.
04

Cannibalization on therapist tax deductions

/every-tax-write-off-for-therapists-2025/ + /tax-deductions-for-therapists-2025/

Two URLs competing for the same query "tax deductions for therapists":

  • /every-tax-write-off-for-therapists-2025/: #63 in January
  • /tax-deductions-for-therapists-2025/: #59 in April (new URL)
  • "are therapy sessions tax deductible": split between both URLs (#48 + #54 in Jan; #57 + #69 in Apr)
  • "therapist tax deductions": #68 split across both
Likely cause: Two URLs targeting the same query split signals. Neither ranks as well as one consolidated page would. The newer URL is slightly outranking the older.
