I'm a Founder With QSBS Stock

QSBS is the single most underused tax break in the founder playbook. Holding qualified small business stock for five years can mean $10 million per holder of capital gains excluded from federal tax. Stacking the exemption across spouse and trusts can push that to $20 million or more. This hub covers the founder lifecycle from 83(b) at incorporation through pre-exit planning and QSBS stacking strategies.
