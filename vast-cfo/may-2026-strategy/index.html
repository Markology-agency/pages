New Plays

FOUR IDEAS
OUTSIDE THE PLAYBOOK.

Things that aren't on the standard SEO checklist but fit how Vast CFO actually wins clients.

Tanya video Q&A on YouTube

Google Search Console shows dozens of long-tail questions Vast already ranks for ("a profitable restaurant typically averages a 28% to 35% food cost percentage" with 0% CTR). Tanya is already on camera for LinkedIn. A YouTube series answering one specific operator question per video would rank for those long-tails and double as social content.

Restaurant break-even calculator v2

The /restaurant-profitability-calculator/ page is already ranking for several calculator-related queries (#30, #43, #42). Upgrading the tool itself, with branded results owners can save or share, would push it into the top 10 and create a natural lead capture for the email follow-up Tanya does best.

Multi-concept hospitality reporting kit

The "Multi-Concept Operator" persona in the client brief has no dedicated content yet. A downloadable reporting template (P&L by location, consolidated cash flow, KPI dashboard) gated behind email capture would attract a higher-tier prospect and let Tanya have a strategic conversation, not an explanatory one.

Quarterly restaurant benchmarks page

Vast's data shows what real restaurant clients run at. A quarterly benchmarks post (food cost %, labor %, EBITDA margin by service style: full-service, fast-casual, QSR, fine-dining) would rank for benchmark queries that have high volume and almost no good answers in search results. Anonymized aggregates only, no client names.
