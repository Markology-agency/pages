What I'd Change Before Beta

  • 01
    YouTube is its own month (Month 11), optional in fast-track. Camera + editing cadence kills most non-naturals. Solos can skip this entirely; the leads-now sprint covers them instead.
  • 02
    Ads stay minimal. Most accountants should not DIY Google Ads. Module 12.1 plus a clean "or hire Markology" link is the right scope.
  • 03
    Fast-track decision deferred to post-build. Per Whitney 2026-06-19: build the full deep-dive first. Once shipped, decide which sub-modules are "must-watch for leads" vs "nice-to-have for understanding."
  • 04
    Practice management integration is Month 2 (its own month). Connecting Claude to Canopy / Carbon / Karbon / QuickBooks is your moat. No other accountant marketing course teaches this. Promoted from a sub-module to a full month because the integration spans practice management + scheduling + CRM + workflow tools.
  • 05
    The "5-tab test" is the canonical teaching device. Named in Month 6 (Website Conversion) and powered by /website-audit. Use on the sales page too: "Open 5 accountant tabs. Yours is one of them. 15 seconds each. What makes them close yours? What makes them stay?"
  • 06
    Tracking + Reporting is Month 7 (not the end). Per Whitney 2026-06-19: measurement has to be wired up AFTER you build your website (Month 6) and BEFORE you spend 5 months on marketing channels. Otherwise members spend months building social, email, content, etc. without knowing what's working. KPIs that matter, conversion tracking, UTMs, GA4 + GSC routines, when to double down vs pivot.
