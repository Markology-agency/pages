THE NICHE QUESTION TO ANSWER FIRST

Ashford Sky markets itself to four audiences simultaneously: e-commerce, SaaS, acquisition entrepreneurs, and Utah small businesses. For a new firm without ranked keywords, that's three audiences too many. The first 6 months should pick ONE wedge and own it. Recommendation: lead with "Outsourced Tax Department for SaaS" — it's the most specific positioning on the site, the highest-margin engagement type, and the audience least likely to be locked in with existing CPAs. Build the SaaS-niche content and trust signals first. Utah local visibility (this audit) becomes secondary work that adds Salt Lake City reach for SaaS clients who care about an in-state CPA. National SaaS clients don't care about geography.

Decision needed: Is the next 6 months SaaS-first, with Salt Lake City local as a parallel track? Or local-first with SaaS as a side bet? The audits look very different depending on the answer.
