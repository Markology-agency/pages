May / June Game Plan

WHAT WE WORK
NEXT.

Six items, ordered by impact. Items 1 and 2 ship this week. Items 3 to 6 ship in the next 30 to 60 days.

01

Title and meta rewrite on the breakout articles

The Distributions in Excess of Basis article and the Accountable Plans article together rack up over 109,000 monthly impressions but convert at 0.22 to 0.35% click-through rate. Sharpening their titles and meta descriptions to match the actual searcher intent ("S-corp distributions taxable", "partnership accountable plan rules") projects roughly 2,500 to 3,000 additional clicks per quarter at zero new content cost.

This Week
02

Set up proper Google Ads conversion tracking

Right now Google Ads has only one conversion action configured ("GTM Submit lead form") and it has registered zero conversions in 90 days. Set up real outcome tracking: phone call clicks, contact form submissions on the actual page, and any Calendly booking events if applicable. Without this, every dollar of ad spend is invisible to the optimization algorithm.

New Plays

FOUR NEW
DIRECTIONS.

Outside the standard SEO and content rhythm. Four bigger swings worth piloting if you're open to them.

Idea 01

Montana small business owner email series

The Distributions article is bringing in dental practice owners, ranchers, outfitters, and other small business owners across Montana. Capture them with a free 7-email "Montana small business tax checklist" series gated by email signup on the article.

Builds an owned list, primes warm leads for your seasonal outreach, and makes Andrew the go-to Montana tax person they call when March panic hits.

Idea 02

Local Missoula partnerships and PR

Three or four placed mentions in Missoula-relevant publications (Missoulian business section, Montana Free Press, local industry associations) substantially move the needle on the local CPA terms currently stuck around #16.

Pair with one or two community signal-builders: chamber of commerce membership listing, BBB profile, sponsorship of a small business event. Modest investment, durable local trust.

Idea 03

Seasonal video content for social

Andrew on camera, two to three minute video, plain-spoken Montana tax tips: "What ranchers need to know about depreciation," "1099 vs W-2 for outfitters," "Why your side gig might be costing you money." Posted to LinkedIn and Facebook on a steady cadence around tax season triggers.

Local audiences trust faces. Andrew's voice is a brand asset that text content can't replace.

Idea 04

Annual "What changed for Montana taxpayers" guide

A downloadable PDF released every January, summarizing federal and Montana tax changes for the year ahead. Promoted on the site, on social, and via email.

One annual deliverable, multiple uses: lead magnet, organic content piece, local press hook, social content for the entire month of January.
