Workstream 2 · Social

Eight content pillars. Proactive voice.

Cadence: 2 posts per week per the existing Affinity calendar, anchored to the SamePager Marketing Top 3 #2 — content cadence on proactive-advisor positioning. Every post embodies the Proactive-Not-Reactive value (the firm's central differentiator). LinkedIn is primary; Facebook + Instagram secondary. Each post passes the shareability test: would a $2M Milwaukee or Chicago owner forward this to a peer?

1
Proactive vs Reactive

What it looks like: a specific moment where a real advisor surfaces something before the client asks. Example: "Three things we flag on a $3M agency's books before they bring up cash flow." Models the brand's central value.

2
Numbers Most Owners Miss

What it looks like: margin by service line, owner pay vs draws, AR aging past 60 days, gross profit per client. Specific numbers, real benchmarks. Aimed at $1M-$15M service-business owners running their books without these reads.

3
Pricing Decisions

What it looks like: Milwaukee + Chicago service-business pricing benchmarks. Retainer vs hourly math. When to raise prices. Built on real industry data (SoDA, Agency Management Institute).

4
CFO Advisory Moments

What it looks like: anonymized case studies — "An owner-led $4M firm came to us thinking they had a sales problem. The math said pricing." Tells the truth, kindly. Aimed at owners considering whether they need a CFO.

5
Tax Strategy Moments

What it looks like: Q2/Q3/Q4 deadline anchors. S-corp election math. Owner pay + distribution timing. WI + IL state-specific rules. Time-sensitive, screenshot-able.

6
Milwaukee + Chicago Trust

What it looks like: local visual signals — Walker's Point, Third Ward, Loop. Community references. Reinforces the local-relationship positioning for owner-led firms who pick CPAs based on relationship, not commodity.

7
Productized Advisory Framework

What it looks like: demystify the three-service-line model (Monthly Accounting → Tax → CFO Advisory). Show the cadence, the deliverables, the scoreboard. Differentiates from transactional-only CPAs.

8
FHA Conversion Posts

What it looks like: direct calls to take the Financial Health Assessment. One post per month. Frames FHA as a diagnostic, not a sales tool. Maps to SamePager Marketing Top 3 #1 (launch FHA as primary lead magnet).

Post format defaults: bold hook in line 1, supporting context in 1-2 short paragraphs, specific number or named rule, single CTA (book a call OR take the FHA — never both), graphic does heavy lifting. 0 em-dashes. No no-X-no-Y cadence. No soft-encouraging cliches ("this is fixable", "you got this"). Tell-the-truth-kindly register.
