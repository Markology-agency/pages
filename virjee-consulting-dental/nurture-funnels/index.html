Where people get nothing

1. Webinar registrants after the replay

The Sept 30 replay went to 59 people. Nothing follows it, so a registrant who didn't book hears nothing until the next invite.

Fix: a short follow-up after each replay (one idea from the webinar, then a book-a-call email).

2. Practice buyers

The 4-email buying series is switched off. Buyers are never offered a call, so a buyer who doesn't book gets nothing.

Agreed: turn it on as soon as the empty table in email #2 is fixed.

3. Valuation calculator users

Their first email thanks them for joining the mailing list. No email mentions their valuation or what to do with the number.

Fix: a first email about their result and a written valuation, then the existing series.

4. After any series ends

Every journey stops after its last email. The Law series even promises "our regular newsletter", but no newsletter is set up in HubSpot.

Fix: add everyone who finishes a series to a monthly newsletter list.

5. Medical contacts who don't book

The medical welcome stays off on purpose. Medical owners and startups who book now get 3 emails, including Dr. Delaney's video, the same as dental owners.

Decided: leave the medical welcome off.

6. Form filled, no call booked

Emily's 4-email Book a Call follow-up catches these people once a deal is created. The 1-hour reminder we built is off so the two don't double up.

Decision for Omar and Emily: keep Emily's sequence alone, or add the 1-hour reminder in front of it.
