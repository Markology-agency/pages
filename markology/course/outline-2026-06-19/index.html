What I'd Change Before Beta

  • 01
    YouTube is risky for most accountants. Camera + editing cadence kills non-naturals. Month 9 stays OPTIONAL in fast-track. For solos, replace with a "leads-now sprint" instead.
  • 02
    Ads stay minimal. Most accountants should not DIY Google Ads. Module 12.3 plus a clean "or hire Markology" link is the right scope.
  • 03
    For fast-track, swap Months 6 and 7. Reviews + GBP drive faster local leads than lead magnets. Deep-dive keeps current order.
  • 04
    Practice management integration promoted to Module 1.4. Connecting Claude to Canopy / Carbon / Karbon / QuickBooks is your moat. No other accountant marketing course teaches this. Foundation module + deeper per-tool workflows in the Bonus library.
  • 05
    The "5-tab test" is the canonical teaching device. Named in Module 1.8 and used on the sales page. Memorable, recommendable, accountant-friendly.
  • 06
    Drop the "weekend completable" framing. At $299/mo to a serious accountant audience, "do it in a weekend" undersells the product. The honest framing is: Month 1 is the substantive core that gets you set up and producing. Months 2 through 12 are monthly new skill drops that compound. Self-paced, no time pressure, but real work.
