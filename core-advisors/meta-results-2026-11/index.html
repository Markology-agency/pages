What happens after someone fills in the form

Each lead arrives in Core's website leads with their answers and phone number. They then receive a series of four emails under Jeremiah's name over ten days, which includes their calculator or Sellability Check link. Since November 16 the last screen of the form also has a button that says "Book your call", and it opens Core's booking page.
