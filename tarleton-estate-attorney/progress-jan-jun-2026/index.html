Executive Summary

The headline. Tarleton's organic footprint roughly tripled in five months: SEMrush went from 127 ranking keywords in January to 298 by mid-May (+135%). Google Search Console clicks grew 61% (Jan to May) while average position improved more than 5 spots (19.9 to 14.5). The site brought in 1,120 organic clicks against 155,952 impressions over the period.

The leads picture. 49 qualified contact-form leads in the period (8 per month average), with 61% in estate planning / wills & trusts and 18% in probate. Nearly half of those leads said they have no will or trust in place, which is a strong intent signal.

The ads pivot. Markology consolidated the Google Ads account from three brand-heavy campaigns down to a single high-intent Probate Lawyer Search campaign. That campaign produced 13 of the 16 paid conversions on $597 of the $968 total H1 spend, working out to a $45.94 cost per lead. The legacy brand campaigns have been paused.

What's next. Three priorities for H2: push the Dallas commercial cluster from page two to page one (six queries within striking distance), launch a Tyler location page to replicate the Dallas/Terrell pattern in a third market, and ship the four remaining wp-admin items from today's June 17 technical audit.

5. Priorities for H2 2026

Priority 1 — Push the Dallas commercial cluster from page two to page one

Six high-intent Dallas queries are sitting between positions 5 and 16 with real impression volume. "Estate attorney dallas" alone shows 672 impressions at position 16.7 with one click. Moving these into the top 5 is the biggest near-term organic lever. Tactic: expand the homepage and "Estate Planning Dallas, TX" service page to 1,300-1,500 words each, add internal links from blog posts to these commercial pages with exact-match anchors, and ship 2-3 supporting Dallas-specific blog posts ("Dallas probate court process," "Dallas County estate planning checklist," "Cost of an estate plan in Dallas").

Priority 2 — Replicate Dallas/Terrell pattern in a third Texas market

The Tyler location page draft is sitting in the SEO folder from May 21. Tyler is roughly two hours east of Dallas, sits in Smith County, and has demonstrated search volume for estate planning attorneys. Cost to publish: one page build + Local schema + GBP listing creation. Replicates the Dallas/Terrell footprint at minimal incremental effort. Recommend building before September to capture Q4 estate-planning seasonality.

Priority 3 — Finish today's audit handoff (robots.txt + llms.txt + 2 H1 edits)

Today's June 17 audit shipped 6 of 10 P1 fixes via REST. The remaining 4 need wp-admin clicks because Rank Math's settings endpoint requires logged-in cookie auth (not Application Password) and Elementor H1 edits don't render-cache from REST writes. All four are documented with paste-ready content in the handoff doc. Expected total time: 15 minutes. Targets: 85-plus overall SEO score.

Priority 4 — Lock in conversion tracking for organic leads

The contact form currently doesn't preserve UTM parameters across the site navigation flow. That's why 48 of 49 H1 leads show as "Organic / Direct" rather than properly attributed. Recommend a small Gravity Forms hidden-field configuration that captures landing page, referrer, and last-touch UTM. With that in place, the Q3 progress report will be able to attribute exactly which channels are producing leads, not just paid.

Priority 5 — H2 content calendar focused on Probate + Business Succession

Probate is the second-largest lead category (9 of 49) but only has a fraction of the blog real estate that estate planning gets. Business succession is the third-largest (5 leads) and has even less coverage. Recommend 6 blog posts per quarter in Q3 and Q4 split evenly between probate workflow content and business-succession content, mapped to the existing Probate Lawyer ad campaign and the underserved "Business / Succession" lead segment.
