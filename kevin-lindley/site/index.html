the problem

You Shouldn't Find Out What You Owe When It's Already Too Late

Most CPAs are what I'd call a compliance CPA. They type in the boxes, file the return, and make sure you don't go to jail. That is a real service, but it isn't planning, and it's why the number in April surprises people.

The complaint I hear on almost every intro call goes the same way. You email a question and a week later you get a response that isn't really an answer. You send documents in March and get a bill in April that nobody warned you about. And the only clear picture you ever get of your business is a year-old snapshot, once the return is finally done.

Meanwhile the income keeps changing. A new contract, a second location, some expert witness work, a rental property. Nobody is looking at the whole picture while it's still moving.

What most folks don't realize is that the CPA isn't doing anything wrong. That is just the level of service they signed up for, and most accounting firms in Plano are built that way on purpose.

Questions People Ask Before the First Call

What's the difference between tax preparation and tax planning?+

Tax preparation is typing in the boxes and filing what already happened. Tax planning is looking at the year while it's still happening, because most of the moves that lower a bill have to be made before December 31, not in April.

When should I elect S-corporation status?+

It depends on your numbers, and it's rarely as early as people think. It usually makes sense once self-employment income is your main income and there is enough of it that running payroll pays for itself. If most of your income is still W-2, the election does less than you'd expect. We run the math before anyone files Form 2553.

How much does a CPA cost for a small business?+

I'm not the cheapest CPA and I don't try to be. We work on a flat monthly retainer, quoted after the intro call once I've seen last year's return. It covers the planning, the filings, and a reasonable amount of consultation in between, so you never get a bill for asking a question.
