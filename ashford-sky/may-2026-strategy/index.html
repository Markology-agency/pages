Period: May 2026 onward (foundation stage) Location: Salt Lake City, UT Domain: ashfordsky.com

THE WEDGE DECISION

Ashford Sky's site lists four target audiences: e-commerce brands, SaaS companies, acquisition entrepreneurs, Utah small businesses. For a new firm, that's three too many. Every new accounting firm that picks four audiences becomes invisible on all four. The firms that grow pick one.

Recommendation: lead with SaaS. The most specific service on the site is "Outsourced Tax Department for SaaS" — that's a positioning competitors don't claim. SaaS companies are higher-margin per engagement than local small businesses, more likely to be growing past $500K (Ashford's target threshold), and less geographically constrained (national TAM vs Salt Lake City). Acquisition entrepreneurs is a related second-niche but smaller market. Utah local is a parallel track that adds geographic reach but doesn't define the firm.

Why SaaS

THREE REASONS
SAAS IS THE WEDGE.

1

Highest service margin

"Outsourced Tax Department for SaaS" is a productized retainer offering. Higher monthly contract values than transactional tax prep. Recurring revenue. SaaS companies budget for ongoing tax/finance support.

2

Competition is thin

Few CPA firms specialize in SaaS. Most generalist firms can't speak ASC 606 revenue recognition, R&D capitalization, sales-tax nexus, deferred revenue. Specialized positioning earns higher fees and lower lead cost.

3

National TAM

Utah is home to a real SaaS scene (Silicon Slopes). But SaaS clients don't need a local CPA. Niche-first means Ashford Sky competes nationally on expertise, not regionally on geography. That's the bigger market.

THREE PRIORITIES
FOR THE FIRST 60 DAYS.

Foundation building. Optimization comes after these are in place.

1

SHIP THE SAAS PILLAR PAGE + 5 SUPPORTING ARTICLES

Build a 2500-word pillar page at /saas-cpa-services/ titled "Outsourced Tax & Accounting for SaaS Companies." (decision framework)." Each links to the pillar. Goal: rank 5 of these 6 pages for SaaS-specific keywords within 90 days.

3

SHIP "MEET THE TEAM" + CPA CREDENTIALS + 3 TESTIMONIALS

The site directs to a "Meet the Team" page for team details but the page isn't visibly linked from the main nav per the WebFetch. For YMYL (tax advice) content, named credentialed authors are the #1 E-E-A-T trust signal Google looks for. Without this, the SaaS content cluster won't convert traffic.
