The June Story: A Competitor's Brand, Intercepted

The single biggest organic entry point this month was the therapist-reviews page (34 clicks, 3,315 impressions). It ranks on page one for roughly a dozen searches for "Heard" — a rival accounting platform for therapists — including "heard accounting" (390 volume, #7) and "heard bookkeeping" (#8). That's high-intent traffic from people comparing providers, landing on Traktion.

July Game Plan

1
Convert the intercept

Turn the "Heard" comparison traffic into consults

The reviews page is already Traktion's top organic door and ranks for a dozen competitor-brand searches. Add a clear, fair Traktion-vs-Heard comparison block and weave internal links from it into the services and CFO pages, so comparison-shoppers have a next step instead of a dead end. This is the highest-leverage page on the site right now.

2
Investigate the Q2 dip

Find the pages that lost impressions from April to June

Impressions eased from the Q1 highs and clicks dipped with them before June's rebound. Pull the page-level impression losers over that window, identify the two or three therapist posts slipping off page one, and refresh them (updated data, tighter title, internal links) to recover the visibility that was already earned.

3
Link the money pages

Strengthen the profitability and CFO cluster with internal links

"CFO for therapists" holds #1 and "average profit of a therapy practice" pulled 28 clicks at #7 — both are decision-stage pages close to conversion. Add internal links into them from the reviews page and the top-performing blog posts, and expand the therapy-practice-profitability cluster with one adjacent post to build topical depth around the terms already ranking.
