Napa small business accounting

I'm Octavio, your local Napa accountant.

Many of my clients are wineries, but the work is the same for any Napa business: a restaurant on First Street, a contractor in American Canyon, a dental office off Trancas. Clean books every month, taxes filed on time, payroll that runs itself, and a straight answer when you ask whether you're making money.

I was born and raised in Napa and spent a decade running finance for wineries up and down the valley before starting Llamas Financial. If you'd rather sit across a table than email a help desk, that's how I work too.

Services

What I handle for Napa businesses

Monthly plans from $500. Pick one service or hand over the whole back office.

Bookkeeping

Monthly reconciliations, clean categorization and financials you can read without a translator.

Tax preparation

Business and owner returns, quarterly estimates, and California sales tax filed before the deadline, not the day of.

Payroll

Employees paid on time, payroll taxes deposited, W-2s and 1099s out in January.

CFO services

Budgets, cash-flow forecasts and pricing help when you're deciding whether to hire, expand or buy the building.

Why local

Why a Napa accountant instead of an app

01

I know the local rules

Napa County business license renewals, the city's TOT and the state's sales tax districts. I file them every month for clients, so you don't learn them the hard way.

02

You meet the person doing the work

No offshore team, no rotating account reps. You call, I pick up. We can sit down at the office or at your shop.

03

Seasonal businesses are my specialty

Napa runs on harvest and tourist season. I build cash-flow plans for businesses that make most of their money in four months.
