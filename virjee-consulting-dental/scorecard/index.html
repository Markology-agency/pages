Red flags: 4 to talk through

1
HubSpot and Shehzad's sheet disagree on 18 clients
Why
Matching July 30 to Oct 27, 10 clients are on the sheet but not Closed Won in HubSpot, 3 are Closed Won in HubSpot but not on the sheet, 2 have close dates more than two weeks apart, 2 won deals have contacts not marked Active Clients, and 1 name is spelled two ways. 3 more deals closed after the sheet's last entry on Oct 27.
Fix
Work through the shared list and correct each one.
Owner: Shehzad and EmilyBy: Nov 13
2
Newsletter opens are about half of a comparable firm's
Why
Virjee's newsletters open at 9.5% and go to 95% of the list. A comparable HubSpot account opens at 21% because HubSpot only sends to its engaged contacts. When the Nov 4 newsletter went to the 581 most engaged readers first, 24.3% opened it.
Fix
Send each newsletter to engaged readers first, then widen.
Owner: MarkologyBy: Nov 11 newsletter
3
Webinar sign-ups from the ads slowed after Nov 3
Why
The ads brought 17 sign-ups Oct 29 to Nov 3, then 1 since, while the form itself works.
Fix
Add a fresh ad this week inside the same campaign.
Owner: MarkologyBy: Nov 10
4
Google search ads bring few form leads
Why
Over the last 90 days non-brand search spent $2,115 for 8 form leads. Four keywords produced every search form lead, and "dental cpa" was paused in October.
Fix
Turn "dental cpa" back on and move budget from searches that produced no leads.
