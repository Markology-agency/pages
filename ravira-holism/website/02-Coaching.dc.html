Ending the war with yourself.

High-functioning people rarely lack insight; they live with chronic internal polarization. When life feels stuck, it’s usually the friction of competing parts:

One part of you
  • Is ready for a real change
  • Holds a clear vision
  • Knows a boundary is needed
Another part of you
  • Panics and demands we play safe
  • Still overfunctions
  • Works to keep everyone comfortable
