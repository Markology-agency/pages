2.Root Cause

Content Freshness Collapse + Missing Schema, NOT a Penalty

This is not a Google manual action, not a noindex/canonical regression, not a site migration. Every one of the 15 top traffic pages still PASSES URL Inspection ("Submitted and indexed") and carries proper canonical tags. Average position barely moved (38.6 → 40.6).

What collapsed was impression-share for long-tail queries, not rank for held queries. Specifically:

The evidence:
  • /13-month-calendar-explained/ — position 14.2 → 13.0 (improved!), impressions 2,225 → 355 (-84%). Page held its top spots, lost long-tail coverage.
  • 22 high-volume queries (10+ impressions in Dec) disappeared entirely by May: "restaurant p&l template", "13 month calendar", "bar bookkeeping software", "accounting software for cafes", "average restaurant labor cost", etc.
  • Last-modified dates are stale: /13-month-calendar-explained/ = 2022-10-13, /how-profitable-are-breweries/ = 2023-04-25, /fica-tip-credit-2025/ = 2022-07-15, /4-basic-aspects-business-revenue/ = 2021-10-18.
  • NO page on the site carries FAQPage schema. Top 15 decayed pages have Article + BreadcrumbList only, missing the extractable Q&A markup that 2025-26 Google increasingly favors for SERP feature inclusion (FAQ rich result + AI Overview citation).
  • Several pages have multiple URLs ranking for the same keyword (cannibalization): "brewery accounting" hits 3 URLs, "marginedge" hits 2, "13-month calendar" hits 5+. Internal cannibalization splits ranking signals.
  • One key page is "Crawled but not indexed": /cash-tips-versus-credit-card-tips/ (last crawled 2025-12-04, modified 2025-11-25, 5,962 words). Lost all 252 Dec impressions.
  • Several pages haven't been crawled by Google since Feb-Apr 2026 — crawl frequency is decaying alongside content freshness.

Mechanism, plain English. Google's 2026 SERPs have shifted hard toward AI Overviews and freshness-weighted SERP features. When a 2022-modified article competes with a 2025-26 article on a long-tail query like "13 month calendar", the older article loses impression share even when its core rank position holds. Add cannibalization splitting equity, and missing FAQ schema dropping the page out of feature-eligibility, and you get exactly this curve: flat-ish positions, collapsed impressions, evaporated long-tail queries.
