The Blueprint

Tighten the active campaign around Cape Cod CPA exact-match

Restructure "Search - Tax / bookkeeping" around exact-match keywords: "[cape cod cpa]", "[tax accountant cape cod]", "[cpa cape cod]", "[cape cod accountant]", "[accountant cape cod]". The Caveat

Statistically, one conversion in 30 days isn't a full read. The directional signal (Cape Cod local intent converts, competitor brand and broad-intent don't) is strong. The "A/B Manage Google Ads Campaign" task on the W3 board (Not Started, due 5/18) is the place to run a proper test against this hypothesis once the negatives are in place.

Recommended next move: add the H&R Block negatives this week, restructure the "Search - Tax / bookkeeping" campaign around Cape Cod CPA exact-match in the next 7 days, decide on the two silent Payroll campaigns in tomorrow's meeting (kill or fix bids), then re-baseline 30 days from now against a cleaner spend.
