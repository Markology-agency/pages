What I'd Change Before Beta

  • 01
    YouTube is risky for most accountants. Camera + editing cadence kills non-naturals. Sub-modules 10.7 through 10.14 stay optional in fast-track. For solos, fast-track skips this and runs the leads-now sprint instead.
  • 02
    Ads stay minimal. Most accountants should not DIY Google Ads. Module 11.1 plus a clean "or hire Markology" link is the right scope.
  • 03
    Fast-track decision deferred to post-build. Per Whitney 2026-06-19: build the full deep-dive version first. Once shipped, decide which sub-modules are "must-watch for leads" vs "nice-to-have for understanding."
  • 04
    Practice management integration in Module 1.4. Connecting Claude to Canopy / Carbon / Karbon / QuickBooks is your moat. No other accountant marketing course teaches this. Setup module + deeper per-tool workflows in the Bonus library.
  • 05
    The "5-tab test" is the canonical teaching device. Named in Module 4 (Website Conversion) and powered by the /website-audit skill. Use it on the sales page too: "Open 5 accountant tabs. Yours is one of them. 15 seconds each. What makes them close yours? What makes them stay?"
  • 06
    The structure: Setup, then Foundation, then everything. Month 1 is technical infrastructure (Claude Code + connections). Month 2 is marketing fundamentals. Months 3 through 12 work through every chapter of your 2024 outline, in your original order. Self-paced, no time pressure.
