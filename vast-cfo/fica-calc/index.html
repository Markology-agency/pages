How this is calculated: The FICA tip credit (IRC §45B) refunds the employer's share of FICA tax (7.65%) on tips above the federal tipped-minimum subsidy. For each employee: creditable tips per week = max(0, weekly tips − (max(0, $5.15 − cash hourly wage) × hours)); annual credit = creditable tips × 7.65% × 52 × number of employees. The $5.15/hr wage floor is frozen in the statute. Estimate only — actual credit depends on accurate tip reporting and per-employee payroll records.
