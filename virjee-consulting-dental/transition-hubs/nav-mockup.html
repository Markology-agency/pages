What changes from today

Moves and additions

Buying & Selling and Dental Consulting are new top-level menus, each with its own hub page. The Buying & Selling hub page keeps the Ownership Transitions name and URL for search. Services is renamed Accounting & Tax. Buying a Practice moves out of it into Buying & Selling. The Valuation Calculator moves to Tools. Today nothing in the menu mentions selling, exit or consulting.

Open questions

Fee Schedule Analysis and the KPI Checker are not built yet, so they would stay out of the menu until they go live. Omar to confirm which pages launch first. E-Books and Tools and Downloads merge into one Tools menu.
