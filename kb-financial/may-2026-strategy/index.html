New Plays

FOUR NEW
DIRECTIONS.

Outside the standard SEO and content rhythm. Four bigger swings worth piloting.

Idea 01

FAANG company-specific equity guides

Build dedicated landing pages for the top 10 FAANG and tech employer equity comp structures: 'Google RSU Guide', 'Meta RSU + ESPP Guide', 'Amazon RSU Vesting Schedule', 'Stripe Equity Guide', 'Anthropic Equity Guide'. Each page targets the specific RSU/ESPP/options structure of that employer.

Highly specific, low competition, high commercial intent. Tech employees search their employer name plus 'RSU' or 'ESPP' constantly.

Idea 02

Landon on-camera technical explainers (LinkedIn native)

Short (60 to 90 second) videos of Landon explaining one specific equity comp concept per week: 'What an 83(b) election actually does', 'When to exercise ISOs before they convert to NSOs', 'Why most people misunderstand the AMT'.

Plays to the brand's tech fluency. Founders and FAANG employees trust experts who can explain things in plain terms on camera. Compliance review on every script.

Idea 03

Liquidity event email alert list

Capture emails on every tender offer / IPO / acquisition page with a free 'Tax Optimization Checklist for [Company] Employees' download. Then a monthly newsletter: 'What we're watching this month in tech liquidity' with educational analysis.

Builds an owned email list of in-market tech employees and founders, primed for outreach when major events happen. Low spend, high lifetime value.

Idea 04

Founder-only content hub at /founders/

Dedicated section of the site for founders specifically: pre-IPO planning, secondary sales, QSBS strategy, founder cap table mechanics, equity dilution analysis. Pair with a quarterly 'Founder Financial Planning' webinar.

HNW founder audience converts at meaningfully higher per-client value than FAANG employees. Worth a focused content destination of its own.
