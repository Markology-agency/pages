How this is measured

Position — where the site appears in Google for that exact search, desktop, United States, checked daily by SEMrush. Blank means outside the top 100.
7 days / 30 days — change in position over that window. Green is an improvement, which means a lower number.
Unconfirmed — the position moved in each of the last three checks and has not settled. Real movement holds for several days; a one-day jump often does not.
Impressions and clicks — Google's own figures for the whole site, not just tracked keywords.
Excluded — 3 keywords sit outside this report: lava lamp encryption wall, lava lamp security wall, wall of lava lamps. The site ranks well for them, but they are a novelty search rather than someone looking for internal audit work, so counting them would overstate the top-3 number.
