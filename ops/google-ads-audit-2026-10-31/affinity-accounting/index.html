Conversion tracking needs fixing

  • Calls from ads (PHONE_CALL_LEAD / CALL_FROM_ADS, primary): biddable at the account level and on all three enabled campaigns. Calls must not be biddable.
  • Phone Clicks (CONTACT / WEBSITE, WEBPAGE, primary): this is a phone-number click, and the CONTACT/WEBSITE goal is biddable at the account level and on all three enabled campaigns. It produced one of the Search campaign's two counted conversions in September 2026.
  • PAGE_VIEW / WEBSITE goal is biddable at the account level and on the agencies campaign. No primary action sits in it today ('About Us' is secondary), but any page-view action set to primary later would be bid on.
  • BOOK_APPOINTMENT / WEBSITE goal is biddable. Its only action, 'Book a Call (Button)', is a button click to the contact page and is secondary. Make the goal non-biddable so a click can never become a bid target.
  • Submit lead form (WEBPAGE, primary): 7 Search events over the year counted as 0 conversions, while the 2 PMax events counted. Something at the campaign goal level kept form submits out of Search bidding until recently. The current setup shows it biddable, so confirm in the UI that new submits now land in the Conversions column.
  • Contact form Submission (CONTACT / WEBPAGE, primary): 0 conversions in 12 months. It is either broken or a duplicate of 'Submit lead form'. Find out which and remove or fix it.
  • Local actions (Directions, Website visits, Other engagements, Menu views) and 'Clicks to call' (GOOGLE_HOSTED) are marked primary. Their goals are not biddable, so they do not drive bids today, but they should be secondary so nobody turns them on by accident.
  • A call asset with (414) 533-8100 is attached to the live Search campaign. House rule is forms only, so remove it.
