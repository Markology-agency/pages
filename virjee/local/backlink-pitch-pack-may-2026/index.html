To: editor@physicianspractice.com · Subject: Op-ed pitch: S-corp election math for solo physicians
Hi, I'd like to write an op-ed for Physicians Practice on the S-corp election math for solo and small-group physicians. The specific angle: a solo physician netting $400K through a PLLC pays roughly $25K in self-employment tax. The same income through an S-corp PLLC with a reasonable $200K salary cuts that to about $10K, saving $15K+ annually after the additional filing costs. The piece would walk through: the salary reasonableness test (the IRS focus area), the state-by-state PLLC vs S-corp interaction, the timing requirements (Form 2553 deadline), and three operational triggers that say wait. 1,200 words.
