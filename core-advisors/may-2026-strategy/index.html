May / June Game Plan

WHAT WE WORK
NEXT.

Six items, ordered by impact. Items 1 to 3 ship this week. Items 4 to 6 ship in the next 30 days. The biggest unlock isn't more rankings. It's converting impressions we already have into clicks, and converting clicks into bookings.

01

Diagnose the 60% session drop

Audit Google Ads spend, campaign status, and conversion tracking. Cross-check direct and referral traffic patterns in Analytics. If something broke, fix it. If a campaign was paused intentionally, replace the lost demand with organic plus retargeting. Done within 7 days.

This Week
02

Add UTM tracking to every Calendly link on the site

Right now we cannot tell if a booked call came from organic search, a paid ad, a referral, social, or direct. Without attribution, every other recommendation is unmeasurable. Tag every Calendly CTA on the site with proper source and campaign parameters. Live data flows in within 24 hours of going live.

This Week
03

Make Calendly intake required + add 2 qualifying questions

Two of the six recent bookings came in with no context, leaving Thomas to qualify cold for the first 5 to 10 minutes of the call. Required field plus two short questions tightens prep, raises close rate, and filters out non-fit callers before they hit the calendar.

This Week
04

Title and meta rewrite project: top 12 pages

Highest-leverage SEO work on the docket. Pages already rank. They just need to win the click. Estimated upside is 5 to 10x organic clicks on those pages within 60 days. No new content required, no new rankings, no backlink work.

30 Days
05

Push three keywords from page 1 to top 3

Dentist advisors at #9 (210 monthly searches). Dental financial advisors at #8 (140). Dental practice value calculator at #9 (110). All three have ranked in striking distance for 12 months and just need a content refresh plus 1 to 2 quality backlinks each.

30 Days
06

Service page rewrite project (all 7 pages)

The seven service pages collectively earned 2 organic clicks across 90 days despite ranking in Google. They are getting served. The titles and content just don't win the click. Restructure with answer-first headings, benchmark numbers, and internal links. These are the highest-intent pages on the site.

30 Days
New Plays

FOUR NEW
DIRECTIONS.

Outside the standard SEO and content rhythm. Bigger swings worth piloting if you're open to them. Four ideas, ranked from lowest-effort to most ambitious.

Idea 01

Podcast guest tour

Pitch you onto 2 to 3 dental practice owner podcasts per quarter. Short list: Shared Practices, The Dentalpreneur, ACT Dental, The Dental Hacks.

Direct access to the exact target audience. Backlink and Calendly CTA on every appearance. One of the most underrated channels for B2B advisors in any niche.

Idea 02

Calculator-page retargeting on Meta

Anyone who hits the Practice Valuation Calculator and doesn't book a call gets retargeted on Facebook and Instagram with a 'Want help reading the number?' creative leading back to your calendar.

Hyper-qualified audience. Low spend, fast feedback loop. Modest budget required to test (~$500 per month).

Idea 03

Targeted LinkedIn outbound for Thomas

10 to 15 personalized DMs per week to dental practice owners in specific situations: 5 to 15 ops, recently bought a practice, recently hit a milestone.

Each message shares one specific tax or financial insight relevant to their situation, with a soft 'happy to chat if useful' close. Conversion rate runs 30 to 50% on this style when done correctly. Small numbers, big returns.

Idea 04

The 'Second Opinion' guarantee

Free 30-minute review for any dentist currently working with another CPA or financial advisor. Pitch: 'If we don't identify $5,000+ in savings or improvements, we'll send you a $200 Amazon gift card.'

Bold offer, but for a target client whose lifetime value is $30K+ per year, the math works heavily in your favor. Removes the biggest objection: 'I already have a guy.'
