The Accountant Who Files but Never Calls

I hear the same complaint on almost every call from an established owner. The accountant doesn't talk to them and isn't proactive, so they end up bringing up the deductions themselves.

Most of the time, the accountant isn't bad. The majority of CPAs are not proactive planning CPAs. What the client bought was a filing service, and that's the level of service they're getting.

I call that a compliance CPA. The return gets filed and you stay out of trouble. That's worth something. It just isn't planning, and a business that's been growing for a few years usually needs planning.

Some accountants just don't like talking to clients. I do. Selfishly, an unresponsive CPA is my favorite thing to hear about on a first call, because being responsive is most of what I do.

Switching Your Business to Us Mid-Year

People think you have to wait until after April to change CPAs. It's the opposite. If you come to me 11 days before a deadline, there's not much I can do. Mid-year gives me time to see where the year is heading and act on it.

1. Prior-year review

I read your last business and personal returns for carryovers and anything that looks off. It happens more than you'd think. I've seen couples filing separately for no reason, when a joint return would have come out better.

2. Books and payroll

We take over what's already there or clean it up. If QuickBooks has loans in the wrong place or payroll lumped into one line, we fix that first.

3. The road map

We map out the rest of the year, including the deductions that haven't been claimed. Those are usually the vehicle, the home office, retirement funding, and paying family members who actually work in the business.

4. The December projection

This is what makes next April the first one without a surprise.

Questions McKinney Business Owners Ask

Is it a hassle to switch CPAs partway through the year?+

Less than people expect. You send the prior-year returns and give me view-only access to the business bank account and QuickBooks. I handle the rest, including anything your old accountant needs to hand over.

What if my books are behind?+

That's common, and it's fixable. We reconcile the accounts, split out what's been lumped together, and get to a clean set of financial statements. Then we build the plan on top of them.

My accountant says my deductions are too aggressive. Are they?+

Some might be, and some might be perfectly normal deductions nobody set up properly. I'll tell you where each one stands and what the risk is. Whether you claim it is your call.

Do you work with businesses that have employees?+

Yes. We set up and oversee payroll through the payroll software, and I stay involved in how the owner is paid, because that part has the biggest tax effect.
