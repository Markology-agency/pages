FAQs

Bookkeeping questions from new owners

The seller's books are a mess. Can you fix them?

Yes, that's the normal starting point, not the exception. We clean up miscategorized history, reconcile what was never reconciled, and rebuild the chart of accounts before moving to monthly support.

Will my books be ready for taxes and my SBA lender?

Yes. Everything is categorized with tax reporting in mind and kept current, so filing season, covenant checks, and lender reviews draw from the same clean set of numbers.

How often are the books updated?

Monthly for most owners. Higher transaction volume or tight cash management in the first year sometimes calls for more frequent updates; we'll recommend a cadence based on how you use the numbers.

Will I have a real person to talk to?

Yes. You work with a team that knows your business and your deal. Questions get answers, not ticket numbers.
