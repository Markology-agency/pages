Before You Book

Do I need to prepare anything for the intro call?−

No. Last year’s return helps if you have it handy, and so does a rough idea of what you expect to earn this year from each source. If you answered the questions first, I’ll already have most of what I need.

How often will we talk once we’re working together?+

It changes through the year. At the start you might talk to me five times in a month while we set up the books, payroll and your road map. After that we talk when something changes, and we always meet for the road map early in the year and the December projection. A reasonable amount of calls, texts and emails in between is part of the fee.

What if it turns out I don’t need you yet?+

Then I'll tell you on the call. If your situation is simple this year, TurboTax may be fine, and I'd rather say so than take your money. We can talk again when something changes, like a new 1099 contract or a business of your own.

What if I change my mind after signing?+

You'll see your tax road map before your first payment on day 30. If it isn't worth it to you, cancel before then with no notice period and no cancellation fee.

What do you need from me to get started?+

Your tax documents and a copy of last year's return. I look it over for anything unusual that carries into this year. You'll get a link to upload everything, so please don't email your W-2.

Can you take over mid-year?+

Yes. We set the engagement to start when it makes sense for you, catch up the books if they need it, and build the road map for the rest of the year. If last year's return is still open, we can handle that too.

Do you work with clients outside Texas?+

Yes. I have clients all over the country. Even a lot of my Dallas clients meet with me over video and I rarely see them in person.

What if I already have a preparer I like?+

That can work. Sometimes the right setup is that your preparer keeps the return, and we handle the monthly books and the planning. We'll talk through what makes sense on the call.
